## Evidence and language semantics

The shared concrete input is 1; SCENARIO deadline; FLOORS 3; CARS 2; CAPACITY 1; DOOR 1; START 1 2; POLICY ROUND; PATIENCE 1; 0 CALL 1 3 2; 1 WHERE 1; END (one item per line). At tick 0 E1 receives G1, opens and boards one person, logs LEFT x1, and reassigns the remainder to E2; E2 moves from floor 2 to 1. Tick 1 begins with ABANDON G1 x1, before WHERE and either car action. WHERE therefore reports waiting=0 car=- riding=E1x1 delivered=0 lost=1; E2 logs IDLE 1 instead of opening. E1 closes/moves at ticks 1 and 2, opens/alights at tick 3 and becomes idle at tick 4, then END is logged. G1 has delivered=1 lost=1 wait=- travel=- cars=1. E1 has distance=2 stops=2 boarded=1 busy=4 util=80.0% energy=12; E2 has distance=1 stops=0 boarded=0 busy=1 util=20.0% energy=1. F1 has groups=1 passengers=2 delivered=1 avg_wait=0.00; summary has delivered=1 abandoned=1 evacuated=0 avg_wait=0.00 avg_travel=3.00 max_wait=0 end=4. The scenario is ranked first and ROUND has one run with the same averages. PHP object variables carry handles even inside value-semantic arrays. Passenger is readonly, fixing its Group handle/count/time but not freezing Group; that distinction explains why lost/delivered updates remain visible while the riding record survives. removeObject takes the array by reference and splices just the matching object; ordinary array assignment alone would not establish this removal. Boarding builds its array_filter snapshot before partial assignment mutates calls. Null missing PARK, false fire and ordered car construction establish the normal path. intdiv and exact echo/emit operations close the output/statistics trace, and ROUND avoids the cost routine.

## Minimization and alternatives

The same WHERE scenario is used in every language. WHERE was compared with CANCEL: it directly exposes the riding/waiting distinction and avoids the additional cancellation-success/error path. ROUND avoids cost, ETA and nearest-policy dependencies. Two cars with capacity one are necessary here to make a partial group and a physically available second car at the deadline. Select only the reached configuration/event arms and their bounds, the tick scheduler, waiting-call removal, partial reassignment, ordinary car movement/boarding/delivery, and output aggregations. OUT/IN/HOLD/FIRE/RESET/CANCEL/STATUS, parking movement, comparison reruns, period reports and other policy implementations are not needed. Their defaults/guards establish that they are inactive; ordinary state is retained where a guard depends on it. For a single ranked run the comparator implementation adds no ordering fact. Each retained line supplies a state, validation, call relationship, transition, formatting or aggregation fact; necessary internal control and callback boundaries are retained, while redundant outer declaration frames and blank lines are omitted. The final excerpt alone was independently checked against all five common requirements, including preservation of the rider and delivered-only average denominators. Internal loop, branch, switch-arm and callback endings explicitly separate expiry from same-tick events, car actions from termination, and repeated contributions from later state/output effects. Where a closing line also begins an adjacent arm, the complete original line and its necessary following boundary are retained. These boundaries do not add a new scenario, command implementation, or answer requirement.
