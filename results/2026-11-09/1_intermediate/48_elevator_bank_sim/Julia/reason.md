## Evidence and language semantics

The shared concrete input is 1; SCENARIO deadline; FLOORS 3; CARS 2; CAPACITY 1; DOOR 1; START 1 2; POLICY ROUND; PATIENCE 1; 0 CALL 1 3 2; 1 WHERE 1; END (one item per line). At tick 0 E1 receives G1, opens and boards one person, logs LEFT x1, and reassigns the remainder to E2; E2 moves from floor 2 to 1. Tick 1 begins with ABANDON G1 x1, before WHERE and either car action. WHERE therefore reports waiting=0 car=- riding=E1x1 delivered=0 lost=1; E2 logs IDLE 1 instead of opening. E1 closes/moves at ticks 1 and 2, opens/alights at tick 3 and becomes idle at tick 4, then END is logged. G1 has delivered=1 lost=1 wait=- travel=- cars=1. E1 has distance=2 stops=2 boarded=1 busy=4 util=80.0% energy=12; E2 has distance=1 stops=0 boarded=0 busy=1 util=20.0% energy=1. F1 has groups=1 passengers=2 delivered=1 avg_wait=0.00; summary has delivered=1 abandoned=1 evacuated=0 avg_wait=0.00 avg_travel=3.00 max_wait=0 end=4. The scenario is ranked first and ROUND has one run with the same averages. Julia mutable struct Group and Car retain identity, whereas Passenger is an immutable record containing the mutable Group reference plus independent count/boarding time. filter! removes only a matching Group from a calls vector; the passenger vector is a distinct field and is unchanged by walk!. The positional constructors and declarations jointly establish every initial counter and inactive optional mode. Ordered comprehensions, findfirst and exclusion by car number establish E1 then E2 assignment. Boarding iterates a newly constructed Group vector. Integer division ÷ and standard aggregation semantics justify exact report values; ROUND excludes the cost routine.

Internal-boundary review: Retain configuration/arity and event-validation branch/loop ends, including the local bounded helper let end before configuration dispatch. Explicit ends separate ROUND assignment from common call insertion, waiting-call removal from lost accounting, WHERE scanning, passenger alighting, boarding/partial reassignment and ordinary movement/busy-time updates. In run!, patience/abandonment scopes end before event processing, event processing ends before car actions, and termination ends before the next tick; therefore E2 cannot board the expired waiting remainder and E1 continues carrying its existing passenger. Output, inactive PERIOD, scenario/configuration handling, empty comparisons, ranking and policy loops are bounded before shared output. Global function/type wrappers are omitted; the runtime-local let helper boundary is preserved.

## Minimization and alternatives

The same WHERE scenario is used in every language. WHERE was compared with CANCEL: it directly exposes the riding/waiting distinction and avoids the additional cancellation-success/error path. ROUND avoids cost, ETA and nearest-policy dependencies. Two cars with capacity one are necessary here to make a partial group and a physically available second car at the deadline. Select only the reached configuration/event arms and their bounds, the tick scheduler, waiting-call removal, partial reassignment, ordinary car movement/boarding/delivery, and output aggregations. OUT/IN/HOLD/FIRE/RESET/CANCEL/STATUS, parking movement, comparison reruns, period reports and other policy implementations are not needed. Their defaults/guards establish that they are inactive; ordinary state is retained where a guard depends on it. For a single ranked run the comparator implementation adds no ordering fact. Each retained line supplies a state, validation, call relationship, transition, formatting or aggregation fact; empty lines and delimiters unrelated to a required scope boundary are omitted. Internal boundaries needed to establish validation, tick ordering, abandonment, reassignment, boarding and shared statistics are retained explicitly; indentation is not treated as a scope guarantee in token-delimited languages. The final excerpt alone was independently checked against all five common requirements, including preservation of the rider and delivered-only average denominators.

The boundary review changes only source-line selection, not the common scenario or answer requirements. Each added original line closes a specifically reviewed internal scope or provides a co-located required boundary; function/class wrappers and wholly omitted command paths are not included merely to make the excerpt compile.
