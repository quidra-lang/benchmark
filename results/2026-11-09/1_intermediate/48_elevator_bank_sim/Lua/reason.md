## Answer requirements shared across languages

1. Trace the common valid input: one scenario deadline; FLOORS 3, CARS 2, CAPACITY 1, DOOR 1, START 1 2, POLICY ROUND, PATIENCE 1; events 0 CALL 1 3 2 and 1 WHERE 1, followed by END. Establish configuration defaults, input/event parsing and relevant validation.
2. Trace the group, waiting-call and riding-passenger state separately: ROUND assigns E1, E1 boards one at tick 0, and the remaining person is reassigned to E2 while E2 reaches floor 1.
3. Establish the tick order: expiry/abandonment precedes same-tick input events, which precede car actions. At deadline 1 only the waiting person is lost, its assigned call is removed, and WHERE still sees the riding portion.
4. Explain why E2 cannot board at the deadline despite being at the landing, and why the E1 passenger remains aboard and is delivered at tick 3. Follow door, direction, movement and termination through END at tick 4.
5. Derive the event log, per-group/per-car/floor/summary output and the single scenario ranking/policy aggregate, distinguishing fully boarded/delivered group fields from delivered-passenger averages and abandonment counts.

## Evidence and language semantics

The shared concrete input is 1; SCENARIO deadline; FLOORS 3; CARS 2; CAPACITY 1; DOOR 1; START 1 2; POLICY ROUND; PATIENCE 1; 0 CALL 1 3 2; 1 WHERE 1; END (one item per line). At tick 0 E1 receives G1, opens and boards one person, logs LEFT x1, and reassigns the remainder to E2; E2 moves from floor 2 to 1. Tick 1 begins with ABANDON G1 x1, before WHERE and either car action. WHERE therefore reports waiting=0 car=- riding=E1x1 delivered=0 lost=1; E2 logs IDLE 1 instead of opening. E1 closes/moves at ticks 1 and 2, opens/alights at tick 3 and becomes idle at tick 4, then END is logged. G1 has delivered=1 lost=1 wait=- travel=- cars=1. E1 has distance=2 stops=2 boarded=1 busy=4 util=80.0% energy=12; E2 has distance=1 stops=0 boarded=0 busy=1 util=20.0% energy=1. F1 has groups=1 passengers=2 delivered=1 avg_wait=0.00; summary has delivered=1 abandoned=1 evacuated=0 avg_wait=0.00 avg_travel=3.00 max_wait=0 end=4. The scenario is ranked first and ROUND has one run with the same averages. Lua tables are identity-bearing references. The selected numeric array construction and ipairs establish car order, while calls and passenger.group hold the same group table. remove uses table.remove on the passed list, and walk changes that list plus only waiting/car/lost/abandoned fields; it leaves riding entries intact. A separate boarding list is constructed before partial reassignment, so that reassignment can safely remove the original call. Missing table keys yield nil, which establishes inactive fire, PARK and PERIOD without extra initialization code. Colon-call desugaring and the Sim metatable connect methods to mutable state; integer // and the selected formatter establish the totals and rounding.

Internal-boundary review: Explicit control and iteration endings separate configuration validation, ROUND assignment, WHERE scans, alighting/boarding, waiting-call removal and partial reassignment from their subsequent shared updates. The patience loop completes before same-tick input events, which complete before car actions; termination is checked after the cars. The report and ranking/policy loops close before subsequent output. These scopes establish that only the waiting remainder is abandoned at deadline 1, E2 cannot board it, and E1 keeps the already-riding passenger until delivery. The selected helpers are top-level functions or methods rather than runtime-local multiline definitions, so their redundant outer endings are not added. Existing commands, scenario, answer requirements and source logic are unchanged.

## Minimization and alternatives

The same WHERE scenario is used in every language. WHERE was compared with CANCEL: it directly exposes the riding/waiting distinction and avoids the additional cancellation-success/error path. ROUND avoids cost, ETA and nearest-policy dependencies. Two cars with capacity one are necessary here to make a partial group and a physically available second car at the deadline. Select only the reached configuration/event arms and their bounds, the tick scheduler, waiting-call removal, partial reassignment, ordinary car movement/boarding/delivery, and output aggregations. OUT/IN/HOLD/FIRE/RESET/CANCEL/STATUS, parking movement, comparison reruns, period reports and other policy implementations are not needed. Their defaults/guards establish that they are inactive; ordinary state is retained where a guard depends on it. For a single ranked run the comparator implementation adds no ordering fact. Each retained line supplies a state, validation, call relationship, transition, formatting or aggregation fact; empty lines and delimiters unrelated to a required scope boundary are omitted. Internal boundaries needed to establish validation, tick ordering, abandonment, reassignment, boarding and shared statistics are retained explicitly; indentation is not treated as a scope guarantee in token-delimited languages. The final excerpt alone was independently checked against all five common requirements, including preservation of the rider and delivered-only average denominators.

The boundary review changes only source-line selection, not the common scenario or answer requirements. Each added original line closes a specifically reviewed internal scope or provides a co-located required boundary; function/class wrappers and wholly omitted command paths are not included merely to make the excerpt compile.
