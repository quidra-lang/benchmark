## Evidence and language semantics

The match loop reselects the best opposite-side order each iteration. The ordering key prioritizes the best price and then the oldest priority. A fill consumes the smaller of the incoming remainder and the maker visible slice; the retained maker remainder is the hidden-plus-visible quantity. Exhausting a slice with a positive remainder assigns a fresh priority and exposes min(peak, remainder), placing it behind older resting orders at the same price. The trade output is produced before that refresh output. The same-trader guard cancels the resting maker and continues before fill, fee, and refresh processing, so the incoming remainder is not consumed. Lua tables are shared references, including table elements visited by ipairs. The same-trader path is an if/else rather than a continue statement: all matching, fees and refresh are inside else, so cancellation reaches the next loop iteration directly. The explicit best comparison supplies price/time ordering and io.write preserves emission order.
