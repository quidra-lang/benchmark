## Evidence and language semantics

Use a 14-command input: register COURSE c with eighteen pars of 4 and stroke indexes 1 through 18; TEE c t 720 113; PLAYER p Amy 200; record ROUND p c t on days 1, 2 and 3 with eighteen scores of 5 each, then day 4 with eighteen scores of 4. Query INDEX p, HISTORY p and LOWINDEX p 5; REMOVE p; repeat those three queries. Registration succeeds and is silent. P=72, R=720 and S=113 make the first three handicaps 20, AGS=90 and differential D=180; no exceptional reduction applies. With fewer than three rounds the initial index 200 persists. The dated index records after the third round are [(1,200),(2,200),(3,160)]. The third calculation selects the newer of the tied differentials, uses k=1, average=180, adjustment=-20, low=200 from prior days, and no cap/maximum flag; the index becomes 160. Before accepting round 4, capture exactly that index, the record prefix of length 3, and the complete calculation. Round 4 uses handicap 16; scores of 4 are below every limit, so AGS=72 and D=0. Since 160-0 >= 100, E=20. The adjusted differentials become 160,160,160,-20. With n=4, the newest round is the single counting round; average=-20 and adjustment=-10 give index=-30. The fourth calculation stores low=160, no cap flags, and a new dated record (4,-30) is appended. The four ROUND lines therefore end respectively in index 20.0, 20.0, 16.0 and +3.0; only round 4 includes exceptional -2.0. Before removal, INDEX prints "index p: +3.0 rounds 4 lowest 1 average -2.0 adjustment -1.0 low 16.0". HISTORY starts "history p: rounds 4"; rounds 1-3 each show ags 90 diff 18.0 adjusted 16.0, and round 4 shows ags 72 diff 0.0 adjusted -2.0 followed by *. LOWINDEX p 5 prints "lowindex p 5: +3.0 day 4". REMOVE prints "removed p 4: index 16.0" after dropping round 4 and restoring the captured state. The next INDEX is "index p: 16.0 rounds 3 lowest 1 average 18.0 adjustment -2.0 low 20.0". HISTORY now has three rows with their original ags 90 diff 18.0, no adjusted suffix, and only round 3 marked *. LOWINDEX p 5 becomes "lowindex p 5: 16.0 day 3" because the removed round's dated index record is also removed. HISTORY derives its adjusted values and counting marks from the remaining rounds; their original D and E are not rewritten. INDEX instead reports the restored calculation, including the old low=200, and LOWINDEX searches the restored dated records in [day-365, day-1], breaking equal-index ties by the earlier date. Merely deleting the score would retain the wrong current index, low-index record and last calculation. Recomputing at day 4 would use low=160 instead of the prior calculation's low=200, even if it happened to recover the same numeric index; remaining score totals also do not encode the seeded index that persisted before three rounds or the chronology used for caps.
