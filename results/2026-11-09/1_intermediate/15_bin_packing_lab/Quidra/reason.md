## Evidence and language semantics

The common witness is three commands: `ITEMS 10 6 6 5 5 4`, `KBOUNDED 2`, `KBOUNDED 2`. All sizes are nonzero and at most 10, k is nonzero, and ITEMS establishes readiness. The ITEMS line is `ITEMS count=5 capacity=10 total=26`. Each run processes the five original numbered items, inspecting respectively 0, 1, 2, 2, 1 active bins (six total). Item 3 cannot fit either load-6 bin, so the existing full-active closure chooses bin 1 (greatest load, smallest number on a tie); it opens bin 3, which takes items 3 and 4, while item 5 goes to bin 2. Current output is `KBOUNDED k=2 bins=3 inspections=6 waste=4 closes=1`, with BIN 1 load=6/10 items=1:6; BIN 2 load=10/10 items=2:6 5:4; BIN 3 load=10/10 items=3:5 4:5. The proposed comparator chooses the smallest load, then largest bin number: in this tie it closes bin 2 instead, leaving BIN 1 load=10/10 items=1:6 5:4, BIN 2 load=6/10 items=2:6, BIN 3 unchanged. Only the closure winner calculation changes. Removing its active entry preserves the relative order of survivors, and the new bin is appended; comparisons for closure do not increment packing inspections. Closure count, creation, placement, numbering, waste and output paths remain unchanged. The second KBOUNDED starts a new local packing using the same five items and capacity; it repeats the applicable result rather than retaining active-bin state. Quidra mutation authority makes the state boundary explicit: command_items receives Lab &lab and installs current items; pack and command_method receive value arguments, while put receives only the selected result Bin by mutable reference. The strict greater-load scan over ascending active bin numbers keeps the earliest/smallest tied number. Replace its winner test by lower load or equal load with greater index. Reading value arguments cannot mutate the caller Lab.

The direct KBOUNDED witness avoids TRACE records and formatting. Every retained group supplies parsing/validation, first-fit inspections, closure and placement, counters/output, or the persistent-state boundary. Full routines would add other packing methods, random generation, bounds, reductions and 2D packing. A closure-only excerpt cannot establish inspections, assignment output or input preservation. An input-copy declaration alone is insufficient in reference-based languages, so the selected executed writes and scalar item representation are retained. Class/implementation and branch headers remain where needed to associate the selected calls with their definitions.
