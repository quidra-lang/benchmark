SELF was chosen over parameterized/arbitrage strategies to avoid their validation, tariff-ranking and export-policy dependencies. A single period and zero PV avoid tariff ties and nonzero charging cases; the shared arithmetic/flow lines remain when needed to establish those zeros and the import/shortfall fields. SET INITIAL was chosen over PRICE because the changed initial charge makes saved-versus-active configuration observable exactly at the required day1/hour0 boundary; including both mutations would add a redundant command route. One overridden hour demonstrates hourly input replacement and a partially served outage. Retain only reached parser/command paths, flow and billing dependencies, copied configuration ownership and output fields; exclude reports, additional strategies and unrelated statistics. Whole routines were considered and rejected in favor of individually required complete lines, including guards where they select the SELF path. Unrelated function/class outer framing and result fields are omitted; the internal control boundaries needed for the SELF path are retained.
