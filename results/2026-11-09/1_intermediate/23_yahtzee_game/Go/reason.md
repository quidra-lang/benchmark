## Evidence and language semantics

Common valid input has 10 commands: GAME 1 A; LOAD 1 2 3 4 5 6 6 6 6 6 2 2 2 2 2; ROLL; SCORE ONES; UNDO; REDO; ROLL; UNDO; HOLD 00000; ROLL. Each semicolon separates a line. The first roll is 1 2 3 4 5, ONES scores 1 (both bonuses remain zero), and scoring starts round 2. UNDO reopens ONES and restores round-1 dice/roll state; REDO re-enters the same score and restores the saved next-turn state. The next roll consumes the five sixes. Undoing the entry again restores the original round-1 hand but leaves the queue consumed; HOLD 00000 clears redo, and the final roll 2 uses the five twos. The face counters reflect all 15 consumed faces. Go fixed [5]int/[5]bool arrays and structs copy by value. Entry.Turn captures *g.Turn as a value, and undo makes a fresh restored local before taking its address; mutable later hands therefore cannot change the historical entry. Redo retains the suspended turn pointer until restoration; successful roll/hold discard redo before it could be reused. Player pointers intentionally share score maps and bonus state. Absent map entries and omitted numeric fields have zero values; derived upper/round/total reflect restoration. Roller is a separate pointer containing the advancing queue slice, PRNG state and count array. The selected upper-category early return is sufficient because only ONES invokes normalScore on this route, with a value-array input. Explicit validation-return paths and error/result slices close parsing through printing.

## Minimization and alternatives

A single first-turn upper-category entry is the smallest trace that covers score insertion, used-box and bonus state, turn advancement, undo/redo restoration and later dice consumption. Choosing ONES avoids unrelated lower-category scoring; no bot, advice, simulation, records or end-of-game path is needed. The bonus calculation and stored bonus delta are retained even though this trace has zero bonus. Loaded faces avoid depending on a long RNG arithmetic trace, but the generator/queue split and independent counters remain explicit. Full score/undo/redo mutation routes and the selected snapshot or copy implementation establish what is restored; the omitted source is not used to claim hidden mutation absence. Parser, rule defaults, successful validation and output helpers close the trace.
