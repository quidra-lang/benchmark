## Evidence and language semantics

Common valid input has 10 commands: GAME 1 A; LOAD 1 2 3 4 5 6 6 6 6 6 2 2 2 2 2; ROLL; SCORE ONES; UNDO; REDO; ROLL; UNDO; HOLD 00000; ROLL. Each semicolon separates a line. The first roll is 1 2 3 4 5, ONES scores 1 (both bonuses remain zero), and scoring starts round 2. UNDO reopens ONES and restores round-1 dice/roll state; REDO re-enters the same score and restores the saved next-turn state. The next roll consumes the five sixes. Undoing the entry again restores the original round-1 hand but leaves the queue consumed; HOLD 00000 clears redo, and the final roll 2 uses the five twos. The face counters reflect all 15 consumed faces. Dart final fields do not themselves freeze List contents, so Turn constructor List.unmodifiable is selected together with the fresh List.of used by rolling. Saved before/after Turn references then hold immutable hands; hold makes a replacement Turn. Player scores and bonus remain mutable, and their selected restoration drives upper/round/total getters. Loaded Queue, randomState and faceTotals are independent and never restored. normalScore branches are retained because evaluate invokes them for every open category with a mutable List input; they prove it is only read. Validation/throw paths precede redoList.clear, and parse dispatch plus StringBuffer/emit establish all scenario output.

## Minimization and alternatives

A single first-turn upper-category entry is the smallest trace that covers score insertion, used-box and bonus state, turn advancement, undo/redo restoration and later dice consumption. Choosing ONES avoids unrelated lower-category scoring; no bot, advice, simulation, records or end-of-game path is needed. The bonus calculation and stored bonus delta are retained even though this trace has zero bonus. Loaded faces avoid depending on a long RNG arithmetic trace, but the generator/queue split and independent counters remain explicit. Full score/undo/redo mutation routes and the selected snapshot or copy implementation establish what is restored; the omitted source is not used to claim hidden mutation absence. Parser, rule defaults, successful validation and output helpers close the trace. Each complete selected line was reviewed for necessity, alternative derived versus stored state was considered, and the excerpt alone was reviewed against the shared requirements.
