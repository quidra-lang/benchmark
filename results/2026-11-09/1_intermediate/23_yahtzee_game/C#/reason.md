## Evidence and language semantics

Common valid input has 10 commands: GAME 1 A; LOAD 1 2 3 4 5 6 6 6 6 6 2 2 2 2 2; ROLL; SCORE ONES; UNDO; REDO; ROLL; UNDO; HOLD 00000; ROLL. Each semicolon separates a line. The first roll is 1 2 3 4 5, ONES scores 1 (both bonuses remain zero), and scoring starts round 2. UNDO reopens ONES and restores round-1 dice/roll state; REDO re-enters the same score and restores the saved next-turn state. The next roll consumes the five sixes. Undoing the entry again restores the original round-1 hand but leaves the queue consumed; HOLD 00000 clears redo, and the final roll 2 uses the five twos. The face counters reflect all 15 consumed faces. C# record values contain an array reference, so State with alone is not sufficient snapshot evidence: the roll clones State.Faces before changing any element. ScoreEntry/RedoEntry retain immutable Turn record properties, and HOLD replaces its scalar hold field. Mutable player scores and bonus are restored explicitly; derived upper bonus/round follow scores. Loaded Queue, RandomState and FaceCounts remain separate and advanced, because Undo/Redo never assign them. The full Normal branches establish that the generic open-category evaluation does not mutate counts. Default nullable array entries are null and numeric fields zero under C# initialization; standard Queue/Stack/List, record with, Array.Clone and nullable arithmetic guarantee the remaining behavior. Validation precedes successful redo clearing, and the switch/exception/output path closes the observable trace.

## Minimization and alternatives

A single first-turn upper-category entry is the smallest trace that covers score insertion, used-box and bonus state, turn advancement, undo/redo restoration and later dice consumption. Choosing ONES avoids unrelated lower-category scoring; no bot, advice, simulation, records or end-of-game path is needed. The bonus calculation and stored bonus delta are retained even though this trace has zero bonus. Loaded faces avoid depending on a long RNG arithmetic trace, but the generator/queue split and independent counters remain explicit.
