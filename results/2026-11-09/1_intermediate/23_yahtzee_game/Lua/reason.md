## Evidence and language semantics

Common valid input has 10 commands: GAME 1 A; LOAD 1 2 3 4 5 6 6 6 6 6 2 2 2 2 2; ROLL; SCORE ONES; UNDO; REDO; ROLL; UNDO; HOLD 00000; ROLL. Each semicolon separates a line. The first roll is 1 2 3 4 5, ONES scores 1 (both bonuses remain zero), and scoring starts round 2. UNDO reopens ONES and restores round-1 dice/roll state; REDO re-enters the same score and restores the saved next-turn state. The next roll consumes the five sixes. Undoing the entry again restores the original round-1 hand but leaves the queue consumed; HOLD 00000 clears redo, and the final roll 2 uses the five twos. The face counters reflect all 15 consumed faces. Lua tables have reference semantics and no implicit snapshot guarantee. The selected roll constructs a fresh faces table and a new turn table, while hold creates a new turn without mutating the previous hand. Score/undo/redo therefore safely retain before/after turn references on this route. Player score tables and bonus fields mutate separately, and upper bonus/round are derived. loaded_head, random_state and face_totals remain separate fields advanced only by roll; no restoration assigns them. normal_score is retained through its alternatives because the generic candidate loop calls it for every open category and Lua does not enforce read-only counts; its body confirms those calls do not change the hand/count input. Validation/pcall and metatable method dispatch, bitmask formatting and output closure provide the complete successful trace.

## Minimization and alternatives

A single first-turn upper-category entry is the smallest trace that covers score insertion, used-box and bonus state, turn advancement, undo/redo restoration and later dice consumption. Choosing ONES avoids unrelated lower-category scoring; no bot, advice, simulation, records or end-of-game path is needed. The bonus calculation and stored bonus delta are retained even though this trace has zero bonus. Loaded faces avoid depending on a long RNG arithmetic trace, but the generator/queue split and independent counters remain explicit. Full score/undo/redo mutation routes and the selected snapshot or copy implementation establish what is restored; the omitted source is not used to claim hidden mutation absence. Parser, rule defaults, successful validation and output helpers close the trace. Each complete selected line was reviewed for necessity, alternative derived versus stored state was considered, and the excerpt alone was reviewed against the shared requirements.
