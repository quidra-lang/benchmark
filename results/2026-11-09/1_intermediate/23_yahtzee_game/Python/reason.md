## Evidence and language semantics

Common valid input has 10 commands: GAME 1 A; LOAD 1 2 3 4 5 6 6 6 6 6 2 2 2 2 2; ROLL; SCORE ONES; UNDO; REDO; ROLL; UNDO; HOLD 00000; ROLL. Each semicolon separates a line. The first roll is 1 2 3 4 5, ONES scores 1 (both bonuses remain zero), and scoring starts round 2. UNDO reopens ONES and restores round-1 dice/roll state; REDO re-enters the same score and restores the saved next-turn state. The next roll consumes the five sixes. Undoing the entry again restores the original round-1 hand but leaves the queue consumed; HOLD 00000 clears redo, and the final roll 2 uses the five twos. The face counters reflect all 15 consumed faces. Turn.copy creates fresh lists through Turn construction, so an entry hand cannot alias later live dice/hold mutations. ScoreEntry retains the Player identity to reopen exactly its box and subtract its stored bonus; the redo pair retains the post-entry turn. upper bonus is derived from boxes, so restoring occupancy also restores it without another saved field. DiceRoller is a distinct Game member and is absent from both restoration routines; deque.popleft and counters therefore stay advanced. Game.roll and set_holds clear redo only after table guards accept the operation. Dynamic handler names, strict integer/name/pattern/category validators and output list emission provide the complete successful route.

## Minimization and alternatives

A single first-turn upper-category entry is the smallest trace that covers score insertion, used-box and bonus state, turn advancement, undo/redo restoration and later dice consumption. Choosing ONES avoids unrelated lower-category scoring; no bot, advice, simulation, records or end-of-game path is needed. The bonus calculation and stored bonus delta are retained even though this trace has zero bonus. Loaded faces avoid depending on a long RNG arithmetic trace, but the generator/queue split and independent counters remain explicit.
