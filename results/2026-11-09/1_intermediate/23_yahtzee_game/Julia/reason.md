## Evidence and language semantics

Common valid input has 10 commands: GAME 1 A; LOAD 1 2 3 4 5 6 6 6 6 6 2 2 2 2 2; ROLL; SCORE ONES; UNDO; REDO; ROLL; UNDO; HOLD 00000; ROLL. Each semicolon separates a line. The first roll is 1 2 3 4 5, ONES scores 1 (both bonuses remain zero), and scoring starts round 2. UNDO reopens ONES and restores round-1 dice/roll state; REDO re-enters the same score and restores the saved next-turn state. The next roll consumes the five sixes. Undoing the entry again restores the original round-1 hand but leaves the queue consumed; HOLD 00000 clears redo, and the final roll 2 uses the five twos. The face counters reflect all 15 consumed faces. Turn, ScoreEntry and RedoEntry are immutable structs; Dice/Holds/Counts are NTuple values of primitive elements. This gives independent saved hand/hold state without copying nested mutable arrays. Player boxes and Game histories are mutable vectors, but restoration explicitly changes only the score/bonus and turn fields; loaded_head, RNG state and face_totals remain advanced. normal_score receives immutable Counts and its upper return supplies ONES, while candidate slots are written by category index. Derived upper bonus/round follow restored boxes. roll_dice! converts dice to a fresh temporary vector and replaces Turn with a Tuple result; set_holds! also replaces Turn, then both clear redo after caller validation. Enum/string parsing and emit formatting establish the same observable trace.

## Minimization and alternatives

A single first-turn upper-category entry is the smallest trace that covers score insertion, used-box and bonus state, turn advancement, undo/redo restoration and later dice consumption. Choosing ONES avoids unrelated lower-category scoring; no bot, advice, simulation, records or end-of-game path is needed. The bonus calculation and stored bonus delta are retained even though this trace has zero bonus. Loaded faces avoid depending on a long RNG arithmetic trace, but the generator/queue split and independent counters remain explicit. Full score/undo/redo mutation routes and the selected snapshot or copy implementation establish what is restored; the omitted source is not used to claim hidden mutation absence. Parser, rule defaults, successful validation and output helpers close the trace.
