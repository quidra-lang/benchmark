## Evidence and language semantics

Common valid input has 10 commands: GAME 1 A; LOAD 1 2 3 4 5 6 6 6 6 6 2 2 2 2 2; ROLL; SCORE ONES; UNDO; REDO; ROLL; UNDO; HOLD 00000; ROLL. Each semicolon separates a line. The first roll is 1 2 3 4 5, ONES scores 1 (both bonuses remain zero), and scoring starts round 2. UNDO reopens ONES and restores round-1 dice/roll state; REDO re-enters the same score and restores the saved next-turn state. The next roll consumes the five sixes. Undoing the entry again restores the original round-1 hand but leaves the queue consumed; HOLD 00000 clears redo, and the final roll 2 uses the five twos. The face counters reflect all 15 consumed faces. Java records hold references rather than deep-copying int arrays; the explicit before.faces().clone() in roll is therefore necessary. The relevant methods never mutate a saved Turn array, while HOLD creates a new Turn. ScoreEntry/RedoEntry safely retain those before/after objects, with the selected start constructor proving fresh zero-roll turns. Player score slots start at -1 and the numeric bonus defaults to zero. Undo/redo modify scores/bonus and turn only, leaving the distinct ArrayDeque loaded queue, randomState and counters advanced. Full normalScore branches establish read-only counts use despite Java mutable array semantics. Guards, exception propagation, switch dispatch and printf/flush close the validation and output route.
