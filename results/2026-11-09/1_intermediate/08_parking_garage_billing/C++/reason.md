## Evidence and language semantics

The command loop sets elapsed minute time, expires waiters before dispatch, and preserves output order. The expiry loop scans the queue in its stored order, removes every entry whose wait is at least 30, retains younger entries, and prints the joined-time-plus-30 deadline. Its other writes are refusal statistics, not parked-vehicle or payment changes. On a successful QUOTE the selected branch only looks up a parked vehicle, calculates charges without loss, and emits the fee line. The fee helpers read payment/vehicle/rate/pass fields and build local amounts; they never assign a vehicle/payment/queue field. Output helpers only format or append/write output. Hence the queue can shrink before QUOTE, while parked data and payments survive; QUOTE itself changes neither. Its selected branch has no admission or payment write. The selected vector/map declarations and const bill/interval interfaces make the relevant garage containers read-only during calculation; Vehicle is passed by const reference. The non-const feeLine body only calls the output append and const location operation. No calculation body is needed to re-establish these guarantees. C++ vector iteration and push_back/swap preserve survivor order.
