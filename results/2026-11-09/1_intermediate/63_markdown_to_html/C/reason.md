## Evidence and language semantics

Use [TOC], a blank line, # Parent {#foo}, a blank line, > ## Foo, then @@END@@. The TOC is recognized before either heading; the quoted line is stripped of > and recursively parsed as a level-2 heading. The explicit heading reserves foo and removes {#foo} from the display source. The nested automatic heading uses raw Foo to form foo, finds it occupied, then chooses and reserves foo-1. Ordinary inline characters are escaped and assembled into the heading HTML; tag stripping that rendered HTML supplies the TOC labels Parent and Foo. Automatic slugging receives raw heading source, before inline rendering. The same final IDs are recorded for both heading attributes and TOC links. The heading-level tree makes the level-2 Foo a child of level-1 Parent. The earlier TOC placeholder is replaced only after the heading traversal, so the leading nav links #foo and #foo-1 agree with h1 id=foo and the quoted h2 id=foo-1. C passes the same Renderer* through blockquote recursion. The zero-initialized Renderer has empty used_ids/headings vectors; calloc in allocate also initializes each new Heading child vector. VECTOR/ADD show ordered vector entries and count increments. string_in uses strcmp on all reserved IDs; the suffix loop stores foo-1 after finding foo. Heading objects retain that exact ID pointer and the tag-stripped inline content. The selected string helpers show concatenation, byte slices and decimal suffix formatting; capacity-growth and allocation tracking do not affect this trace and are omitted. table_of_contents links the recorded Heading objects by level, and document scans and substitutes the completed TOC into the earlier body marker. Explicit IDs are reserved at visit time, not by an inferred global prescan.
