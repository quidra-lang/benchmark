## Evidence and language semantics

Use [TOC], a blank line, # Parent {#foo}, a blank line, > ## Foo, then @@END@@. The TOC is recognized before either heading; the quoted line is stripped of > and recursively parsed as a level-2 heading. The explicit heading reserves foo and removes {#foo} from the display source. The nested automatic heading uses raw Foo to form foo, finds it occupied, then chooses and reserves foo-1. Ordinary inline characters are escaped and assembled into the heading HTML; tag stripping that rendered HTML supplies the TOC labels Parent and Foo. Automatic slugging receives raw heading source, before inline rendering. The same final IDs are recorded for both heading attributes and TOC links. The heading-level tree makes the level-2 Foo a child of level-1 Parent. The earlier TOC placeholder is replaced only after the heading traversal, so the leading nav links #foo and #foo-1 agree with h1 id=foo and the quoted h2 id=foo-1. Go pointer receivers carry one renderer through quoted-block recursion. Omitted fields in &renderer and &headingEntry composite literals have zero values, so heading/child slices start empty; usedIDs is explicitly initialized with make. The map missing-key result is false, which makes the suffix loop stop at the first free ID. Slices of heading pointers retain the same entries when TOC children are attached. main completes r.blocks before evaluating r.toc for strings.ReplaceAll. Reservation is sequential during rendering, without an inferred prescan of later explicit IDs.
