## Evidence and language semantics

parsePlay uses validPoint and throws syntax errors; bar25/off0 remain own numbers. Deep recursive copy makes the trial independent; Black board display reads25-p. positionKey covers both complete rows. Only matching a legal position reaches applyPlay; syntax/rule/unmatched exceptions precede it, and accepted text comes from canonical notation. Numeric notation always uses the moving player's own numbering: Black own point p corresponds to board point25-p, with bar/off remaining special sentinels25/0 rather than ordinary reflected board points. For a Black position with the usual starting checkers and dice1,2, PLAY 1 2 6/4 6/5 requests own6->4 and6->5, i.e. board19->21 and19->20; both are legal, non-hitting moves using both dice. Descending source/target canonical order gives OK 6/5 6/4, independent of input token order. A proposed final position is isolated from current state; the returned/generated legal candidate supplies canonical notation and is committed only after all checks. The generator search itself is outside this parsing routine: its legal collection is consumed by exact complete-position comparison, so evaluation, dice search and game/match scoring are unnecessary to explain recognition or failure non-mutation.
