The caller prepares one graph and uses it for both union and intersection. Each traversal starts with fresh marks and output; therefore the first crossing in the prepared order is initially eligible. During one ring, crossings are marked, points collected in the operation-dependent direction, intermediate noncrossing vertices followed, and the other polygon side entered until returning to the start. Marking prevents duplicate rings only within that traversal. Rings without crossings are handled separately so reusing crossing preparation does not drop them. The raw rings then enter the existing canonical assembly: containment depth determines outer/hole orientation, rotations are applied, and outer/hole lists are ordered in new result containers. Retain prepared geometry and ordering while polygon inputs are unchanged; reset traversal marks, start/current state, operation direction flags and all output accumulators on every call. Persisting an earlier visited set would make the next operation skip its first already marked crossing and suppress a valid first ring.
