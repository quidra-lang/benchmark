The common requirements are to change only the confirmed-track miss threshold from reaches DELETE to exceeds DELETE, follow per-scan miss updates and next-scan live gating/assignment and measurement-driven creation, demonstrate DELETE=2, and retain tentative rules/formats.

The selected Swift scan loop forms live from tracks whose status is not deleted; it builds gated costs from those live tracks and passes them to assignment. Assignment supplies the per-track matched measurement and used-measurement set. A hit resets consecutive misses to zero and increases hits; only the unmatched branch increments misses. The later confirmed-status guard currently deletes at misses >= DELETE, setting deleted status and deletion scan. The proposed boundary is that comparison alone: use strict greater-than. For a confirmed track starting at miss count0 and DELETE2, first unmatched scan gives1 and survives; second gives2, which currently deletes but would survive; a third consecutive unmatched scan gives3 and deletes under the new rule. A hit at the following scan instead resets the count and can retain the track.

Because live is reconstructed at every scan, the newly surviving count2 track participates in the following scan's prediction/gating/assignment. It may consume a gated measurement that would otherwise be unassigned. The separate creation loop makes tracks only for measurements absent from the assignment's used columns, so delaying deletion can reduce or change new-track creation indirectly; creation is not triggered simply because an older track missed. Unassigned measurements still follow that same creation path. Tentative tracks continue through the unchanged hit-confirmation and age/window-deletion branches. Output construction needs no format change; different lifecycle/assignment data can flow into the existing fields.
