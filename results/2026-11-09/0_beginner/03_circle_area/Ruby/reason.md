The area field has the literal label "area=" and exactly three fractional digits. Fixed-point formatting rounds and pads fractional zeros; the radius and circumference calculations are outside this expression question.

The format template and its continued argument line associate area with %.3f. Both complete lines are needed for that association.

The complete area-format expression alone establishes the label and precision. Retaining radius initialization or circumference processing would add no evidence for this expression question.
