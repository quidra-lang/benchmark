[Overview]
Write a program that manages the circulation work of a library. It registers book titles (one title can have several copies) and members and, while advancing the date, processes borrowing, returning, renewing loans, reservations and holds, lost books, withdrawals, the charging and payment of late fines and other charges, and the setting of closed days, and it displays book-name searches, an overdue list, notices, the status of members and books, the inventory, histories and statistics. The program reads commands from standard input, processes them one at a time in the order written, and writes the results to standard output.
All money amounts are handled as integers in which 0.01 is 1 (this unit is called a "cent" below); floating-point numbers are not used.

[Input format]
The first line contains N, the number of command lines that follow. Each of the following N lines is one command. Each line consists of one or more tokens separated by a single space, with no space at the start or end of the line. The program ends after processing all N commands.
In the description below, <...> denotes a part where a value goes. The symbols < > themselves never appear in the input or the output.

The commands are:

Date and closed days:
DAY <day>
CLOSED <day>

Registration and withdrawal:
ADDBOOK <ISBN> <count> <price> <book-name>
ADDMEMBER <member-id> <type> <member-name>
WITHDRAW <ISBN> <copy-no>

Loans and reservations:
BORROW <member-id> <ISBN>
RETURN <member-id> <ISBN>
RENEW <member-id> <ISBN>
RENEWALL <member-id>
RESERVE <member-id> <ISBN>
CANCEL <member-id> <ISBN>
LOST <member-id> <ISBN>

Charges:
PAYFINE <member-id> <amount>
WAIVE <member-id> <item-no>
FINES <member-id>

Display:
SEARCH <keyword>
OVERDUE
NOTICES
MEMBER <member-id>
MEMBERS
BOOK <ISBN>
INVENTORY
HISTORY <member-id>
STATS

The format and meaning of each value are as follows.
- <day>, <count>, <copy-no> and <item-no> are decimal integers without extra leading zeros.
- <ISBN> is a number that identifies a book title; it is a string of digits, the uppercase letter X and hyphens "-". How it is handled is explained in Rule 2.
- <price> and <amount> consist of an integer part and a fractional part joined by ".". The integer part is a decimal integer without extra leading zeros (0 when it is zero), the fractional part is exactly two digits, and "a.bc" means a*100+bc cents (for example, 12.50 is 1250 cents and 0.05 is 5 cents).
- <member-id> is a string that identifies a member, and <type> is a string that gives the member's type.
- <book-name> is the string formed by joining the 5th and later tokens of the ADDBOOK line (counting the command name as the 1st), in this order, with a single space between consecutive tokens (that is, from the 5th token of the line to the end of the line). Likewise, <member-name> is the 4th and later tokens of the ADDMEMBER line, and <keyword> is the 2nd and later tokens of the SEARCH line, joined with a single space between consecutive tokens.

[Processing]
Rule 1. Initial state
At the start, the current day is 1, and there are no titles, no members and no closed days.

Rule 2. ISBN normalization
A title is identified by an ISBN consisting of 13 digits (ISBN-13). An input <ISBN> is normalized to 13 digits by the following procedure before it is used.
(1) Remove all hyphens. Call the resulting string s.
(2) If s has 10 characters, the first 9 characters are digits and the 10th character is a digit or X (ISBN-10): let the values of the characters (a digit has its own value, X has the value 10) be d1, d2, ..., d10 from the front; s is valid if 10*d1+9*d2+8*d3+...+2*d9+1*d10 is divisible by 11. If valid, the normalized ISBN is the 13 digits formed by appending the check digit described below to the 12 digits consisting of "978" followed by the first 9 characters of s.
(3) If s has 13 characters, all of them digits, and the first 3 characters are 978 or 979 (ISBN-13): let the digits be d1, d2, ..., d13 from the front; s is valid if the sum with weight 1 for odd-numbered digits and weight 3 for even-numbered digits, d1+3*d2+d3+3*d4+...+3*d12+d13, is divisible by 10, and then s itself is the normalized ISBN.
(4) If neither (2) nor (3) applies, or one applies but s is not valid, the ISBN is invalid.
The check digit in (2) is, for the 12 digits d1...d12 with T = d1+3*d2+d3+3*d4+...+d11+3*d12, the remainder of (10 - (the remainder of T divided by 10)) divided by 10.
All <ISBN> values that normalize to the same ISBN refer to the same title (for example, 4-00-000005-5, 4000000055, 978-4-00-000005-5 and 9784000000055 all refer to 9784000000055). The output always shows the normalized ISBN. Below, "ISBN" on its own means the normalized ISBN, and "ISBN order" means ascending order of the ISBN regarded as an integer (which is the same as lexicographic order as strings).

Rule 3. Member types
There are two member types, STUDENT and STAFF, with the following rules.
- STUDENT: loan limit 3 books, loan period 14 days, late fine 25 cents per day, late fine cap 1000 cents per loan, reservation limit 2, hold fee 50 cents.
- STAFF: loan limit 10 books, loan period 28 days, late fine 10 cents per day, late fine cap 2000 cents per loan, reservation limit 5, hold fee 100 cents.
As common rules independent of the type, a loan can be renewed at most twice, and the hold period is 3 days. In addition, a loan for which 365 days have passed since its due day is automatically treated as lost, and a member whose balance exceeds 500 cents is blocked (each is explained below).

Rule 4. Titles and copies
- A title has an ISBN, a book name, a price, a sequence of copies, a reservation queue (a sequence of members, empty when created) and a borrow count (0 when created).
- Copies are distinguished by a copy number assigned per title as 1, 2, 3, ... in the order they were added. Each copy is in one of the following states:
  AVAILABLE (can be borrowed), LOANED (on loan), HOLD (on hold), LOST (lost), WITHDRAWN (withdrawn)
- A LOANED copy is also called a "loan". A loan has the borrowing member, the loan day, the due day and the renewal count. A HOLD copy is also called a "hold". A hold has the member it is held for and the hold expiry day.
- Holdings: the number of copies of the title whose state is AVAILABLE, LOANED or HOLD. LOST and WITHDRAWN copies are not counted in the holdings, but they remain in the sequence of copies, and their copy numbers are never used again.

Rule 5. Members
- A member has a member ID, a type, a member name, a set of loans, a set of holds, a sequence of charge items, and a history (a sequence of lines).
- Reservation count: the number of title queues the member is in, plus the number of holds for the member.
- Balance: the sum of the unpaid amounts of the member's charge items (Rule 7).
- Blocked: the state in which the balance exceeds 500 cents (is 501 cents or more).
- Overdue: a loan whose due day is before the current day is said to be overdue. The days late are "current day - due day", and 0 if the loan is not overdue.
- Late fine: the smaller of (the loan's days late * the borrowing member's late fine per day) and that member's late fine cap per loan.
- "Member ID order" is the lexicographic order comparing member IDs by character code (if one is a prefix of the other, the shorter comes first; for example, S1, S10, S2 in this order).

Rule 6. Closed days and deadline days
- The closed days are the set of days set by CLOSED.
- "The first open day on or after t" for a day t is the smallest day that is >= t and is not a closed day.
- When a due day is determined (BORROW and renewal), it is the first open day on or after "current day + the borrowing member's loan period".
- When a hold expiry day is determined, it is the first open day on or after "current day + 3".
- Days late are counted in days including closed days.
- When the current day is a closed day, BORROW is not possible (see Rule 11). All other commands can be used on closed days too.

Rule 7. Charge items
- To charge a member means to append a new item to the end of that member's sequence of items. An item has an item number (numbered from 1 in order among that member's items), a charge day (the current day at that time), a kind, an ISBN (the ISBN of the title of the loan or hold that caused the charge), an amount, and an unpaid amount (equal to the amount when created).
- The kind is one of FINE (late fine), FEE (hold fee) and PRICE (replacement cost of a lost book).
- A charge whose amount is 0 is not made (no item is added, and the item number does not advance).

Rule 8. Releasing a copy
When a copy becomes free, for example by a return, it is processed as follows. This is called "releasing the copy".
- If the title's queue is not empty, remove the member at the front of the queue from the queue and make the copy a hold (HOLD) for that member. Determine the hold expiry day as in Rule 6 and output "HOLD <ISBN> copy <copy-no> <member-id> until <hold-expiry-day>".
- If the queue is empty, make the copy AVAILABLE. Nothing is output in this case.

Rule 9. Ending a loan and processing a loss
- Ending a loan: compute the loan's days late L and late fine F at that time, remove the loan from the borrowing member's set of loans, and charge that member F with kind FINE. Then append the line "<result> <ISBN> copy <copy-no> from <loan-day> to <current-day> late <L> fine <F>" to the end of that member's history. <result> is RETURNED for RETURN and LOST for processing a loss.
- Processing a loss (common to LOST and automatic loss): first end the loan, and append to the end of the history line added at that time a single space followed by "price <price>" (<price> is the title's price). Next, charge the member the title's price with kind PRICE, make the copy LOST, and output "<heading> <member-id> <ISBN> copy <copy-no> late <L> fine <F> price <price> balance <balance>". <heading> is LOST for the LOST command and AUTOLOST for automatic loss, and <balance> is the balance after the charges. After that, if the title's holdings have become 0 and its queue is not empty, output "DROPPED <ISBN> <member-id>" for each member in the queue, one line per member, in order from the front, and empty the queue.

Rule 10. Error handling
The error conditions listed in each command's description are checked in the order listed. For the first condition that applies, output only one line consisting of ERROR followed by a single space and the reason (for example, ERROR unknown member), and the command ends without changing anything. If no condition applies, process the command as described and output the lines as described.
Checking the following three conditions, common to many commands, in this order is called "checking the member and title".
1. There is no member with that member ID: unknown member
2. <ISBN> is an invalid ISBN: invalid isbn
3. No title with that ISBN is registered: unknown book

Rule 11. Commands
* DAY <day>
Errors:
1. <day> is less than or equal to the current day: invalid day
Output "DAY <day>", then advance the current day one day at a time up to <day>. Each time the day advances by one, let x be the new current day and perform the following (1) and (2) in this order.
(1) Automatic loss: for each loan for which "due day + 365" is <= x at the time (1) starts, one at a time in member ID order, and within the same member in ISBN order, process the loss (heading AUTOLOST).
(2) Hold expiry: for each hold whose hold expiry day is before x at the time (2) starts, one at a time in ISBN order, and within the same ISBN in copy number order, process as follows. Charge the member the hold is for that member's hold fee with kind FEE, remove the copy from that member's set of holds, and output "EXPIRED <ISBN> copy <copy-no> <member-id> fee <hold-fee>". Then release the copy. A member whose hold expired does not return to the queue.

* CLOSED <day>
Errors:
1. <day> is less than or equal to the current day: invalid day
2. <day> is already a closed day: already closed
Add <day> to the closed days and output "CLOSED <day>". Next (since <day> is now a closed day, "the first open day on or after <day>" below is a day after <day>), examine all titles in ISBN order, and the copies of each title in copy number order, and output the following lines.
- For a loan whose due day is <day>, change the due day to "the first open day on or after <day>" and output "MOVED <ISBN> copy <copy-no> <member-id> due <new-due-day>". <member-id> is the borrowing member.
- For a hold whose hold expiry day is <day>, change the hold expiry day to "the first open day on or after <day>" and output "MOVED <ISBN> copy <copy-no> <member-id> until <new-hold-expiry-day>". <member-id> is the member the hold is for.

* ADDBOOK <ISBN> <count> <price> <book-name>
Errors:
1. <ISBN> is an invalid ISBN: invalid isbn
2. A title with that ISBN is already registered and its book name does not exactly match <book-name> (uppercase and lowercase letters are distinguished): title mismatch
If no title with that ISBN is registered, register a new title with book name <book-name> and price <price>. If it is already registered, change the title's price to <price>. In either case, add <count> new copies, numbered in order starting from the number after the largest copy number so far (from 1 for a new title), and output "ADDBOOK <ISBN> added <count> total <holdings>". <holdings> is the holdings after the addition. Then release each added copy, one at a time in copy number order.

* ADDMEMBER <member-id> <type> <member-name>
Errors:
1. A member with the same member ID already exists: duplicate member
2. <type> is neither STUDENT nor STAFF: unknown type
Register the member and output "ADDMEMBER <member-id> <type> loans <loan-limit> days <loan-period>". The registered member has no loans, holds, items or history, and is in no queue.

* WITHDRAW <ISBN> <copy-no>
Withdraws a copy, for example a damaged one.
Errors:
1. <ISBN> is an invalid ISBN: invalid isbn
2. No title with that ISBN is registered: unknown book
3. <copy-no> is less than 1 or greater than the number of copies added to the title so far: unknown copy
4. The state of the copy is not AVAILABLE: copy not available
Make the copy WITHDRAWN and output "WITHDRAW <ISBN> copy <copy-no> total <holdings>". <holdings> is the holdings after the withdrawal.

* BORROW <member-id> <ISBN>
Errors:
1. Checking the member and title
2. The current day is a closed day: library closed
3. The member has already borrowed a copy of the title: already borrowed
4. The member is blocked: member blocked
5. The member has an overdue loan: overdue loans
6. The member's number of loans is greater than or equal to the loan limit: loan limit
7. The title has neither a hold for the member nor an AVAILABLE copy: all copies on hold if the title has a hold (for another member), otherwise no copy available
If the title has a hold for the member, lend that copy (the hold is then removed from the member's set of holds); otherwise lend the AVAILABLE copy with the smallest copy number. Make the copy LOANED, with this member as the borrowing member, the current day as the loan day, the due day as in Rule 6, and a renewal count of 0. Add 1 to the title's borrow count and output "BORROW <member-id> <ISBN> copy <copy-no> due <due-day>".

* RETURN <member-id> <ISBN>
Errors:
1. Checking the member and title
2. The member has not borrowed a copy of the title: not borrowed
End that loan (result RETURNED) and output "RETURN <member-id> <ISBN> copy <copy-no> late <L> fine <F> balance <balance>". L and F are the days late and late fine computed when ending the loan, and <balance> is the balance after the charge. Then release the copy.

* RENEW <member-id> <ISBN>
Errors:
1. Checking the member and title
2. The member has not borrowed a copy of the title: not borrowed
3. The member is blocked: member blocked
4. The loan is overdue: loan overdue
5. The loan's renewal count is 2: renewal limit
6. The title's queue is not empty: title reserved
Renew the loan. That is, change the due day as in Rule 6 (the first open day on or after "current day + loan period"), add 1 to the renewal count, and output "RENEW <member-id> <ISBN> copy <copy-no> due <new-due-day> renewals <renewal-count>". <renewal-count> is the value after adding 1.

* RENEWALL <member-id>
Errors:
1. There is no member with that member ID: unknown member
2. The member has no loans: no loans
3. The member is blocked: member blocked
Examine the member's loans one at a time in ISBN order. Check the conditions of RENEW errors 4, 5 and 6 in this order; if any condition applies, output "SKIP <ISBN> <reason>" using the reason of the first condition that applies (the reason is one of loan overdue, renewal limit and title reserved). If none applies, renew the loan in the same way as RENEW and output the same line as RENEW. After all loans have been examined, output "RENEWALL <member-id> renewed <number-renewed> skipped <number-of-SKIP-lines-output>".

* RESERVE <member-id> <ISBN>
Errors:
1. Checking the member and title
2. The member has already borrowed a copy of the title: already borrowed
3. The member is in the title's queue, or the title has a hold for the member: already reserved
4. The member is blocked: member blocked
5. The member's reservation count is greater than or equal to the reservation limit: reservation limit
6. The title's holdings are 0: no copies
7. The title has an AVAILABLE copy: copy available
Append the member to the end of the title's queue. Let p be the member's position in the queue after appending (the front is 1). Sort the due days of the title's loans (LOANED copies) in ascending order (equal values are listed separately), and let the p-th value be the estimate. If there are fewer than p loans, the estimate is none. Then output "RESERVE <member-id> <ISBN> position <p> estimate <estimate>".

* CANCEL <member-id> <ISBN>
Errors:
1. Checking the member and title
2. The member is not in the title's queue, and the title has no hold for the member either: not reserved
If the member is in the queue, remove the member from the queue and output "CANCEL <member-id> <ISBN> queue". Otherwise (when there is a hold), remove the hold from the member's set of holds, output "CANCEL <member-id> <ISBN> hold copy <copy-no>", and then release the copy. No hold fee is charged.

* LOST <member-id> <ISBN>
Reports that a borrowed book has been lost.
Errors:
1. Checking the member and title
2. The member has not borrowed a copy of the title: not borrowed
Process the loss for that loan (heading LOST).

* PAYFINE <member-id> <amount>
Errors:
1. There is no member with that member ID: unknown member
2. The member's balance is 0: no fines
Apply <amount> to paying the items. Examine the member's items in item number order, and to each item whose unpaid amount is not 0, apply as much as possible of the part of <amount> not yet applied, without exceeding the item's unpaid amount, and reduce the unpaid amount by the applied amount. Stop when all of <amount> has been applied or all items have been examined. With the paid amount being the total applied and the change being <amount> minus the paid amount, output "PAYFINE <member-id> paid <paid-amount> change <change> balance <balance>". <balance> is the balance after the payment. Then, for each item to which this command applied an amount, in item number order, output "ITEM <item-no> <kind> paid <applied-amount> remaining <unpaid-amount>". <unpaid-amount> is the value after the application.

* WAIVE <member-id> <item-no>
Waives the unpaid amount of an item.
Errors:
1. There is no member with that member ID: unknown member
2. <item-no> is less than 1 or greater than the member's number of items: unknown item
3. The item's unpaid amount is 0: item settled
Let the waived amount be the item's unpaid amount, set the unpaid amount to 0, and output "WAIVE <member-id> item <item-no> <kind> amount <waived-amount> balance <balance>". <balance> is the balance after the waiver.

* FINES <member-id>
Errors:
1. There is no member with that member ID: unknown member
First output "FINES <member-id> items <number-of-items-with-nonzero-unpaid-amount> balance <balance>". Then output the items whose unpaid amount is not 0, one per line in item number order, as "<item-no> day <charge-day> <kind> <ISBN> amount <amount> remaining <unpaid-amount>".

* SEARCH <keyword>
There are no errors. For the book name and the keyword, make strings in which each uppercase letter (A to Z) is changed to the corresponding lowercase letter, and find the titles whose string made from the book name contains the string made from the keyword as a contiguous substring (characters other than letters, and spaces, are compared as they are). Sort the titles found by the character-code order of the book name with uppercase letters changed to lowercase (lexicographic order; if one is a prefix of the other, the shorter comes first), and titles with the same such string in ISBN order. First output "SEARCH <number-found>", then output one line per title as "<ISBN> <number-of-AVAILABLE-copies>/<holdings> <book-name>". The book name is written as registered (without changing uppercase or lowercase).

* OVERDUE
There are no errors. Sort the overdue loans of all members by due day ascending, within the same due day in member ID order, and within the same member in ISBN order. First output "OVERDUE <number>", then output one line per loan as "<member-id> <ISBN> copy <copy-no> due <due-day> late <days-late> fine <late-fine>". The late fine is the amount if the book were returned that day.

* NOTICES
There are no errors. Creates notices to send to members. Examine the members one at a time in member ID order, and for each member the following lines are targeted for output, in this order.
- The overdue loans, by due day ascending and within the same due day in ISBN order, one line per loan: "OVERDUE <member-id> <ISBN> due <due-day>".
- The loans whose due day is >= the current day and <= "current day + 2", by due day ascending and within the same due day in ISBN order, one line per loan: "DUESOON <member-id> <ISBN> due <due-day>".
- The holds, by hold expiry day ascending and within the same hold expiry day in ISBN order, one line per hold: "PICKUP <member-id> <ISBN> until <hold-expiry-day>".
First output "NOTICES <number-of-target-lines>", then output the target lines.

* MEMBER <member-id>
Errors:
1. There is no member with that member ID: unknown member
Output the following lines in order.
- "MEMBER <member-id> <type> <member-name>"
- "STATUS loans <number-of-loans>/<loan-limit> reservations <reservation-count>/<reservation-limit> balance <balance> <state>". <state> is BLOCKED if the member is blocked, otherwise OK.
- The member's loans, by due day ascending and within the same due day in ISBN order, one line per loan: "LOAN <ISBN> copy <copy-no> due <due-day> renewals <renewal-count>". For an overdue loan, this is followed by a single space and "late <days-late> fine <late-fine>".
- The member's holds, by hold expiry day ascending and within the same hold expiry day in ISBN order, one line per hold: "HOLD <ISBN> copy <copy-no> until <hold-expiry-day>".
- The queues the member is in, in ISBN order of the titles, one line per queue: "QUEUE <ISBN> position <position-in-queue>". Positions are counted with the front as 1.

* MEMBERS
There are no errors. First output "MEMBERS <number-of-members>", then output all members in member ID order, one line per member, as "<member-id> <type> loans <number-of-loans> reservations <reservation-count> balance <balance> <state>". <state> is the same as in MEMBER.

* BOOK <ISBN>
Errors:
1. <ISBN> is an invalid ISBN: invalid isbn
2. No title with that ISBN is registered: unknown book
Output the following lines in order.
- "BOOK <ISBN> <book-name>"
- "PRICE <price> copies <holdings> borrowed <borrow-count>"
- All copies of the title (including LOST and WITHDRAWN ones), one line per copy in copy number order. If the state is LOANED, "COPY <copy-no> LOANED <borrowing-member-id> due <due-day>"; if HOLD, "COPY <copy-no> HOLD <held-for-member-id> until <hold-expiry-day>"; otherwise "COPY <copy-no> <state>" (for example, COPY 3 AVAILABLE).
- One line consisting of "QUEUE <number-in-queue>" followed by the member IDs in the queue in order from the front, each preceded by a single space ("QUEUE 0" if the number is 0).

* INVENTORY
There are no errors. First output "INVENTORY titles <number-of-titles> copies <total-holdings> available <a> loaned <l> hold <h> lost <x> withdrawn <w>". a, l, h, x and w are the numbers of copies, over all titles, whose state is AVAILABLE, LOANED, HOLD, LOST and WITHDRAWN respectively. Then output all titles in ISBN order, one line per title, as "<ISBN> copies <holdings> available <a> loaned <l> hold <h> queue <number-in-queue>". Here a, l and h are the counts for that title's copies.

* HISTORY <member-id>
Errors:
1. There is no member with that member ID: unknown member
First output "HISTORY <member-id> <number-of-history-lines>", then output all of the member's history lines in the order they were added.

* STATS
There are no errors. Output the following lines in order.
- "STATS day <current-day> titles <number-of-titles> members <number-of-members> closed <number-of-closed-days>". The number of closed days is the number of all closed days set by CLOSED so far.
- "LOANS borrowed <b> returned <r> renewed <w> lost <x> active <a>". b is the number of successful BORROW commands, r is the number of successful RETURN commands, w is the total number of renewals so far (renewals by RENEW and RENEWALL combined), x is the number of times a loss was processed (LOST and automatic loss combined), and a is the total number of current loans.
- "MONEY fines <f> fees <e> charges <c> paid <p> waived <v> outstanding <o>". f, e and c are the totals of the amounts charged so far as FINE, FEE and PRICE respectively, p is the total paid amount of PAYFINE, v is the total waived amount of WAIVE, and o is the total of the current balances of all members.
- Sort the titles whose borrow count is 1 or more by borrow count descending, and within the same borrow count in ISBN order, and output at most the first 3 of them, one line per title, as "TOP <rank> <ISBN> <borrow-count> <book-name>". The ranks are 1, 2, 3 in the sorted order.

[Output format]
- For each command, output the lines specified in [Processing] in the specified order. On an error, output only one line starting with ERROR.
- Every separator within a line is a single space, with no space at the start or end of a line (spaces inside book names and member names are as in the input).
- All integers are written in decimal notation without extra leading zeros.
- A money amount with value v in cents is written as the quotient of v divided by 100 in decimal, followed by "." and the remainder of v divided by 100 in two digits (with a leading 0 if it is one digit) (for example, 1250 is 12.50, 5 is 0.05, and 0 is 0.00). Money amounts are never negative.
- The output consists only of ASCII characters, every line ends with a newline (LF), and no blank lines are output.

[Constraints]
- 1 <= N <= 1000.
- Each command name is one of those listed, and the number of tokens of each command and the format of each token are always correct. ADDBOOK has 5 or more tokens, ADDMEMBER 4 or more, SEARCH 2 or more, and every other command has the number of tokens shown in the list. Unregistered member IDs and ISBNs, invalid ISBNs, out-of-range copy numbers and item numbers, and types other than STUDENT and STAFF may appear.
- <day> is between 1 and 3650 inclusive.
- <count> is between 1 and 20 inclusive. The total number of copies added to one title is at most 99.
- <copy-no> and <item-no> are between 0 and 999 inclusive.
- <price> is between 0.01 and 999.99 inclusive, and <amount> is between 0.01 and 9999.99 inclusive.
- <ISBN> is a string of 1 to 17 characters consisting of digits, the uppercase letter X and hyphens.
- <member-id> is a string of 1 to 8 characters consisting of uppercase letters and digits, and <type> is a string of 1 to 10 characters consisting of uppercase letters.
- <member-name> is a string of 1 to 30 characters consisting of words made of letters, ".", "'" and "-", separated by a single space.
- <book-name> is a string of 1 to 60 characters consisting of words made of letters, digits and the symbols "'", "-", ":", ",", ".", "!", "?" and "&", separated by a single space. <keyword> is a string of the same form with 1 to 30 characters.
- The absolute value of every integer appearing in the computation (excluding the ISBN itself) is less than 2^31.

[Example]
Input:
