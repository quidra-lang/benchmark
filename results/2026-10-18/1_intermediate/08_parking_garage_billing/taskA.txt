[Overview]
Write a program that assigns spots in a multi-level parking garage and computes parking fees. Each level provides spots of different sizes, and the program handles vehicle entry and exit, automatic spot assignment according to the vehicle type, a waiting queue when the garage is full, fee computation (free time, a fee per 30 minutes, a maximum fee per 24 hours, a night surcharge, passes, lost tickets, electric car charging, validation time, prepayment), closing spots and moving vehicles, and various displays and reports. The program reads commands from standard input, processes them one at a time in the order written, and writes the results to standard output.

[Input format]
The first line contains N, the number of command lines that follow. Each of the following N lines is one command. Each line consists of three or more tokens separated by a single space, with no space at the beginning or end of the line. The first two tokens of each line are the time of the command, the third token is the command name, and the fourth and later tokens are the arguments of the command.
A time is written in the form "<day> <HH>:<MM>" (example: "3 08:05" is 8:05 on day 3). <day> is a decimal integer of at least 1, and <HH> and <MM> are each two-digit decimal numbers (<HH> is 00 to 23, <MM> is 00 to 59).
In the descriptions below, <...> denotes a part where a value goes and [...] denotes a part that may be omitted; the symbols < > [ ] themselves appear in neither the input nor the output. However, the "->" in output lines beginning with MOVE is two characters (- and >) that are output as they are.

The commands (the part without the time) are as follows.

Facility and fee settings:
LEVEL <moto_count> <compact_count> <large_count> <ev_count>
CLOSE <spot>
OPEN <spot>
RATE <type> <rate> <cap>
FEE <item> <amount>

Vehicle operations:
ENTER <plate> <type>
LEAVE <plate>
EXIT <plate> [LOST]
PAY <plate>
QUOTE <plate>
CHARGE <plate> <kwh>
VALIDATE <plate> <minutes>
PASS <plate> <days>
UNPASS <plate>
MOVE <plate> <spot>
REBALANCE

Displays and reports:
WHERE <plate>
LIST [<level>]
MAP
OCCUPANCY
SIGN
RATES
PASSES
OVERSTAY <hours>
HISTORY <plate>
REVENUE
DAILY <day>
STATS

The meaning of each value is as follows.
- <plate> is the license plate string of a vehicle and is used to distinguish vehicles.
- <type> is the type of the vehicle. The valid vehicle types are the five types MOTORCYCLE (motorcycle), CAR (ordinary car), ECAR (electric car), VAN (large vehicle), and BUS (bus), but other strings may also be written.
- <spot> is a string of the form "L<level>-<number>". For example, L2-14 denotes spot number 14 on level 2 (see 3).
- <item> is the name of a fee item. The valid items are the three items NIGHT, LOST, and KWH, but other strings may also be written.
- The LOST of EXIT indicates that the parking ticket was lost.
- <moto_count>, <compact_count>, <large_count>, <ev_count>, <rate> (base rate), <cap> (maximum fee), <amount>, <kwh> (amount of energy, in kWh), <minutes>, <days>, <hours>, <level>, and <day> are decimal integers.

[Processing]
1. Times and durations
- The time of a command is represented as the number of minutes elapsed since 00:00 on day 1, "(day - 1) * 1440 + HH * 60 + MM". Below, times are handled as these elapsed minute counts, and the difference between two times is a duration in minutes.
- Each command is processed at the time written on its line. This time is called the "current time".
- The date of a time t is "floor(t / 1440) + 1" (the quotient of t / 1440 with the fractional part discarded, plus 1). For example, the date of 23:59 on day 2 is 2, and the date of 00:00 on day 3 is 3. Today's date means the date of the current time.
- When a time is output, it is written in the form "<date>-<HH>:<MM>". <HH> and <MM> are each two digits, with a leading 0 if they have one digit (example: 3-08:05).
- When a duration m (minutes) is output, write "the quotient of m / 60", ":", and "the remainder of m / 60 (two digits, with a leading 0 if it has one digit)" concatenated in this order (example: 25:50 for 1550 minutes, 0:07 for 7 minutes). Below, this form is called duration notation.

2. Initial state
Initially there are no levels, no vehicles, no vehicles waiting to enter, no passes, and no exit records, and all statistics are 0. The initial fee settings are as follows.
- Base rate and maximum fee per vehicle type: MOTORCYCLE has base rate 100 and maximum fee 800. CAR has 200 and 1600. ECAR has 200 and 1600. VAN has 300 and 2400. BUS has 600 and 4800.
- Amount per item: NIGHT (night surcharge) is 300, LOST (lost-ticket fee) is 2000, KWH (charging fee per 1 kWh) is 30.
All amounts are integers (without units). When a fee is computed, the settings at the moment of that computation are used. Changing a setting does not change amounts that have already been computed.

3. Levels and spots
- Each time LEVEL is executed, one new level is added. The level added by the first LEVEL is level 1, the next one is level 2, and so on. Levels are never removed.
- The spots of each level are numbered in order from 1 within that level. First <moto_count> numbers are given to MOTO spots, then <compact_count> numbers to COMPACT spots, <large_count> numbers to LARGE spots, and <ev_count> numbers to EV spots, in this order. For example, on a level added by LEVEL 2 4 5 2, spots 1 to 2 are MOTO, 3 to 6 are COMPACT, 7 to 11 are LARGE, and 12 to 13 are EV spots.
- MOTO, COMPACT, LARGE, and EV are called spot kinds. EV is a spot with charging equipment.
- A spot is written "L<level>-<number>".
- Spot order: the order in which spots are arranged by increasing level, and within the same level by increasing number, is called spot order.
- Each spot has a state of whether it is closed (initially not closed) and the vehicle parked in it (initially none). A spot that is not closed and has no vehicle parked in it is called a free spot.
- For a spot specified by <spot>, if its level does not exist yet, or if the number of spots on that level is smaller than its number, that spot is considered not to exist.

4. Vehicle types and spot assignment
- A vehicle other than BUS uses one spot. The spot kinds each vehicle type can use, and their preference order, are as follows.
  MOTORCYCLE: MOTO, COMPACT, LARGE, in this order
  CAR: COMPACT, LARGE, in this order
  ECAR: EV, COMPACT, LARGE, in this order
  VAN: LARGE only
- A BUS uses three LARGE spots on the same level with consecutive numbers (hereafter called a consecutive triple).
- Spot assignment: when assigning a spot to a vehicle of a type other than BUS, check the kinds one by one, starting from the first kind in that type's preference order, for whether a free spot of that kind exists. Among the free spots of the first kind for which a free spot is found, choose the first one in spot order. That is, the preference order of kinds takes precedence over level and number. If no kind has a free spot, no spot can be assigned. For a BUS, among the consecutive triples in which all three spots are free spots, choose the one whose first spot comes earliest in spot order. If there is no such consecutive triple, no spot can be assigned.
- Vehicle location: the location of a vehicle other than BUS is the spot that vehicle uses (example: L1-7). The location of a BUS is written "L<level>-<first_number>..<last_number>" (example: L1-8..10).
- Location order: the order in which vehicles are arranged by the spot order of the spot they use (for a BUS, its first spot) is called location order.

5. Parked vehicles and the waiting queue
- A parked vehicle has a plate, a vehicle type, a location, an entry time, a charged energy and a charging fee (both 0 at entry), a validation time (0 at entry), and a payment record (none at entry). When a vehicle is parked, it is parked in the assigned spot(s), and its entry time is set to the current time.
- The parked count is the number of parked vehicles (a BUS also counts as one vehicle).
- Waiting queue: a vehicle that could not be assigned a spot may line up in the waiting queue (see ENTER). The queue holds at most 3 vehicles. Each vehicle in the queue has a plate, a vehicle type, and the time it joined the queue. Positions in the queue are counted with the head of the queue as 1.
- Queue processing: the following procedure is called "queue processing". Examine the vehicles in the queue one at a time in order from the head; if a spot can be assigned to that vehicle at that moment, remove the vehicle from the queue, park it, and output "ADMIT <plate> <type> <location> waited <waited_time>". <waited_time> is the duration from the time the vehicle joined the queue to the current time, written in duration notation. If no spot can be assigned, the vehicle stays in the queue. The procedure ends when every vehicle in the queue has been examined once. Therefore, a vehicle further back in the queue may enter before one ahead of it. Queue processing is performed when LEVEL, OPEN, EXIT, MOVE, or REBALANCE has been processed without an error, after all other output of that command has been output.
- Vehicles that give up: before starting to process each command, remove from the queue, in order from the head, every vehicle for which the duration from the time it joined the queue to the current time is 30 minutes or more, and for each such vehicle output "GIVEUP <plate> <type> <time>". <time> is the time 30 minutes after the time the vehicle joined the queue. This output is made before the output of that command, including when the command results in an error.

6. Passes
- There is one pass per plate, and it has a last day (a date). A pass is valid at time t if a pass for that plate exists and the date of t is less than or equal to its last day.
- A pass can be registered regardless of whether a vehicle with that plate is parked, and it does not depend on the vehicle type.

7. Fee computation
7.1 Interval fee computation
For a vehicle with plate p and vehicle type k, the parking fee and the night surcharge for parking from time a to time b are computed as follows, using a free time F (minutes). Let T = b - a.
(1) If the pass for p is valid at time b, the parking fee and the night surcharge are both 0.
(2) Otherwise, if T is F or less, the parking fee and the night surcharge are both 0.
(3) If neither applies, divide the parking time into segments of 1440 minutes each starting from a, called segment 0, segment 1, .... The number of minutes m of segment i is the smaller of "T - 1440 * i" and 1440, and only the segments for which m is 1 or more are considered. For each segment, the billable minutes are m (except that for segment 0 only, they are m - F; in that case m - F is always 1 or more). The block count is the billable minutes divided by 30 and rounded up to an integer (ceil(billable minutes / 30)), and the fee of the segment is the smaller of "block count * the base rate of k" and "the maximum fee of k". The sum of the fees of all segments is the parking fee. The night surcharge is "(date of b - date of a) * the NIGHT amount".
For example, with base rate 200, maximum fee 1600, and NIGHT 300, computing from 07:00 on day 1 to 08:00 on day 2 (T = 1500) with F = 15 gives: segment 0 has billable minutes 1425, block count 48, and fee 1600; segment 1 has billable minutes 60, block count 2, and fee 400; so the parking fee is 2000 and the night surcharge is 300.

7.2 Exit charges
The exit charges of a parked vehicle v (six amounts: parking fee, night surcharge, lost fee, charging fee, total, and amount due) are computed as follows, assuming that v exits at the current time. The lost fee is the LOST amount if loss is specified, and 0 otherwise. The charging fee is the charging fee of v.
- If v has no payment record: perform the interval fee computation from the entry time of v to the current time, with free time "15 + the validation time of v", to obtain the parking fee and the night surcharge. The total is "parking fee + night surcharge + lost fee + charging fee", and the amount due is equal to the total.
- If v has a payment record (loss is never specified in this case): if the duration from the payment time in the payment record to the current time is 15 minutes or less, the additional parking fee and the additional night surcharge are both 0. If it exceeds 15 minutes, perform the interval fee computation from the payment time to the current time with free time 15, and take its results as the additional parking fee and the additional night surcharge. The parking fee is "the parking fee in the payment record + the additional parking fee", the night surcharge is "the night surcharge in the payment record + the additional night surcharge", the lost fee is 0, the total is "parking fee + night surcharge + charging fee", and the amount due is "additional parking fee + additional night surcharge".
- Fee line: the single line "<label> <plate> <location> stay <stay_time> parking <parking_fee> night <night_surcharge> lost <lost_fee> charge <charging_fee> total <total> due <amount_due>" is called a fee line. <stay_time> is the duration from the entry time of v to the current time, written in duration notation.

8. Annotation
The annotation of a parked vehicle v is the string formed by concatenating, in this order, only those of the following items that apply, each preceded by one space (it is the empty string if none of them applies).
- "PASS" if a pass for the plate of v is valid at the current time
- "VAL <validation_time>" if the validation time of v is not 0
- "KWH <charged_energy>" if the charged energy of v is not 0
- "PAID" if v has a payment record

9. Error handling
The error conditions listed in the description of each command are checked in the order listed. For the first condition that applies, output only one line consisting of ERROR followed by one space and the message (example: ERROR unknown type TRUCK), and the command ends without changing anything (the GIVEUP of 5 is processed before the command, regardless of whether there is an error). If no condition applies, process the command as described and output the lines as described. <plate>, <spot>, <type>, and so on in a message are the values written in that command.
Below, "is not parked" means that no vehicle with that plate is parked (this includes the case where it is in the waiting queue).

10. Commands
* LEVEL <moto_count> <compact_count> <large_count> <ev_count>
No errors. Add a new level and output "LEVEL <level> spots <spot_count> MOTO <moto_count> COMPACT <compact_count> LARGE <large_count> EV <ev_count>". <level> is the number of the added level, and <spot_count> is the sum of the four numbers. Then perform queue processing.

* CLOSE <spot>
Errors:
1. The spot does not exist: unknown spot <spot>
2. The spot is already closed: spot <spot> is already closed
3. A vehicle is parked in the spot: spot <spot> is occupied by <occupant_plate> (the plate of that vehicle)
Close the spot and output "CLOSE <spot> <spot_kind>".

* OPEN <spot>
Errors:
1. The spot does not exist: unknown spot <spot>
2. The spot is not closed: spot <spot> is not closed
Remove the closure of the spot and output "OPEN <spot> <spot_kind>". Then perform queue processing.

* RATE <type> <rate> <cap>
Errors:
1. Not a valid vehicle type: unknown type <type>
2. The maximum fee is smaller than the base rate: cap below rate for <type>
Change the base rate and the maximum fee of that vehicle type and output "RATE <type> <rate> <cap>".

* FEE <item> <amount>
Errors:
1. Not a valid item: unknown fee <item>
Change the amount of that item and output "FEE <item> <amount>".

* ENTER <plate> <type>
Errors:
1. Not a valid vehicle type: unknown type <type>
2. A vehicle with that plate is parked: <plate> is already parked at <occupant_location> (the location of that vehicle)
3. A vehicle with that plate is in the waiting queue: <plate> is already waiting
If a spot can be assigned to the vehicle, park it and output "ENTER <plate> <type> <location>". If no spot can be assigned and the queue holds fewer than 3 vehicles, put the vehicle at the end of the queue at the current time and output "WAIT <plate> <type> <position>". <position> is the number of vehicles in the queue after the vehicle has joined it. If no spot can be assigned and the queue holds 3 vehicles, refuse the entry and output "FULL <plate> <type>".

* LEAVE <plate>
Errors:
1. No vehicle with that plate is in the waiting queue: <plate> is not waiting
Remove the vehicle from the queue and output "LEAVE <plate> <type>".

* EXIT <plate> [LOST]
Errors:
1. The vehicle with that plate is not parked: <plate> is not parked
2. LOST is written and the vehicle has a payment record: <plate> has already paid
Compute the exit charges, treating loss as specified if LOST is written, and output a fee line with the label EXIT. Then free the spot(s) of the vehicle, let the vehicle exit, and keep an exit record for that plate (entry time, exit time (the current time), location at exit, vehicle type, total, and whether loss was specified). Also add the exit to the statistics (11). Then perform queue processing.

* PAY <plate>
Pays the fee at a payment machine before exiting.
Errors:
1. The vehicle with that plate is not parked: <plate> is not parked
2. The vehicle has a payment record: <plate> has already paid
Compute the exit charges without specifying loss, keep a payment record (the payment time (the current time), and the parking fee and night surcharge at that time), and output "PAY <plate> parking <parking_fee> night <night_surcharge> charge <charging_fee> total <total>".

* QUOTE <plate>
Errors:
1. The vehicle with that plate is not parked: <plate> is not parked
Compute the exit charges without specifying loss and output a fee line with the label QUOTE. Nothing is changed.

* CHARGE <plate> <kwh>
Errors:
1. The vehicle with that plate is not parked: <plate> is not parked
2. The vehicle is not an ECAR: <plate> is not an electric car
3. The spot of the vehicle is not an EV spot: <plate> is not at a charger
4. The vehicle has a payment record: <plate> has already paid
5. "The charged energy of the vehicle + <kwh>" exceeds 100: charge limit exceeded for <plate>
Let the cost be "<kwh> * the KWH amount", add <kwh> to the charged energy of the vehicle, and add the cost to the charging fee of the vehicle. Then output "CHARGE <plate> kwh <kwh> cost <cost> stay_kwh <charged_energy> stay_cost <charging_fee>". <charged_energy> and <charging_fee> are the values after the addition.

* VALIDATE <plate> <minutes>
Adds validation time, for example for shopping at a store.
Errors:
1. The vehicle with that plate is not parked: <plate> is not parked
2. The vehicle has a payment record: <plate> has already paid
3. "The validation time of the vehicle + <minutes>" exceeds 180: validation limit exceeded for <plate>
Add <minutes> to the validation time of the vehicle and output "VALIDATE <plate> <minutes> total <validation_time>". <validation_time> is the value after the addition.

* PASS <plate> <days>
No errors. If the pass for that plate is valid at the current time, add <days> to its last day. Otherwise (both when there is no pass and when it has expired), register for that plate a pass whose last day is "today's date + <days> - 1". Then output "PASS <plate> until <last_day>".

* UNPASS <plate>
Errors:
1. The pass for that plate is not valid at the current time (including when there is no pass): <plate> has no pass
Delete the pass for that plate and output "UNPASS <plate>".

* MOVE <plate> <spot>
Errors:
1. The vehicle with that plate is not parked: <plate> is not parked
2. The vehicle is a BUS: bus <plate> cannot be moved
3. The spot does not exist: unknown spot <spot>
4. The spot is the current spot of the vehicle: <plate> is already at <spot>
5. The spot is closed: spot <spot> is closed
6. Another vehicle is parked in the spot: spot <spot> is occupied by <occupant_plate> (the plate of that vehicle)
7. The kind of the spot is not a kind that the vehicle's type can use: <type> cannot use <spot_kind> spot <spot> (<type> is the type of the vehicle)
Move the vehicle to the specified spot (its former spot becomes free) and output "MOVE <plate> <old_location> -> <spot>". The entry time, charged energy, charging fee, validation time, and payment record do not change. Then perform queue processing.

* REBALANCE
No errors. Moves vehicles to spots of a kind higher in their preference order. Arrange the vehicles other than BUS that are parked at the moment the command starts in location order, and process them one at a time in that order as follows. If the kind of the vehicle's spot is the first kind in the preference order of its type, do nothing. Otherwise, perform spot assignment for the vehicle at that moment (in the state reflecting the moves made so far) (the vehicle's own spot is not a free spot). If a spot is chosen and its kind comes earlier than the kind of the current spot in the preference order of the type, move the vehicle to that spot and output "MOVE <plate> <old_location> -> <new_spot>". After all vehicles have been processed, output "REBALANCE <moved_count>", then perform queue processing.

* WHERE <plate>
Errors:
1. No vehicle with that plate is parked, none is in the waiting queue, and there is no exit record for that plate: unknown plate <plate>
If the vehicle with that plate is parked, output "WHERE <plate> <location> <type> <entry_time> stay <stay_time><annotation>". <stay_time> is the duration from the entry time to the current time in duration notation. If it is not parked but is in the waiting queue, output "WHERE <plate> waiting <position>". Otherwise, output "WHERE <plate> gone <last_exit_time>".

* LIST [<level>]
Errors:
1. <level> is written and that level does not exist: unknown level <level>
The target vehicles are all parked vehicles if <level> is not written, and the vehicles on that level (for a BUS, whose first spot is on that level) if it is written. First output "LIST <target_count>", then output the target vehicles in location order, one vehicle per line, as "<location> <plate> <type> <entry_time><annotation>".

* MAP
No errors. First output "MAP <level_count>". Then, for each level in increasing order of level, output one line "L<level> <symbols>". <symbols> represents the spots of that level in increasing order of number, one character per spot: # for a closed spot, the uppercase initial letter of the kind for a spot with a vehicle parked in it (M for MOTO, C for COMPACT, L for LARGE, E for EV), and the lowercase initial letter of the kind (m, c, l, e) for a free spot. The symbol string is split every 10 characters from the start, with one space between the pieces (example: "MMCCCCLLLL LEE" for a level with 13 spots).

* OCCUPANCY
No errors. First output "OCCUPANCY vehicles <parked_count> waiting <queue_count>". Then, for each level in increasing order of level, output one line "L<level> MOTO <used>/<available> COMPACT <used>/<available> LARGE <used>/<available> EV <used>/<available> closed <closed>". <available> is the number of spots of that kind on that level that are not closed, <used> is the number of spots of that kind on that level in which a vehicle is parked (for a BUS, all three spots are counted), and <closed> is the number of closed spots on that level. Write 0/0 even if there are no spots of that kind. Finally output "TOTAL MOTO <used>/<available> COMPACT <used>/<available> LARGE <used>/<available> EV <used>/<available> closed <closed> rate <usage>%". Each number on this line is the total over all levels. <usage> is, with q = floor(the sum of the used counts of the four kinds * 1000 / the sum of the available counts of the four kinds), "the quotient of q / 10", ".", and "the remainder of q / 10" concatenated in this order (example: 86.9 if q is 869, 100.0 if q is 1000). If the sum of the available counts is 0, q is 0.

* SIGN
No errors. Acts as the sign at the entrance, showing for each vehicle type the number of vehicles that can enter right now. Output "SIGN MOTORCYCLE <count> CAR <count> ECAR <count> VAN <count> BUS <count>". The count of a type other than BUS is the total number of free spots of the kinds that type can use. The count of BUS is obtained as follows: on each level, consider every run of free LARGE spots with consecutive numbers, extended until it cannot be extended any further either forward or backward (for example, if spots 7 to 11 are LARGE and spots 7, 8, 9, and 11 are free spots, there are two runs: spots 7 to 9 and spot 11), and sum floor(number of spots in the run / 3) over all runs. For a type whose count is 0, write FULL instead of 0.

* RATES
No errors. First output "RATES", then output one line "<type> <rate> <cap>" for each of MOTORCYCLE, CAR, ECAR, VAN, BUS in this order, and finally output "NIGHT <amount> LOST <amount> KWH <amount>".

* PASSES
No errors. The targets are the passes valid at the current time. First output "PASSES <target_count>", then output the target passes in lexicographic order of plate, one pass per line, as "<plate> until <last_day>". If a vehicle with that plate is parked, append " IN" (one space and IN) to the end of the line. Lexicographic order compares character codes (ASCII) one character at a time from the start, and the string whose first differing character has the smaller code comes first (digits come before uppercase letters). If one string matches the beginning of the other, the shorter one comes first.

* OVERSTAY <hours>
No errors. The targets are the parked vehicles for which the duration from the entry time to the current time is "<hours> * 60" minutes or more. First output "OVERSTAY <hours> <target_count>", then output the target vehicles in order of earliest entry time (in location order for the same entry time), one vehicle per line, as "<location> <plate> <type> <entry_time> stay <stay_time>".

* HISTORY <plate>
Errors:
1. There is no exit record for that plate: no history for <plate>
First output "HISTORY <plate> visits <record_count> paid <sum_of_totals>", where <sum_of_totals> is the sum of the totals of the records, then output the exit records of that plate in the order in which the exits occurred, one record per line, as "<entry_time> <exit_time> <exit_location> <type> <total>". For an exit with loss specified, append " LOST" to the end of the line.

* REVENUE
No errors. First output "REVENUE", then output one line "<type> exits <exit_count> parking <parking_fee> night <night_surcharge> lost <lost_fee> charge <charging_fee> total <total>" for each of MOTORCYCLE, CAR, ECAR, VAN, BUS in this order. Each number is the exit count, or the sum of that amount, over all exits of that vehicle type so far. Finally, output a line of the same form with the label TOTAL, in which each number is summed over the five vehicle types.

* DAILY <day>
No errors. For the date <day>, first output "DAILY <day> entries <entries> exits <exits> refused <refused> revenue <revenue>", then output one line "<type> entries <entries> exits <exits> refused <refused> revenue <revenue>", counted for that vehicle type only, for each of MOTORCYCLE, CAR, ECAR, VAN, BUS in this order. The counting rules are as in 11.

* STATS
No errors. First output "STATS parked <parked_count> peak <peak_count> at <time>". <peak_count> is the maximum parked count immediately after parking a vehicle so far, and <time> is the time when that maximum was first reached. If no vehicle has been parked so far, write "peak 0 at -". Then output one line "<type> entries <entries> exits <exits> refused <refused> avg_stay <average_stay>" for each of MOTORCYCLE, CAR, ECAR, VAN, BUS in this order, and finally output a line of the same form, counted over all five vehicle types together, with the label ALL. Each number is over all days so far, counted as in 11. <average_stay> is, if the exit count is 1 or more, with q = floor(total stay * 10 / exit count), "the quotient of q / 10", ".", and "the remainder of q / 10" concatenated in this order (in minutes). If the exit count is 0, write 0.0.

11. Statistics
- Entries: the number of times a vehicle was parked (parked by ENTER, or ADMITted by queue processing). In DAILY, count those for which the date of the time of parking is <day>.
- Exits: the number of times a vehicle exited by EXIT. In DAILY, count those for which the date of the exit time is <day>.
- Refused: the number of times entry was refused with FULL plus the number of times a vehicle was removed from the queue with GIVEUP. In DAILY, count those for which the date is <day>, where the date is that of the command's time for FULL, and that of the output <time> (30 minutes after joining the queue) for GIVEUP.
- The amounts of REVENUE and the revenue of DAILY: sums of the respective amounts of the exit charges computed by EXIT. The revenue of DAILY is the sum of the totals of the exits for which the date of the exit time is <day>. A payment (PAY) is not added to the statistics until the vehicle exits.
- Total stay: the sum of "exit time - entry time" over the vehicles that exited.
- A vehicle removed from the queue by LEAVE is not counted in any count.

[Output format]
- For each command, output the lines specified in [Processing] in the specified order. On an error, output only the one line beginning with ERROR (GIVEUP lines may be output before it).
- Every separator within a line is a single space, and there is no space at the beginning or end of a line.
- Every number is written as a decimal integer without extra leading zeros, except for the two-digit parts of times and of duration notation, and the one-digit fractional part of the usage rate and the average stay.
- The output consists of ASCII characters only, every line ends with a newline (LF), and no empty lines are output.

[Constraints]
