[Overview]
Write a program that plays the card game "Hearts" with four players. It reads a sequence of commands from standard input and performs rule settings, settings of bot strategies (a bot is a player that decides its moves automatically), simulation and aggregation of games played by bots only, and play at the "table". At the table, the deal, the passes and the card plays can be directed one move at a time by commands, and a move that violates the rules is rejected as an error. Moves that are not directed can be left to the bots, and the table also supports taking back a move (undo), declaring that one takes all remaining tricks (claim), analyzing the hands, showing a finished hand again, and more. Random numbers come only from the specified generator, so the results are fully determined.

[Input format]
The first line of standard input is the number of commands N. Each of the following N lines contains one command. Each line consists of a command name and zero or more arguments, separated by single spaces, with no whitespace at the start or end of the line. There are the following 26 command formats.

RULE name value
BOT seat strategy
SETTINGS
GAME seed verbosity
MATCH seed count
ROTATE seed count
NEW seed
ABANDON
STACK seat c1 c2 ... ck
DEAL
SHOW
PASS seat c1 c2 c3
PLAY seat card
AUTO mode
UNDO
CLAIM seat
LEGAL
PREVIEW
SCORE
TAKEN
TRICKS
VOIDS
UNSEEN
STATS
HISTORY
REPLAY h

seat is a word denoting a seat, c1, c2, etc. and card are words denoting cards, strategy is a word denoting a strategy name, and mode is a word denoting the kind of AUTO. How these words are written and what they mean is explained in [Processing]. The input may contain words that are not valid as these words; in that case an error is reported. seed, verbosity, count and h are decimal integers. For name and value, see the description of RULE.

[Processing]
Execute the commands in order from the first line, and output the result of each according to [Output format].

1. Cards
- There are 52 cards, each a combination of one of 4 suits (C: clubs, D: diamonds, S: spades, H: hearts) and one of 13 ranks (from low to high: 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9, T, J, Q, K, A). T stands for 10.
- A card is written as two characters: the "rank character" followed by the "suit character" (e.g. 2C, TD, QS, AH). A card word in the input is an invalid card unless it is a two-character word whose first character is one of the 13 characters above and whose second character is one of C, D, S, H (both uppercase letters).
- The height of cards is compared by rank only. When choosing the "highest card" or the "lowest card" from a set of cards and several cards have the rank to be chosen, choose the card whose suit comes first in the suit order C, D, S, H (e.g. among 9C, 9H, 3S the highest card is 9C). Whenever this document arranges cards "from highest to lowest" or "from lowest to highest", cards of the same rank are also arranged in the suit order C, D, S, H.
- When a sequence of cards is output, it is always arranged "by suit in the order C, D, S, H, and within the same suit by rank from lowest to highest", separated by single spaces. This order is called the "display order". When there are no cards to output, write - in place of the sequence of cards.
- The 13 hearts and QS are called "penalty cards". The point value of a card is 1 for each heart, 13 for QS, -10 for JD only when the JACK rule is ON, and 0 for every other card.

2. Seats and pass directions
- The four seats are called N, E, S, W, and this order is called the "seat order". A seat word in the input is an invalid seat unless it is one of N, E, S, W. Below, N, E, S, W are also denoted by the numbers 0, 1, 2, 3 respectively.
- Cards are played in the order N -> E -> S -> W -> N -> .... The "next seat" of seat i is seat (i+1) mod 4.
- There are 4 pass directions: LEFT, RIGHT, ACROSS, HOLD. The pass target of seat i is seat (i+1) mod 4 for LEFT, seat (i+3) mod 4 for RIGHT, and seat (i+2) mod 4 for ACROSS. For HOLD, no pass is made.

3. Rules
There are the following 7 rules, all initially at their default values. They can be changed with the RULE command, and a change stays in effect until the end of the input.
- TARGET: an integer from 1 to 1000 inclusive; the default is 100. The cumulative score at which a game ends (section 6).
- LIMIT: an integer from 1 to 100 inclusive; the default is 100. The maximum number of hands played in one game (section 6).
- MOON: one of ADD, SUB, AUTO; the default is ADD. How points are assigned when someone shoots the moon (section 6).
- JACK: either ON or OFF; the default is OFF. When ON, JD is worth -10 points.
- QSBREAK: either ON or OFF; the default is OFF. When ON, hearts are broken when QS is played (section 5).
- BLOOD: either ON or OFF; the default is OFF. When ON, penalty cards may be discarded on the first trick (section 5).
- PASS: a string of 1 to 8 characters inclusive consisting only of the 4 characters L, R, A, H; the default is LRAH. The pass direction of the k-th hand of a game (k = 1, 2, ...) is determined by the character at position ((k-1) mod (number of characters)) + 1, counting from the start of this string; L means LEFT, R means RIGHT, A means ACROSS, and H means HOLD.

4. Random numbers and dealing
- The random number generator has a state x (an integer from 1 to 2147483646 inclusive). Each time one random number is drawn, x is updated to (48271 * x) mod 2147483647, and the updated x is the random number. The generator is initialized with seed when a game starts (x = seed), and within a game its state carries over across hands.
- The cards of each hand are dealt by the following procedure. The "specified hands" are those specified by the STACK command at the table (section 12); GAME, MATCH and ROTATE have none.
(1) Each seat that has a specified hand takes those cards as its hand.
(2) Arrange the remaining cards (the 52 cards minus the cards of the specified hands) in display order as an array d[0], d[1], ..., d[m-1].
(3) For i = m-1, m-2, ..., 1 in this order, draw one random number r, let j = r mod (i+1), and swap d[i] and d[j] (if m is 1 or less, no random numbers are drawn).
(4) Let f[0], f[1], ..., f[u-1] be the seats without a specified hand, in seat order, and for p = 0, 1, ..., m-1, add d[p] to the hand of seat f[p mod u].
If there are no specified hands, then m = 52, 51 random numbers are drawn, and d[p] is dealt to seat p mod 4. In every case each seat's hand has 13 cards.

5. Progress of a hand
A game consists of one or more hands, numbered 1, 2, 3, ... starting from the first hand of the game. Each hand proceeds as follows.
(1) Deal: deal 13 cards to each seat by the procedure in section 4, and determine the pass direction of this hand by the PASS rule in section 3.
(2) Pass: if the direction is not HOLD, each of the 4 players chooses 3 different cards from their own hand. The chosen cards are removed from the hand immediately. When all 4 players have finished choosing, each seat gives its chosen 3 cards to its pass target seat, and the cards given are added to the hand of the receiving seat. Since receiving happens after everyone has finished choosing, every seat chooses its 3 cards from the 13 cards it was dealt.
(3) Play: after the pass exchange (right after the deal for HOLD), the seat holding 2C becomes the leader of the first trick. In a trick, starting from the leader and proceeding in next-seat order, the 4 players each play one card from their hand. The leader playing the first card is called "leading", and the suit of that card is called the "led suit". When 4 cards have been played, the seat that played the highest card of the led suit becomes the "winner" of that trick, takes all 4 cards, and becomes the leader of the next trick. The hand ends when 13 tricks have been completed (it may also end earlier by CLAIM).

Terms and rules:
- The trick played while the hand has no completed trick yet is called the "first trick".
- "Hearts are broken" means that in the hand (completed tricks and the current trick together) one or more hearts have been played, or QSBREAK is ON and QS has been played.
- "Playable cards": the cards a seat may play from its hand are as follows.
(a) When leading, on the first trick: only 2C.
(b) When leading, other than on the first trick: if hearts are not broken and the hand contains a non-heart card, all non-heart cards in the hand. Otherwise (including when holding only hearts), all cards in the hand.
(c) When not leading, if the hand contains a card of the led suit: all cards of that suit in the hand.
(d) When not leading, if the hand contains no card of the led suit, on the first trick with BLOOD OFF: all cards in the hand except penalty cards. However, if the hand consists only of penalty cards, all cards in the hand.
(e) Otherwise: all cards in the hand.

6. Scoring and end of the game
- When a hand ends, each seat's "hand score" is the sum of the point values of the cards that seat took in that hand.
- When a single seat takes all 13 hearts and QS, that seat is said to have "shot the moon". In that case, remove the 26 points from hearts and QS from the hand score of the seat that shot the moon (the -10 points of JD remain), and assign points by one of the following methods.
 ADD: add 26 to the hand score of each of the other 3 players.
 SUB: add -26 to the hand score of the seat that shot the moon.
If the MOON rule is ADD, use ADD; if SUB, use SUB. If it is AUTO: if, with the cumulative scores (below) of each seat that would result from using ADD, the game ends (the end condition is below) and the rank (below) of the seat that shot the moon is not 1st, use SUB; otherwise use ADD.
- Each seat's "cumulative score" is 0 at the start of the game, and the hand score is added to it each time a hand ends.
- After a hand ends and the cumulative scores have been updated, the game ends if the number of hands played in the game is LIMIT or more, or if any seat's cumulative score is TARGET or more.
- Rank: seats are ranked 1st, 2nd, 3rd, 4th in increasing order of cumulative score. Among seats with equal cumulative scores, the seat earlier in seat order ranks higher.
- For each game, the following 4 statistics are counted for each seat: tricks is the number of tricks taken (including tricks deemed taken by CLAIM), hearts is the number of hearts taken, queens is the number of hands in which the seat took QS, and moons is the number of hands in which the seat shot the moon. The statistics are updated by adding that hand's contribution each time a hand ends.

7. Bot strategies
Each seat has one strategy set, initially BASIC for all seats. There are 5 strategies: BASIC, LOW, HIGH, SMART, MOON, and a strategy name word in the input is invalid unless it is one of these. A bot chooses 3 cards when passing, and one card from the "playable cards" of section 5 when playing, by the following rules of the strategy set for that seat at that moment.

Choosing passes: arrange the cards of the hand in the following priority order, remove every card that appears for the second or later time, and choose the first 3 cards.
- BASIC: list QS, KS, AS (those held) in this order, then the held hearts from highest to lowest, then all cards in the hand from highest to lowest.
- SMART: when the seat holds QS and holds 5 or more spades (including QS), QS is said to be "protected". If QS is not protected, first list QS, KS, AS (those held) in this order (if it is protected, do not list them). Next, among clubs and diamonds, take the suit with fewer cards among the suits of which at least one card is held (if equal, clubs; if neither is held, skip this step) and list its cards from highest to lowest, then the held hearts from highest to lowest, then all cards in the hand from highest to lowest.
- LOW: list all cards in the hand from highest to lowest.
- HIGH: list all cards in the hand from lowest to highest.
- MOON: list the non-heart cards from lowest to highest, followed by the hearts from lowest to highest.

Choosing plays: below, the "winning card" is the highest card among the cards of the led suit already played to the current trick.
- LOW: play the lowest of the playable cards.
- HIGH: play the highest of the playable cards.
- BASIC:
 When leading: among the suits that contain playable cards, choose the suit with the fewest playable cards (if equal, the suit earlier in the suit order C, D, S, H), and play the lowest playable card of that suit.
 When the hand contains a card of the led suit: if there are playable cards lower than the winning card, play the highest of them; otherwise play the lowest of the playable cards.
 When the hand contains no card of the led suit: if QS is among the playable cards, play QS. Otherwise, if there are hearts among the playable cards, play the highest heart. If neither, play the highest of the playable cards.
- SMART: the same as BASIC, except in the following 3 cases. Here "QS has not been played" means that QS is in neither the completed tricks of the hand nor the current trick.
 When leading: if QS has not been played, the seat itself does not hold QS, and there are spades lower than QS among the playable cards, play the lowest of them. Otherwise the same as BASIC.
 When the hand contains a card of the led suit, there is no playable card lower than the winning card, and the seat is the 4th (last) to play to the trick: play the highest of the playable cards other than QS (if only QS is playable, play QS).
 When the hand contains no card of the led suit: if QS is among the playable cards, play QS. Otherwise, if QS has not been played and AS or KS is among the playable cards, play AS if it is playable, otherwise KS. If neither, the same as BASIC.
- MOON: if any seat other than itself has taken one or more penalty cards in the completed tricks of the hand, choose by the same rules as BASIC. Otherwise as follows.
 When leading: among the suits that contain playable cards, choose the suit with the most playable cards (if equal, the suit earlier in the suit order), and play the highest playable card of that suit.
 When the hand contains a card of the led suit: if the highest of the playable cards is higher than the winning card, play that card; otherwise play the lowest of the playable cards.
 When the hand contains no card of the led suit: play the lowest of the playable cards other than penalty cards. If only penalty cards are playable, play the lowest of the playable cards.

8. Game record lines
As a game progresses, the following "record lines" arise. Each record line has a "verbosity level" of 0, 1 or 2. Parts enclosed in < > are replaced by values.
- hand <hand number> pass <pass direction> (verbosity 1): when a hand is dealt.
- <seat>: <hand> (verbosity 2): right after the hand line, 4 lines with the 13 cards dealt to each seat, in seat order.
- <seat> passes <3 cards> (verbosity 2): when a seat has chosen its 3 pass cards.
- lead <seat> (verbosity 2): when play starts (right after the pass exchange, or for HOLD right after the 4 hand lines). The seat is the seat holding 2C.
- trick <number>: <seat1> <card1> <seat2> <card2> <seat3> <card3> <seat4> <card4> -> <winner> <points> (verbosity 2): when a trick is completed. The number is the trick number counted from 1 within the hand. seat1 is the leader, and the seats and the cards they played are listed in the order the cards were played. points is the sum of the point values of the 4 cards.
- claim <seat> tricks <number of tricks> points <points> (verbosity 2): when a CLAIM is accepted. The number of tricks is the number of remaining tricks (13 minus the number of completed tricks), and points is the sum of the point values of all cards that remained in the 4 hands.
- moon <seat> <method> (verbosity 1): when a hand ends, if some seat shot the moon. method is ADD or SUB, whichever was actually used.
- points N <hand score> E <hand score> S <hand score> W <hand score> (verbosity 1): when a hand ends (after the moon line).
- scores N <cumulative score> E <cumulative score> S <cumulative score> W <cumulative score> (verbosity 1): right after the points line.
- When the game ends, the following 9 lines (verbosity 0) right after the scores line: 1 line game over hands <number of hands played in the game>, 4 lines rank <rank> <seat> <cumulative score> in rank order, and 4 lines stats <seat> tricks <tricks> hearts <hearts> queens <queens> moons <moons> in seat order.
The "record" of a hand is the list of the record lines of verbosity 1 and 2 that arose in that hand, in the order they arose. However, it does not include the trick lines of tricks taken back by UNDO (section 12).

9. Setting commands
- RULE name value: set the value of rule name to value. If name is none of the 7 rule names in section 3, the error is unknown rule. If value is not a value given in section 3 (a decimal integer within the range for TARGET and LIMIT, one of the listed words for MOON, JACK, QSBREAK and BLOOD, a string of the described form for PASS), the error is bad value. If the table state (section 11) is not "No game", the rules cannot be changed and the error is game in progress.
- BOT seat strategy: set the strategy of seat seat to strategy. This can be executed even during a game at the table, and all later bot choices use the new strategy.
- SETTINGS: output the current rules and the strategy of each seat.

10. Simulation commands
GAME, MATCH and ROTATE play games separate from the table, with all seats left to the bots. They use the current rules and strategies, and do not affect the table's game (including its state and its generator). One game repeats the following until the game ends: deal a hand; if the direction is not HOLD, the 4 players choose their 3 pass cards by bot in seat order; and play 13 tricks by bot play.
- GAME seed verbosity: unless seed is from 1 to 2147483646 inclusive and verbosity is from 0 to 2 inclusive, the error is bad value. Initialize the generator with seed, play one game, and output all record lines whose verbosity level is verbosity or less, in the order they arise.
- MATCH seed count: unless seed is in the same range as for GAME and count is from 1 to 50 inclusive, the error is bad value. Initialize the generator with seed only once, and play count games in a row. The generator is not reinitialized in between; the second and later games draw random numbers from the state at the end of the previous game. No record lines are output; the aggregation (below) is output.
- ROTATE seed count: unless seed is in the same range as for GAME and count is from 1 to 10 inclusive, the error is bad value. Initialize the generator only once as for MATCH, and play 4 rounds for r = 0, 1, 2, 3 in this order. Each round plays count games, and in the games of round r, the strategy set for seat i (i = 0, 1, 2, 3) is used by the bot at seat (i+r) mod 4. The ranks of each game are determined by the rules of section 6 from the cumulative scores of the seats that actually played in that game, and the result of seat (i+r) mod 4 is aggregated as that of the original seat i for which the strategy is set. No record lines are output; the aggregation is output.
- Aggregation (common to MATCH and ROTATE): for each seat (for ROTATE, each original seat), compute the numbers of games in which its rank was 1st, 2nd, 3rd and 4th, the sum total of its cumulative scores at the end of the games, their average average, and the sum of each of the 4 statistics of section 6 (tricks, hearts, queens, moons). average is written as follows, where g is the number of games and v = (100 * total) / g. Here / is integer division that rounds toward negative infinity (e.g. -500 / 7 = -72). If v is negative, write -, then write the decimal representation of |v| / 100, then ., then the decimal representation of |v| mod 100 zero-padded to 2 digits (e.g. v = 5788 gives 57.88, v = 5 gives 0.05, v = -72 gives -0.72).

11. Table states
The table holds one game. The table state is one of the following 4, initially "No game".
- "No game": there is no game.
- "Between hands": there is a game, and no hand is being played.
- "Passing": a hand has been dealt, and the passes are not finished yet.
- "Playing": the play of a hand is in progress.
The table's game initializes the generator with the seed of NEW, and proceeds by the rules of sections 5 and 6. At the table, all record lines are output regardless of verbosity level. When a hand ends, the state becomes "Between hands" if the game does not end, and "No game" if it ends. For each finished hand, the table's game stores the hand number, the pass direction, the hand scores, the seat that shot the moon, and the record of the hand.

12. Table commands
- NEW seed: unless seed is in the same range as for GAME, the error is bad value. If the state is not "No game", the error is game in progress. Start a new game (cumulative scores and statistics are 0, there are no stored hands and no specified hands), and set the state to "Between hands".
- ABANDON: if the state is "No game", the error is no game. Discard the game and set the state to "No game".
- STACK seat c1 c2 ... ck: specify the hand to be dealt to seat seat in the next hand dealt at the table. If the number of cards k is not 13, the error is need 13 cards. If the state is "No game", the error is no game; if it is "Passing" or "Playing", the error is hand in progress. If the same card appears two or more times among c1, ..., ck, the error is duplicate card; if a card already specified for a seat other than seat is included, the error is stacked for. If seat already has a specification, it is replaced by the new one. The specifications are used the next time a hand is dealt at the table (DEAL or AUTO GAME), and are cleared at that point.
- DEAL: if the state is "No game", the error is no game; if it is "Passing" or "Playing", the error is hand in progress. Deal the next hand. If the pass direction is not HOLD, the state becomes "Passing"; if HOLD, it becomes "Playing".
- SHOW: if the state is neither "Passing" nor "Playing", the error is no hand. Output each seat's current hand (while passing, a seat's hand does not include the cards it has chosen to pass).
- PASS seat c1 c2 c3: seat seat chooses c1, c2, c3 as its 3 pass cards. If the state is not "Passing", the error is not passing; if seat has already chosen its 3 cards in that hand, the error is already passed; if the same card appears two or more times among c1, c2, c3, the error is duplicate card; if a card not in the hand of seat is included, the error is does not hold. When the 4th player finishes choosing, perform the pass exchange and start play.
- PLAY seat card: seat seat plays card card. If the state is not "Playing", the error is not playing; if the seat to play next is not seat, the error is waiting for; if card is not in the hand of seat, the error is does not hold. If card is not a playable card of section 5, the error is must lead 2C when leading on the first trick, hearts not broken when leading other than on the first trick, must follow if the hand contains a card of the led suit, and no points on first trick otherwise.
- AUTO mode: mode is one of PASS, CARD, TRICK, HAND, GAME; otherwise the error is bad value.
 AUTO PASS: if the state is not "Passing", the error is not passing. The seats that have not yet chosen their 3 cards choose 3 cards by bot, in seat order.
 AUTO CARD: if the state is not "Playing", the error is not playing. The seat to play next plays one card chosen by bot.
 AUTO TRICK: if the state is not "Playing", the error is not playing. Repeat having the seat to play next play a card chosen by bot until the current trick is completed.
 AUTO HAND: if the state is neither "Passing" nor "Playing", the error is no hand. If passing, first do the same as AUTO PASS. Then play cards by bot until the hand ends.
 AUTO GAME: if the state is "No game", the error is no game. If in the middle of a hand, first finish that hand in the same way as AUTO HAND. Then, until the game ends, repeat dealing a hand and finishing it in the same way as AUTO HAND.
- UNDO: if the state is not "Playing", the error is not playing; if no card has been played yet in that hand, the error is nothing to undo. Take back the card played last in that hand, and restore the state to just before that card was played. If that card completed a trick, the trick returns to having 3 cards played, the cards taken by the winner are restored as well, and the trick line of that trick is removed from the record of the hand. Passes and finished hands cannot be taken back.
- CLAIM seat: seat seat declares that it takes all remaining tricks. If the state is not "Playing", the error is not playing; if the seat to play next is not seat, the error is waiting for; if one or more cards have been played to the current trick, the error is trick in progress. The declaration is accepted if, for every card in the hand of seat, no other player's hand holds a higher card of the same suit; otherwise the error is claim rejected. If accepted, seat takes all cards remaining in the 4 hands, and the hand ends with seat deemed to have taken all remaining tricks.

13. Information commands
These commands do not change any state. Below, the "seat to act" is the seat to play next.
- LEGAL: if the state is not "Playing", the error is not playing. Output the playable cards of the seat to act and the card that seat's bot would choose.
- PREVIEW: if the state is not "Playing", the error is not playing. For each playable card of the seat to act, in display order, output what the winner and points of the current trick would be if, after that card is played, the remaining seats of the current trick played cards by bot.
- SCORE: if the state is "No game", the error is no game. Output the cumulative scores, and if the state is "Playing", then also output the sum of the point values of the cards each seat has taken in that hand.
- TAKEN: if the state is not "Playing", the error is not playing. Output, for each seat, the cards it has taken in that hand whose point value is not 0.
- TRICKS: if the state is not "Playing", the error is not playing. Output the completed tricks of that hand and the cards played to the current trick.
- VOIDS: if the state is not "Playing", the error is not playing. In the completed tricks and the current trick of that hand, a seat that played a card of a suit different from the led suit is known not to hold any card of that led suit. For each seat, output the suits it is known in this way not to hold.
- UNSEEN: if the state is not "Playing", the error is not playing. Output the cards that are not in the hand of the seat to act and have been played neither in the completed tricks of that hand nor in the current trick.
- STATS: if the state is "No game", the error is no game. Output each seat's statistics (section 6) for the game. The contribution of the hand in play is not included.
- HISTORY: if the state is "No game", the error is no game. For each finished hand of the game, output the hand number, the pass direction, the hand scores, and the seat that shot the moon.
- REPLAY h: if the state is "No game", the error is no game. Unless h is at least 1 and at most the number of finished hands of the game, the error is bad value. Output the record of the h-th hand.

14. Order of error checks
On an error, nothing is changed and only the single error line is output. Each command is checked in the following order, and the first error that applies is reported.
(1) The command name is none of the 26 formats: unknown command.
(2) The argument words are examined from left to right. If a seat word is invalid, bad seat; if a card word is invalid, bad card; if the value of strategy, mode, seed, verbosity or count is invalid, bad value. For RULE, if name is invalid, unknown rule; if value is invalid, bad value. For STACK, the number of cards is checked (need 13 cards) after all card words have been examined.
(3) Errors due to the state: no game, hand in progress, no hand, not passing, not playing, game in progress.
(4) The errors specific to each command, checked in the order written in sections 12 and 13. The range of h for REPLAY is also checked here. For duplicate card, the cards are scanned from left to right and the first card that has the same card somewhere to its left is reported. For does not hold and stacked for, the leftmost of the applicable cards is reported.

[Output format]
For each command, output the following lines in this order. Words within a line are separated by single spaces, no line has trailing whitespace, and every line ends with a newline. Numbers are written in decimal without extra leading zeros, and negative numbers have a -. Parts enclosed in < > are replaced by values. The period at the end of each item below is sentence punctuation and is not included in the output. Every sequence of cards is written in display order, and - is written when there are no cards (section 1).
- Error: only the single line error: <reason>. <reason> is one of the following.
 unknown command <command name>
 unknown rule <name>
 bad value <the invalid word>
 bad seat <the invalid word>
 bad card <the invalid word>
 need 13 cards
 game in progress
 no game
 hand in progress
 no hand
 not passing
 not playing
 <seat> already passed
 duplicate card <card>
 <seat> does not hold <card>
 <card> stacked for <the seat for which that card is specified>
 waiting for <the seat to play next>
 must lead 2C
 must follow <the character of the led suit>
 hearts not broken
 no points on first trick
 nothing to undo
 trick in progress
 claim rejected
- RULE: rule <name> <value>.
- BOT: bot <seat> <strategy name>.
- SETTINGS: 1 line rules TARGET <value> LIMIT <value> MOON <value> JACK <value> QSBREAK <value> BLOOD <value> PASS <value> and 1 line bots N <strategy name> E <strategy name> S <strategy name> W <strategy name>.
- GAME: 1 line game seed <seed>, followed by the record lines.
- MATCH: 1 line match seed <seed> games <number of games> hands <total number of hands over all games>, followed by 4 lines in seat order <seat> places <number of 1st places> <number of 2nd places> <number of 3rd places> <number of 4th places> total <total> average <average> tricks <sum of tricks> hearts <sum of hearts> queens <sum of queens> moons <sum of moons>.
- ROTATE: the same form as MATCH, except that the first line is rotate seed <seed> games <number of games> hands <total>, and in the seat lines <seat> is followed by a single space and the strategy name set for that seat (e.g. N BASIC places 2 3 1 2 total ...). The number of games is 4*count.
- NEW: new game seed <seed>.
- ABANDON: game abandoned.
- STACK: stacked <seat>.
- DEAL: the record lines that arose (the hand line, the 4 hand lines, and for HOLD the lead line).
- SHOW: 4 lines in seat order <seat>: <hand>.
- PASS: the record lines that arose (the passes line; when the passes are finished, followed by the lead line).
- PLAY: 1 line <seat> plays <card>, followed by the record lines that arose.
- AUTO: the record lines that arose. However, for AUTO CARD, output 1 line <seat> plays <card> before the record lines.
- UNDO: undo <seat> <the card taken back>.
- CLAIM: the record lines that arose (the claim line and after).
- LEGAL: legal <seat>: <playable cards> bot <card the bot would choose>.
- PREVIEW: for each playable card, in display order, preview <card> -> <winner> <points>.
- SCORE: scores N <cumulative score> E <cumulative score> S <cumulative score> W <cumulative score>. If playing, followed by taken N <points> E <points> S <points> W <points>.
- TAKEN: 4 lines in seat order <seat>: <cards>.
- TRICKS: for each completed trick, in order, a trick line (the same form as in section 8). If one or more cards have been played to the current trick, followed by current: <seat> <card> ... (seats and cards listed in the order played). If there are neither completed tricks nor cards in the current trick, 1 line no tricks.
- VOIDS: voids N <suits> E <suits> S <suits> W <suits>. <suits> is the characters of the suits known not to be held, in the suit order C, D, S, H, without separators, or - if there are none.
- UNSEEN: unseen <seat>: <cards>.
- STATS: 4 lines in seat order stats <seat> tricks <tricks> hearts <hearts> queens <queens> moons <moons>.
- HISTORY: for each finished hand, in order, hand <number> pass <direction> points N <points> E <points> S <points> W <points>. If some seat shot the moon, append a single space and moon <seat> to the end of that line. If there are no finished hands, no hands.
- REPLAY: each line of the record of the h-th hand.

[Constraints]
- 1 <= N <= 400.
- Each word in a line is a string of 1 to 10 characters inclusive consisting of printable ASCII characters (other than space). Command names consist of letters only.
- The number of arguments of a command in the formats is as given in its format. However, the arguments of STACK are one seat word and 1 to 20 inclusive card words.
- seed, verbosity, count and h are decimal integers (with no extra leading zeros or +, and negative numbers with a -) of at most 10 digits excluding the sign. The name of RULE is a word consisting only of uppercase letters, and value is either a word consisting only of uppercase letters or a decimal integer of the same form.
- The total number of games played by GAME, MATCH, ROTATE and AUTO GAME is at most 300 (ROTATE counts as 4*count games).
- All values, including the integers written in the input, fit in integers greater than -2^31 and less than 2^31. However, the random number computation 48271 * x exceeds 2^31, so compute it with 64-bit integers. Floating-point numbers are not needed.
