[Overview]
Write a program that manages the floor, kitchen and billing of a restaurant. It registers tables and servers (staff members in charge of tables) and either guides arriving parties to a table or puts them on a waiting list. It sets up the menu, stock and happy hour, and handles per-seat orders, sending orders to the kitchen by course, cooking progress at each station, serving dishes, remakes, voids, and taking stations down and back up. For billing it handles comps (making items free of charge), moving items between seats, discounts, bills, split bills (even split and by seat), and payments by cash and card, and it produces various displays and business statistics. The program reads commands from standard input, processes them one at a time in the order written, and writes the results to standard output.
All amounts of money are handled as integers counted in units of 0.01 (hereafter called "cents"); floating-point numbers are not used.

[Input format]
The first line contains the number N of command lines that follow. Each of the following N lines is one command. Each line consists of one or more tokens separated by a single space, with no spaces at the beginning or end of the line. The first token is the command name, and the second and later tokens are the arguments of the command. The program ends after processing all N command lines.
In the description below, <...> denotes a place where a value goes. The symbols < and > themselves never appear in the input or the output.

The commands are listed below.

Floor:
TABLE <table number> <seats>
STAFF <server name>
SEAT <party name> <party size>
CANCEL <party name>
MOVE <table number 1> <table number 2>

Menu:
MENU <item name> <station> <prep time> <price> <course>
STOCK <item name> <stock count>
HAPPY <discount rate> <minutes>
ITEMS

Orders and kitchen:
ORDER <table number> <seat number> <item name> <quantity>
FIRE <table number> <course>
TICK <minutes>
SERVE <table number>
RUSH <order number>
REMAKE <order number>
VOID <order number>
STATION <station> <operation>

Billing:
COMP <order number> <reason>
TRANSFER <order number> <seat number>
DISCOUNT <table number> <discount rate>
BILL <table number>
SPLIT <table number> EVEN <split count>
SPLIT <table number> BYSEAT
PAY <table number> <payment method> <amount>
CLOSE <table number>

Display:
STATUS <table number>
KITCHEN
FLOOR
REPORT

The meaning of each value is as follows.
- <table number>, <table number 1> and <table number 2> are numbers that identify tables.
- <server name> is the name of a server, <party name> is the name of a party of guests that has arrived, and <item name> is the name of a menu item.
- <station> is the name of a station. The valid stations are the four GRILL, FRY, COLD and BAR, but other strings may also be written.
- <course> is the name of a course. The valid courses are the four DRINK, STARTER, MAIN and DESSERT, but other strings may also be written.
- <payment method> is the method of payment. The valid payment methods are the two CASH (cash) and CARD (card), but other strings may also be written.
- <operation> is DOWN (take the station down) or UP (bring the station back up).
- <reason> is the reason for comping an order line (making it free of charge).
- <price> and <amount> are written in the form "<integer part>.<fraction part>". <integer part> is a decimal integer without extra leading zeros (it may be 0), <fraction part> is exactly two decimal digits, and the value represents "<integer part> * 100 + <fraction part>" cents (e.g. 12.50 is 1250 cents, 0.05 is 5 cents).
- <order number> is the number of an order line (see section 6); in the input it is written as a decimal integer without #.
- The other values (<seats>, <party size>, <prep time> (minutes), <stock count>, <discount rate> (percent), <minutes>, <seat number>, <quantity>, <split count>) are decimal integers.

[Processing]
1. Time
- Time is expressed as the number of minutes elapsed since opening, and is 0 at the start. Only TICK advances time. Each command is processed at the time at that moment. This time is called the "current time".
- The restaurant opens at 11:00. To output a time t, let m be "660 + t", and write "the quotient of m / 60" and "the remainder of m / 60", each as two digits (with a leading 0 if it is one digit), separated by ":" (e.g. 11:00 if t is 0, 12:35 if t is 95). Hereafter this form is called clock notation.
- The difference between two times is a duration in minutes, and a duration is written as a plain decimal integer.

2. Writing amounts and handling fractions
- To output an amount of c cents, write "the quotient of c / 100", ".", and "the remainder of c / 100 (as two digits, with a leading 0 if it is one digit)" consecutively in this order (e.g. 5 cents is 0.05, 123456 cents is 1234.56, 0 cents is 0.00). An output amount is never negative.
- For an integer x >= 0 and an integer r with 0 <= r <= 100, define the following. Here a quotient is the integer obtained by discarding the fractional part.
  rounded r% of x: "the quotient of (x * r + 50) / 100"
  truncated r% of x: "the quotient of x * r / 100"
  r% remainder of x: "the remainder of x * r / 100"
For example, when x is 1235 and r is 8, x * r is 9880, so the rounded value is 99, the truncated value is 98 and the remainder is 80.

3. Initial state
Initially there are no tables, servers, waiting parties, menu items or order lines; all stations are up, and there are no queued or cooking order lines. No happy hour is set, and all statistics are 0.

4. Tables, servers and parties
- A table has a number and a seat count. A table where no party is seated is called "free", and a table where a party is seated is called "occupied". Tables are never removed.
- Servers are kept in the order in which they were registered by STAFF. This order is called registration order. Servers are never removed.
- Checks: placing a party at a table is called seating. Each seating creates one check for that party. A check has a party name, a party size, a table, an assigned server, a seating time, a list of order lines (section 6), a paid amount (initially 0) and a discount rate (initially 0). An occupied table has exactly one check, the check of the party seated there; it is called "the check of that table". A check whose paid amount is 1 or more is called "locked".
- The table load of a server is the number of checks of occupied tables whose assigned server is that server.
- Seating procedure: to seat a party with party name n and party size p at table T, make the server with the smallest table load at that moment (on a tie, the server earlier in registration order) the assigned server, create a check with the current time as its seating time, and make T occupied as the table of that check.
- Table selection: for a party of size p, among the tables that are free and have p or more seats, select the one with the fewest seats (on a tie, the one with the smallest number). If there is no such table, no table can be selected.
- Waiting list: a party that cannot be seated immediately joins the waiting list (hereafter simply called the list). Each party in the list has a party name, a party size and the time at which it joined. Positions in the list are counted with the head as 1.
- Waitlist procedure: examine the parties in the list one at a time from the head. If a table can be selected for that party, remove the party from the list, seat it at that table, and output "SEATED <party name> <party size> table <table number> server <server name> waited <wait time>". The server name is the name of the assigned server, and the wait time is the duration from the time the party joined to the current time. If no table can be selected, the party stays in the list. The procedure ends when every party in the list has been examined once. Therefore a party further back in the list may be seated first.
- Closing a check: to close a check, add that check to the statistics (section 11), put the check in the closed state, make the check's table free, and output "FREE table <table number> <party name> stayed <stay time>". The stay time is the duration from the seating time to the current time. Then perform the waitlist procedure. A closed check is no longer the check of any table.
- For a party name, if the check with that party name is the check of some occupied table, the party is said to be "seated"; if a party with that party name is in the list, the party is said to be "waiting". After the check is closed, the same party name may be used again.

5. Menu, stock and happy hour
- Each menu item has an item name, a station, a prep time (minutes), a price (cents) and a course. Items are never removed from the menu.
- Stock: the stock of each item is initially unlimited. Once a stock count is set by STOCK, the item's stock is limited from then on; the stock count decreases with ORDER and increases with VOID. A stock that has become limited never becomes unlimited again.
- Happy hour: HAPPY sets a discount rate and an end time. Happy hour is active when it has been set and the current time is earlier than the end time.

6. Order lines
- A single order accepted by ORDER is called an order line. Order lines are given order numbers starting from 1 in the order in which they are accepted (voided order lines also keep their numbers; their numbers do not become gaps). When an order number is output, it is prefixed with # (e.g. #12).
- Each order line has an order number, a check, a seat number, an item name, a quantity, a station, a prep time, a unit price, a course, a state, a comp reason (initially none), a fire time and a ready time. The station, prep time and course are the menu values at the time the order was accepted, and the unit price is the value determined by ORDER; they do not change even if the menu changes later.
- The amount of an order line is 0 if it has a comp reason, and "unit price * quantity" otherwise.
- The state of an order line is one of the following six.
  ORDERED: the order has been accepted but not yet sent to the kitchen
  QUEUED: sent to the kitchen and waiting in a station's queue
  COOKING: being cooked
  READY: finished and waiting to be served
  SERVED: served
  VOID: voided
- An order line whose state is not VOID is called an active order line. The list of order lines of a check is in increasing order of order number.
- An order line whose state has ever been COOKING is called a "started order line".
- The order line with order number k "does not exist" when k is less than 1 or greater than the largest order number assigned so far (if there are no order lines at all, no order line of any number exists).

7. Kitchen
- There are four stations, GRILL, FRY, COLD and BAR, and this order is called station order. The number of order lines a station can cook at the same time is 1 for GRILL, FRY and COLD, and 2 for BAR. An order line is cooked in one go regardless of its quantity, and cooking takes that order line's prep time.
- Each station has a queue (a list of order lines) and a set of order lines being cooked, and is in one of two states: up or down.
- Cook-start procedure: to perform the cook-start procedure for station S at time tau, repeat the following while S is up, the queue of S is not empty, and the number of order lines being cooked at S is less than the number S can cook at the same time: take the order line at the head of the queue of S, make it an order line being cooked at S, and set its state to COOKING and its ready time to "tau + prep time". This procedure outputs nothing.
- An order line being cooked finishes when TICK reaches its ready time (see TICK). Order lines being cooked at a station that is down still finish as scheduled.

8. Check amounts and per-seat allocation
The amounts of a check are computed as follows.
- Subtotal: the sum of the amounts of the check's active order lines
- Discount: the rounded (check's discount rate)% of the subtotal
- After discount: subtotal - discount
- Service charge: the rounded 18% of the after-discount amount if the party size of the check is 6 or more, and 0 if it is less than 6
- Tax: the rounded 8% of the after-discount amount
- Total: after discount + service charge + tax
- Due: total - paid amount
For example, if the party size is 6, the subtotal is 13748 and the discount rate is 0, then the discount is 0, the after-discount amount is 13748, the service charge is the quotient of (247464 + 50) / 100, which is 2475, the tax is the quotient of (109984 + 50) / 100, which is 1100, and the total is 17323.

Per-seat allocation: the seats that appear as seat numbers of the check's active order lines are called "the check's seats". When each of the check's seats is given one integer v (0 or more), the allocation of each seat at rate r (percent) is computed as follows. Let V be the sum of the v values.
(1) Set the allocation of each seat to the truncated r% of that seat's v.
(2) Let L be "the rounded r% of V - the sum of the allocations from (1)" (L is at least 0 and at most the number of the check's seats).
(3) Sort the check's seats in decreasing order of the r% remainder of v (on a tie, in increasing order of seat number), and add 1 to the allocation of each of the first L seats.
The sum of the allocations obtained in this way equals the rounded r% of V.

9. Error handling
Check the error conditions listed in the description of each command in the listed order. For the first condition that holds, output only one line consisting of ERROR followed by one space and the message (e.g. ERROR unknown table 9), and the command ends without changing anything. If no condition holds, process the command as described and output the lines as described. Values such as <table number>, <item name> and <order number> in a message are the values written in that command unless stated otherwise.
Errors common to many commands are named as follows.
- Table error: (a) no table with that table number exists: unknown table <table number>, (b) that table is free: table <table number> is free. Check (a), then (b).
- Order number error: no order line with that order number exists: unknown line <order number>
- Modification error: (a) the state of the order line is VOID: #<order number> is void, (b) the check of the order line is closed: #<order number> is on a closed check, (c) the check of the order line is locked: check for table <table number of that check> is locked. Check (a), (b), then (c).
- Unserved error: the check has an active order line whose state is not SERVED: table <table number> has <number of such order lines> unserved items (items is written even when the number is 1)

10. Commands
* TABLE <table number> <seats>
Errors:
1. A table with that table number already exists: table <table number> already exists
Add a table with that number and seat count in the free state, and output "TABLE <table number> seats <seats>". Then perform the waitlist procedure.

* STAFF <server name>
Errors:
1. A server with that server name already exists: <server name> is already on duty
Add the server at the end of registration order, and output "STAFF <server name>".

* SEAT <party name> <party size>
Errors:
1. The party with that party name is seated: <party name> is already seated at table <table number of that party's check>
2. The party with that party name is waiting: <party name> is already waiting
3. There is no table whose seat count is greater than or equal to the party size (occupied tables are included in this check; this includes the case where there are no tables at all): no table for party of <party size>
4. There are no servers at all: no server on duty
If a table can be selected for the party, seat the party at that table and output "SEAT <party name> <party size> table <table number> server <server name>" (the server name is the name of the assigned server). Even if there are parties in the list, the party is seated immediately if a table can be selected. If no table can be selected, the party joins the end of the list at the current time, and "WAIT <party name> <party size> position <position>" is output. The position is the number of parties in the list after joining.

* CANCEL <party name>
Removes a party that has stopped waiting from the list.
Errors:
1. The party with that party name is not waiting: <party name> is not waiting
Remove the party from the list, and output "CANCEL <party name> <party size> waited <wait time>". The party size is the size of that party, and the wait time is the duration from the time the party joined to the current time.

* MOVE <table number 1> <table number 2>
Moves the party at the table with table number 1 to the table with table number 2.
Errors:
1. No table with table number 1 exists: unknown table <table number 1>
2. No table with table number 2 exists: unknown table <table number 2>
3. The table with table number 1 is free: table <table number 1> is free
4. The table with table number 2 is occupied (this includes the case where the two numbers are the same): table <table number 2> is occupied
5. The seat count of the table with table number 2 is less than the party size of the check of the table with table number 1: table <table number 2> is too small for <party name of that check>
Make the check of the table with table number 1 the check of the table with table number 2 (the table with table number 1 becomes free and the table with table number 2 becomes occupied), and output "MOVE <party name> table <table number 1> -> <table number 2>". The other contents of the check (assigned server, seating time, order lines, etc.) do not change. Then perform the waitlist procedure.

* MENU <item name> <station> <prep time> <price> <course>
Errors:
1. Not a valid station: unknown station <station>
2. Not a valid course: unknown course <course>
If there is no menu item with that item name, add the item and output "MENU ADD <item name> <station> <prep time> <price> <course>". If it already exists, replace the item's station, prep time, price and course, and output "MENU UPDATE <item name> <station> <prep time> <price> <course>". The price is written in the form of section 2. The stock does not change.

* STOCK <item name> <stock count>
Errors:
1. There is no menu item with that item name: unknown item <item name>
Make the item's stock limited, set its stock count to <stock count>, and output "STOCK <item name> <stock count>".

* HAPPY <discount rate> <minutes>
There are no errors. Set happy hour (replacing any previous setting) with discount rate <discount rate> and end time "current time + minutes". Then output "HAPPY <discount rate>% until <end time in clock notation>".

* ITEMS
There are no errors. First output "ITEMS <number of menu items>". Then sort the menu items in course order (the order DRINK, STARTER, MAIN, DESSERT), and within the same course in lexicographic order of item name, and output one line per item: "<course> <item name> <station> <prep time> <price> <stock>". <stock> is - if the stock is unlimited, and the stock count if it is limited.
Lexicographic order compares characters one by one from the beginning by character code (ASCII); the string whose first differing character has the smaller character code comes first. If one string is a prefix of the other, the shorter one comes first.

* ORDER <table number> <seat number> <item name> <quantity>
Errors:
1. Table error
2. The check of that table is locked: check for table <table number> is locked
3. The seat number is greater than the party size of the check of that table: seat <seat number> out of range for table <table number>
4. There is no menu item with that item name: unknown item <item name>
5. The item's stock is limited and the stock count is less than the quantity: only <stock count> <item name> left
Create a new order line and add it to the end of the list of order lines of the check of that table. Its state is ORDERED; the seat number, item name and quantity are the values in the command; the station, prep time and course are the item's menu values. The unit price is "price - the rounded (happy hour discount rate)% of the price" if the item's course is DRINK and happy hour is active, and the price otherwise. Then output "ORDER #<order number> table <table number> seat <seat number> <item name> x<quantity> <amount>". The amount is the amount of the order line. If the item's course is DRINK and happy hour was active (including the case where the discount is 0 and the unit price equals the price), append " HAPPY" (one space and HAPPY) to the end of this line. Then, if the item's stock is limited, subtract the quantity from the stock count, and if the stock count becomes 0 as a result, output "SOLDOUT <item name>".

* FIRE <table number> <course>
Sends the order lines of that course to the kitchen together.
Errors:
1. Table error
2. Not a valid course: unknown course <course>
3. The check of that table has no order line of that course whose state is ORDERED: nothing to fire for <course> at table <table number>
4. The course is MAIN or DESSERT, and among the courses that come before it in the order STARTER, MAIN, DESSERT, there is a course for which the check of that table still has an order line whose state is ORDERED: fire <the earliest such course> first
(DRINK can be fired at any time, and other courses can be fired even if DRINK order lines remain. STARTER is not restricted by earlier courses either.)
Take the order lines of the check of that table that are of that course and whose state is ORDERED one at a time in increasing order of order number, set the state to QUEUED and the fire time to the current time, and add the order line to the end of the queue of its station. Then output "FIRE table <table number> <course> <number list>". The number list is the "#<order number>" of the fired order lines in the order they were fired, separated by a single space. Then, at the current time, perform the cook-start procedure for all stations in station order.

* TICK <minutes>
Advances time. There are no errors. Let the target time E be "current time + minutes", and first output "TICK <E in clock notation>". Then repeat the following (1) to (3).
(1) If there are no order lines being cooked, or if the minimum c of the ready times of the order lines being cooked is greater than E, end the repetition.
(2) Finish all order lines being cooked whose ready time is c. The order is station order, and within the same station increasing order of order number. For each order line, remove it from the order lines being cooked, set its state to READY, add the ticket time "c - fire time" to that station's record (section 11), and output "READY <c in clock notation> <station> #<order number> <item name> x<quantity> table <table number> seat <seat number>". The table number is the number of the table of the order line's check at that moment.
(3) At time c, perform the cook-start procedure for all stations in station order, and go back to (1).
When the repetition ends, set the current time to E.

* SERVE <table number>
Errors:
1. Table error
2. The check of that table has no order line whose state is READY: nothing ready for table <table number>
Set every order line of the check of that table whose state is READY to SERVED, in increasing order of order number, and add its quantity to the served count (section 11) of its item name. Then output "SERVE table <table number> <number list>". The number list is the "#<order number>" of the served order lines in increasing order of order number, separated by a single space.

* RUSH <order number>
Hurries an order line that has been sent to the kitchen.
Errors:
1. Order number error
2. The state of the order line is not QUEUED: #<order number> is not queued
Take the order line out of its queue, put it back at the head of the same queue, and output "RUSH #<order number> <station>" (<station> is the station of the order line).

* REMAKE <order number>
Remakes a dish that is finished or has been served.
Errors:
1. Order number error
2. The check of the order line is closed: #<order number> is on a closed check
3. The state of the order line is neither READY nor SERVED: #<order number> is not ready or served
Set the state of the order line to QUEUED and its fire time to the current time, put it at the head of the queue of its station, add 1 to that station's remake count (section 11), and output "REMAKE #<order number> <item name> <station>". Then, at the current time, perform the cook-start procedure for that station. The unit price, amount and comp reason do not change. A remake is possible even if the check is locked.

* VOID <order number>
Errors:
1. Order number error
2. The state of the order line is VOID: #<order number> is already void
3. The order line is a started order line: #<order number> has already started
If the state of the order line is QUEUED, remove the order line from its queue. Set the state of the order line to VOID, and if the item's stock is limited at this point, add the quantity to the stock count. Add 1 to the void count (section 11), and output "VOID #<order number> <item name> x<quantity>".

* STATION <station> <operation>
Errors:
1. Not a valid station: unknown station <station>
2. The operation is DOWN and the station is down: station <station> is already down
3. The operation is UP and the station is up: station <station> is not down
If DOWN, put the station in the down state. A station that is down does not start cooking new order lines (the cook-start procedure does nothing). If UP, put the station in the up state, and perform the cook-start procedure for that station at the current time. In either case, output "STATION <station> <operation>".

* COMP <order number> <reason>
Comps an order line (makes it free of charge).
Errors:
1. Order number error
2. Modification error
3. The order line already has a comp reason: #<order number> is already comped
Output "COMP #<order number> <item name> <amount> <reason>". The amount is the amount of the order line before it is comped. Add 1 to the comp count and this amount to the comp amount (section 11), and set the comp reason of the order line to <reason>.

* TRANSFER <order number> <seat number>
Moves an order line to another seat on the same check.
Errors:
1. Order number error
2. Modification error
3. The seat number is greater than the party size of the order line's check: seat <seat number> out of range for table <table number of that check>
4. The seat number is the same as the seat number of the order line: #<order number> is already on seat <seat number>
Output "TRANSFER #<order number> seat <old seat number> -> <seat number>", and set the seat number of the order line to <seat number>.

* DISCOUNT <table number> <discount rate>
Errors:
1. Table error
2. The check of that table is locked: check for table <table number> is locked
Set the discount rate of the check of that table to <discount rate> (0 means back to no discount), and output "DISCOUNT table <table number> <discount rate>%".

* BILL <table number>
Errors:
1. Table error
Output the bill of the check of that table. First output "BILL table <table number> <party name> guests <party size>". Then, for each of the check's seats in increasing order of seat number, output "SEAT <seat number> <seat subtotal>", followed by the active order lines with that seat number in increasing order of order number, one per line, as "#<order number> <item name> x<quantity> <amount>". The seat subtotal is the sum of the amounts of the active order lines with that seat number. For an order line with a comp reason, append " COMP <reason>" to the end of the line. Then output "SUBTOTAL <subtotal>", then "DISCOUNT <discount rate>% <discount>" if the discount rate is not 0, and "SERVICE <service charge>" if the party size is 6 or more, and then output "TAX <tax>" and "TOTAL <total>". If the check is locked, finally output "PAID <paid amount>" and "DUE <due>".

* SPLIT <table number> EVEN <split count>
* SPLIT <table number> BYSEAT
Shows the amounts of a split bill. Changes nothing.
Errors:
1. Table error
2. The due of the check of that table is 0: nothing to split for table <table number>
3. EVEN, and the split count is greater than the party size of the check of that table: cannot split table <table number> <split count> ways
4. BYSEAT, and the check of that table is locked: table <table number> has payments
For EVEN: divide the due D among k people, where k is the split count. Let q be the quotient of D / k and r the remainder. First output "SPLIT table <table number> EVEN <k> due <D>", then, for i = 1, 2, ..., k in this order, output "PAYER <i> <amount>". The amount is q + 1 if i <= r, and q otherwise.
For BYSEAT: compute an amount for each of the check's seats. First, the discount of each seat is its allocation at rate "the check's discount rate" with v = each seat's subtotal, and the after-discount amount of each seat is "subtotal - discount". If the party size is 6 or more, the service charge of each seat is its allocation at rate 18 with v = each seat's after-discount amount; if it is less than 6, the service charge of each seat is 0. The tax of each seat is its allocation at rate 8 with v = each seat's after-discount amount. The total of each seat is "after discount + service charge + tax". First output "SPLIT table <table number> BYSEAT total <total of the check>", then output one line per seat of the check in increasing order of seat number: "SEAT <seat number> subtotal <subtotal> discount <discount> service <service charge> tax <tax> total <total>". The sum of the totals of the seats equals the total of the check.

* PAY <table number> <payment method> <amount>
Errors:
1. Table error
2. Not a valid payment method: unknown method <payment method>
3. Unserved error for the check of that table
4. The due of the check of that table is 0: nothing to pay for table <table number>
Let D be the due and a be <amount>, and let the applied amount be the smaller of a and D. Add the applied amount to the paid amount of the check, and also to the amount received for that payment method (section 11).
- If a is less than D: output "PAY table <table number> <payment method> <a> remaining <D - a>".
- If a is D or more: if the payment method is CASH, output "PAY table <table number> CASH <a> change <a - D>" (a - D is the change). If it is CARD, output "PAY table <table number> CARD <a> tip <a - D>" (a - D is the tip), and add a - D to the tips total and to the tips of the check's assigned server (section 11). In either case, then close the check (section 4).

* CLOSE <table number>
Closes a check without payment (for example when nothing was ordered, or when everything was comped).
Errors:
1. Table error
2. Unserved error for the check of that table
3. The due of the check of that table is not 0: table <table number> still owes <due>
Close the check of that table (section 4).

* STATUS <table number>
Errors:
1. Table error
First output "STATUS table <table number> <party name> guests <party size> since <seating time in clock notation>", then output all order lines of the check of that table (including those whose state is VOID) in increasing order of order number, one per line, as "#<order number> seat <seat number> <item name> x<quantity> <course> <state>". For an order line whose state is COOKING, append " until <ready time in clock notation>" to the end of the line, and for an order line with a comp reason, append " COMP <reason>" to the end of the line (when both are appended, until comes first).

* KITCHEN
There are no errors. First output "KITCHEN <current time in clock notation>", then output one line per station in station order: "<station> <status> cooking <cooking list> queue <queue list>". The status is up if the station is up, and down if it is down. The cooking list is the order lines being cooked at that station in increasing order of ready time (on a tie, increasing order of order number), each written as "#<order number>@<ready time in clock notation>", separated by a single space. The queue list is the order lines in the queue from the head, each written as "#<order number>", separated by a single space. Either list is written as - if it is empty.

* FLOOR
There are no errors. First output "FLOOR <current time in clock notation> free <number of free tables> seated <number of occupied tables> waiting <number of parties in the list>". Then output all tables in increasing order of number, one per line. A free table is "table <table number> seats <seats> free", and an occupied table is "table <table number> seats <seats> <party name> guests <party size> server <server name> since <seating time in clock notation> items <number of active order lines> total <total>" (party name through total are the values of the check of that table). If the check of an occupied table is locked, append " paid <paid amount>" to the end of the line. Finally, output the parties in the list from the head, one per line, as "WAITING <position> <party name> <party size> since <time joined in clock notation>".

* REPORT
There are no errors. Output the statistics of section 11 as lines in the following order.
"REPORT <current time in clock notation>"
"COVERS <guest count> parties <party count>"
"TURNS <number of closed checks> avg_stay <average stay>"
"SALES DRINK <amount> STARTER <amount> MAIN <amount> DESSERT <amount>"
"TOTALS subtotal <amount> discount <amount> service <amount> tax <amount> total <amount>"
"PAYMENTS cash <amount> card <amount> tips <amount>"
"COMPS <comp count> <comp amount> VOIDS <void count>"
"TOP <rank> <item name> <served count>" (0 to 3 lines)
"<station> tickets <count> avg <average> max <maximum> remakes <remake count>" (4 lines, in station order)
"SERVER <server name> tables <table load> parties <parties assigned> sales <amount> tips <amount>" (in registration order of servers, one line per server)

11. Statistics
- Guest count and party count: the sum of the party sizes of the parties that were seated (both by SEAT and by the waitlist procedure), and the number of seatings.
- Number of closed checks: the number of times a check was closed (both by PAY and by CLOSE). The average stay is, if the number of closed checks is 1 or more, the quotient (fractional part discarded) of "sum of the stay times at closing / number of closed checks", and is written as - if it is 0.
- SALES amounts: the amounts of the active order lines of closed checks, summed per course of the order line (amounts that do not include discount, service charge or tax).
- TOTALS amounts: the subtotals, discounts, service charges, taxes and totals of closed checks, each summed using the values at the time of closing.
- PAYMENTS amounts: cash and card are the sums of the applied amounts of PAY for CASH and CARD respectively (including payments to checks that are not yet closed). tips is the total of the tips that arose from CARD payments.
- Comp count and comp amount: the number of successful COMP commands, and the sum of the amounts output by them.
- Void count: the number of successful VOID commands.
- Served count: for each item name, the sum of the quantities served by SERVE (portions served again after a remake are also counted). Sort the items whose served count is 1 or more in decreasing order of served count (on a tie, lexicographic order of item name), and output "TOP <rank> <item name> <served count>" for at most the first 3 items. The rank is the position in the sorted order (1, 2, 3).
- Per-station record: the count is the number of order lines of that station finished by TICK (including remakes), the maximum is the maximum of their ticket times, and the average is the quotient (fractional part discarded) of "sum of the ticket times / count". If the count is 0, write "<station> tickets 0 avg - max - remakes <remake count>". The remake count is the number of successful REMAKE commands for order lines of that station.
- Per-server record: the table load is the value at the time of REPORT (section 4), parties assigned is the number of seatings with that server as the assigned server, sales is the sum of the totals at closing of the checks whose assigned server is that server, and tips is the sum of the tips that arose on checks whose assigned server is that server.

[Output format]
- For each command, output the lines specified in [Processing] in the specified order. On an error, output only one line starting with ERROR.
- Every separator within a line is a single space, and there are no spaces at the beginning or end of a line.
- Integers are written as decimal integers without extra leading zeros, except for the two-digit parts of clock notation and the two-digit fractional part of amounts.
- The output consists of ASCII characters only, every line ends with a newline (LF), and no blank lines are output.

[Constraints]
- 1 <= N <= 1000. The command name, the number of arguments and the format of each token are always correct, but a command may refer to a table, server, party, item or order number that does not exist.
- Integers in the input are written without extra leading zeros.
- <server name> and <party name> consist of 1 to 10 uppercase English letters, <item name> consists of 1 to 12 uppercase English letters, <station>, <course> and <payment method> consist of 1 to 10 uppercase English letters, and <reason> consists of 1 to 12 lowercase English letters. The <operation> of STATION is DOWN or UP, and the second argument of SPLIT is EVEN or BYSEAT; <split count> follows only for EVEN.
- 1 <= table number <= 99, 1 <= seats <= 20, 1 <= party size <= 20, 1 <= seat number <= 20, 1 <= quantity <= 20, 1 <= order number <= 9999.
- 1 <= prep time <= 60, 0.00 <= price <= 500.00, 0 <= stock count <= 999.
- The discount rate of DISCOUNT is between 0 and 100 inclusive, the discount rate of HAPPY is between 1 and 100 inclusive, and the minutes of HAPPY are between 1 and 120 inclusive.
- 2 <= split count <= 20, 0.01 <= amount of PAY <= 99999.99.
- The minutes of TICK are 1 or more, and the sum of the minutes of all TICK commands is at most 600.
- The absolute value of every integer that appears in the computation (including amounts in cents) is less than 2^31.
