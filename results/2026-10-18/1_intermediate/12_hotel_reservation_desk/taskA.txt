[Overview]
Write a program that handles the front-desk operations of a hotel. It covers registering rooms and rates, seasonal rates, blocking rooms, accepting, modifying and cancelling reservations, a waitlist, check-in, room moves, stay extensions, extra charges and deposits, checkout settlement, invoices, and various reports (availability, occupancy, revenue metrics, room status, reservation lists, arrivals and departures, guest history, and revenue). The program reads commands one line at a time from standard input and, for each command, writes the result in the specified format to standard output.
Dates are given in the form "year-month-day". Do not use the language's date library for date calculations; instead, convert dates to the day numbers (the number of days since January 1, 2000) defined in [Processing] and calculate with those. All amounts of money are handled as integers counted in cents (units of 1/100), and all calculations are done with integers.

[Input format]
The first line contains N, the number of command lines that follow. Starting from line 2, N lines follow, each containing one command. Each line is a sequence of tokens separated by a single ASCII space, with no whitespace at the beginning or end of the line. The program ends after processing all N command lines.

The token formats are as follows.
- Room number: a decimal integer from 1 to 9999, with no extra leading zeros (e.g. 101).
- Room type: 1 to 8 characters consisting only of uppercase letters (A-Z). Only the three types SINGLE, DOUBLE and SUITE are valid; any other (e.g. TWIN) causes the error defined in [Processing].
- Date: 10 characters in the form "YYYY-MM-DD". YYYY is 4 digits, MM and DD are 2 digits each, and they are separated by ASCII hyphens "-" (e.g. 2026-03-01). The characters always follow this form, but a date that does not exist (e.g. 2026-02-30) may be given.
- Reservation ID: 1 to 8 characters that start with an uppercase letter and consist only of uppercase letters and digits (0-9) (e.g. R12, A7).
- Guest name, item name: 1 to 16 characters consisting only of lowercase letters (a-z) (e.g. alice, minibar).
- Rate, amount: an integer part and a fractional part joined by ".". The integer part is a decimal number from 0 to 99999 with no extra leading zeros ("0" when it is 0). The fractional part is exactly 2 digits (e.g. 0.00, 12.50, 300.00). "a.bc" represents the integer a*100+bc counted in cents.
- Percentage: a decimal integer from 1 to 500, with no extra leading zeros.
- Status: one of booked, inhouse, checkedout, cancelled, noshow.

There are the following 28 kinds of commands. Each <...> part is filled with a token in the format above.
ROOM <room number> <room type> <rate>
RATE <room number> <rate>
SEASON <start date> <end date> <percentage>
BLOCK <room number> <start date> <end date>
UNBLOCK <room number> <start date> <end date>
CLEAN <room number>
TODAY <date>
BOOK <reservation ID> <guest name> <room type> <check-in date> <check-out date>
BOOKROOM <reservation ID> <guest name> <room number> <check-in date> <check-out date>
WAITLIST <reservation ID> <guest name> <room type> <check-in date> <check-out date>
MODIFY <reservation ID> <check-in date> <check-out date>
CANCEL <reservation ID>
CHECKIN <reservation ID>
MOVE <reservation ID> <room number>
EXTEND <reservation ID> <check-out date>
CHARGE <reservation ID> <amount> <item name>
PAY <reservation ID> <amount>
CHECKOUT <reservation ID>
INVOICE <reservation ID>
AVAIL <room type> <start date> <end date>
OCCUPANCY <date>
STATS <start date> <end date>
ROOMS
LIST <status>
QUEUE
SCHEDULE <date>
GUEST <guest name>
REVENUE <start date> <end date>
The <rate> of ROOM and RATE is the base rate of the room; the <amount> of CHARGE and PAY is the amount of the extra charge and of the deposit, respectively.

[Processing]
== Dates and days of the week ==
- A date "YYYY-MM-DD" is "valid" only if, letting Y, M and D be the values of YYYY, MM and DD read as decimal numbers (the year, month and day), all of 2000<=Y<=2099, 1<=M<=12 and 1<=D<=(the number of days in month M of year Y) hold. The numbers of days in January through December are 31, 28, 31, 30, 31, 30, 31, 31, 30, 31, 30, 31 in order, except that February of a leap year has 29 days. A year Y is a leap year if Y is divisible by 4 and either "not divisible by 100" or "divisible by 400".
- Each valid date is assigned a "day number". The day number of 2000-01-01 is 0, and it increases by 1 for each day. Hereafter, dates are compared (<, <=, etc.) by their day numbers, and the difference of two dates (-) is the difference of their day numbers.
- When a date is output, it is written in the form "YYYY-MM-DD" (the year with 4 digits and the month and day with 2 digits each, with a leading 0 when they are a single digit).
- The day of the week is SAT, SUN, MON, TUE, WED, THU, FRI when the remainder of the day number divided by 7 is 0, 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, respectively (2000-01-01 is a Saturday). Wherever an output format says "<date and weekday>", write the date, one ASCII space, and the day of the week (e.g. 2026-03-01 SUN).
- "The night of date d" is the single night from the evening of date d to the next morning. A stay with check-in date a and check-out date b consists of the nights of every date d with a<=d<b, b-a nights in total (the night of the check-out date is not included).
- "The period a..b" is all dates from a to b inclusive (both ends included), in ascending order of date.

== State and initial state ==
- Today: initially 2026-01-01. It is advanced by TODAY.
- Rooms: initially there are none. They are registered by ROOM. Each room has a room number, a room type, a base rate and a cleaning state (clean or dirty). The cleaning state right after registration is clean. Rooms are never removed.
- Seasons: initially there are none. They are numbered 1, 2, 3, ... in the order they are registered by SEASON. Each season has a start date, an end date and a percentage.
- Blocks: a set of (room, date) pairs. That a pair (r, d) is in the set is expressed as "the night of d in room r is blocked". Initially it is empty.
- Reservations: initially there are none. Each reservation has a reservation ID, a guest name, a current room, a check-in date, a check-out date, a night list, a status, a list of extra charges, a deposit, an amount paid, a posting date, and a fee (a cancellation fee or a no-show fee).
  The night list is the list of (date, room of that night, rate of that night) for each night, in ascending order of date; it contains exactly one night for each date from the check-in date through the day before the check-out date.
  The status is one of booked (reserved), inhouse (staying), checkedout (settled), cancelled (cancelled), noshow (did not show up); it is booked when the reservation is created.
  The list of extra charges is the list of (item name, amount) in the order they were added; it is initially empty. The deposit, the amount paid and the fee are initially 0, and the posting date is determined at checkout, cancellation or no-show.
- Waitlist: a sequence whose entries are (reservation ID, guest name, room type, check-in date, check-out date); initially it is empty.
- Used reservation IDs: the set of reservation IDs that were created as reservations or added to the waitlist. Once a reservation ID has become used, it stays used even if the reservation is cancelled or the entry leaves the waitlist.
A command that results in an error does not change the state at all; it only outputs one error line.

== Terms and ordering ==
- "Ascending order of reservation ID" is lexicographic order by character code (ASCII) (when one is a prefix of the other, the shorter one comes first; for example, A7 < R10 < R7).
- The "first night's rate" of a reservation is the rate of the first night in its night list. The "lodging charge" of a reservation is the sum of the rates in its night list. The "room type" of a reservation is the room type of its current room.
- The "in-house guest" of room r is the reservation whose status is inhouse and whose current room is r (by the rules below, there is at most one).
- Lines marked "(indented)" in the output formats are output with two ASCII spaces at the beginning of the line.

== Amount notation ==
An amount (a non-negative integer number of cents c) is written as "the quotient of c divided by 100", ".", and "the remainder of c divided by 100 in 2 digits (with a leading 0 if it is a single digit)", concatenated (e.g. 0 is 0.00, 1250 is 12.50, 105000 is 1050.00). Hereafter, every amount written in the output uses this notation. All calculations of proportions and fractions are done with integers; floor(x/y) is the integer obtained by discarding the fractional part of the quotient of x divided by y (every division in this problem is between non-negative numbers).

== Night rates ==
- The rate of the night of date d in room r is floor((base rate of r)*p/100). Here p is the percentage of the last registered season among the seasons satisfying start date<=d<=end date, or 100 if there is no such season.
- "Pricing room r from a to b" means creating, for each date d with a<=d<b, in ascending order of date, (d, r, the rate of the night of d in room r at that time).
- The rate of a night is fixed at the time it is priced and does not change even if RATE or SEASON commands come later.

== Occupancy and availability ==
- When the night list of a reservation whose status is booked, inhouse or checkedout contains (d, r, ...), that reservation is said to "occupy the night of d in room r". Reservations that are cancelled or noshow occupy nothing.
- The night of d in room r is "free" if that night is not blocked and no reservation occupies it. For a reservation x, the night is "free except for x" if that night is not blocked and either no reservation occupies it or the only reservation occupying it is x.
- By the rules below, at most one reservation occupies any one night of any one room, and a blocked night is never occupied.
- For a room type t, "searching for a free room from a to b" means examining the rooms whose room type is t in ascending order of room number and choosing the first room in which the nights of all dates d with a<=d<b are free. If there is no such room, the result is "no free room". When "searching for a free room except for x", read "free" as "free except for x".

== Tiers and discounts ==
- A guest's "total paid" is the sum of the amounts paid of all reservations with that guest name. The "points" are floor(total paid/100) (one point for every 1.00 paid).
- The "tier" is GOLD if the points are 2000 or more, SILVER if they are 500 or more and less than 2000, and BRONZE if they are less than 500.
- The "discount percentage" of each tier is 10 for GOLD, 5 for SILVER, and 0 for BRONZE.

== Bill calculation ==
The bill of reservation x is calculated in the following order. The tier is determined by the points of x's guest at the time of the calculation.
- Lodging L: the sum of the rates in x's night list.
- Discount C: floor(L*discount percentage/100).
- Penalty P: the amount of the early-departure penalty if one is recorded for x, otherwise 0.
- Extras E: the sum of the amounts in x's list of extra charges.
- Subtotal S=L-C+P+E, tax T=floor(S/10), total A=S+T.

== Deposit notation ==
For a reservation x and some amount m (such as the total of a bill or a cancellation fee), the "deposit notation" is "deposit <deposit> due <m-deposit>" if m>=(deposit of x), and "deposit <deposit> refund <deposit-m>" if m<(deposit of x).

== Creating a reservation ==
"Creating a reservation" with reservation ID i, guest name g, room r, check-in date a and check-out date b means creating a reservation with reservation ID i, guest name g, current room r, check-in date a, check-out date b, status booked, and a night list that is "room r priced from a to b" (the list of extra charges, the deposit, etc. have their initial values), and adding i to the used reservation IDs. After creating the reservation, output the following "booking line".
"BOOK <i> <g> room <r> <room type of r> <a> -> <b> nights <b-a> total <lodging charge>"
Reservations are kept in the order they were created (this is used by GUEST).

== Checkout ==
"Checking out" reservation x on date e means doing the following in order.
1. If e<(check-out date of x), it is an early departure. Record the date and rate of the night of e in x's night list as the "early-departure penalty", remove all nights whose dates are e or later from the night list, and change x's check-out date to e (when the guest leaves on the check-in date, the night list becomes empty).
2. Calculate the bill and record its L, C, P, E, T, A in x. Set x's amount paid to A, its status to checkedout, and its posting date to e.
3. Set the cleaning state of x's current room to dirty.
4. Output "CHECKOUT <reservation ID> nights <number of nights in the night list> lodging <L> discount <C> penalty <P> extras <E> tax <T> total <A> <deposit notation for A>".

== Waitlist processing ==
Each time the processing of a command line finishes (including when it resulted in an error), after its output, examine the waitlist entries once each, in order from the front. For each entry:
- If check-in date<today, remove the entry from the waitlist and output (indented) "WAITLIST <reservation ID> expired".
- Otherwise, search for a free room of the entry's room type from its check-in date to its check-out date. If one is found, remove the entry from the waitlist, create a reservation with that room, and output the booking line (indented). If there is no free room, leave the entry as it is.
Each later entry is examined in the state after the earlier entries have been processed. The order of the entries that are not removed does not change.

== Check procedure ==
Each command performs the checks in the order written below, outputs the line of the first error that applies, and stops. If none applies, the command succeeds, changes the state and outputs its result. Frequently used checks are summarized here.
- Reservation check: if there is no reservation with the reservation ID, "ERROR unknown reservation <reservation ID>" (waitlist entries are not reservations).
- Status check: if the reservation's status is not the specified one, "ERROR invalid status <reservation ID> <status of that reservation>".
- Room check: if no room with the room number is registered, "ERROR unknown room <room number>".
- Type check: if the room type is none of SINGLE, DOUBLE, SUITE, "ERROR invalid type <room type>".
- Reservation ID check: if the reservation ID is used, "ERROR duplicate reservation <reservation ID>".
- Stay check (check-in date a, check-out date b): (1) if a is not valid, "ERROR invalid date <a>", (2) if b is not valid, "ERROR invalid date <b>", (3) if a<today, "ERROR past date <a>", (4) if b<=a, "ERROR invalid range <a> <b>", (5) if b-a>30, "ERROR too long <b-a>".
- Period check (start date a, end date b): (1) if a is not valid, "ERROR invalid date <a>", (2) if b is not valid, "ERROR invalid date <b>", (3) for BLOCK and UNBLOCK only, if a<today, "ERROR past date <a>", (4) if b<a, "ERROR invalid range <a> <b>", (5) if b-a+1>31, "ERROR too long <b-a+1>".
The dates, room numbers, room types, reservation IDs and guest names filled into the <...> of error lines are the tokens exactly as written in the input, or the values written in the notations above (either way they give the same string).

== Commands ==
- ROOM <r> <t> <m>: (1) if room r is already registered, "ERROR duplicate room <r>", (2) type check. On success, register a room with room number r, room type t, base rate m and cleaning state clean, and output "ROOM <r> <t> <m>".
- RATE <r> <m>: (1) room check. On success, change the base rate of r to m and output "RATE <r> <base rate before the change> -> <m>".
- SEASON <a> <b> <p>: (1) if a is not valid, "ERROR invalid date <a>", (2) if b is not valid, "ERROR invalid date <b>", (3) if b<a, "ERROR invalid range <a> <b>". On success, register the season and output "SEASON <number> <a> -> <b> <p>%".
- BLOCK <r> <a> <b>: (1) room check, (2) period check, (3) if, among the dates e in the period a..b, there is one for which some reservation occupies the night of e in r, let d be the earliest such date and output "ERROR room unavailable <r> <d>". On success, add (r, d) to the blocks for each date d in the period a..b (pairs that are already blocked stay as they are), and output "BLOCK <r> <a> -> <b> added <number of pairs newly added>".
- UNBLOCK <r> <a> <b>: (1) room check, (2) period check. On success, remove (r, d) from the blocks for each date d in the period a..b, and output "UNBLOCK <r> <a> -> <b> removed <number of pairs actually removed>".
- CLEAN <r>: (1) room check, (2) if the cleaning state of r is clean, "ERROR already clean <r>". On success, set r to clean and output "CLEAN <r>".
- TODAY <d>: (1) if d is not valid, "ERROR invalid date <d>", (2) if d<today, "ERROR past date <d>". On success, output "TODAY <date and weekday of d>" and then do the following in order.
  (a) Check out the reservations whose status is inhouse and whose check-out date<d, one at a time in ascending order of (check-out date, reservation ID), each on its check-out date (this is not an early departure). The CHECKOUT lines are output (indented).
  (b) Make the reservations whose status is booked and whose check-in date<d no-shows, one at a time in ascending order of (check-in date, reservation ID). Making a reservation a no-show means setting its fee and amount paid to the first night's rate, its status to noshow, and its posting date to its check-in date. For each, output (indented) "NOSHOW <reservation ID> <guest name> fee <fee> <deposit notation for the fee>".
  (c) Set today to d.
  If d is the same date as today, no reservations match (a) or (b).
- BOOK <i> <g> <t> <a> <b>: (1) reservation ID check, (2) type check, (3) stay check, (4) search for a free room of type t from a to b, and if there is no free room, "ERROR no availability <t> <a> <b>". On success, create a reservation with the room found.
- BOOKROOM <i> <g> <r> <a> <b>: (1) reservation ID check, (2) room check, (3) stay check, (4) if, among the dates e with a<=e<b, there is one for which the night of e in r is not free, let d be the earliest such date and output "ERROR room unavailable <r> <d>". On success, create a reservation with room r.
- WAITLIST <i> <g> <t> <a> <b>: (1) reservation ID check, (2) type check, (3) stay check. On success, search for a free room of type t from a to b, and if one is found, create a reservation with that room (outputting the booking line, as BOOK does). If there is no free room, append (i, g, t, a, b) to the end of the waitlist, add i to the used reservation IDs, and output "WAITLIST <i> queued position <number of waitlist entries after appending>".
- MODIFY <i> <a> <b>: (1) reservation check, (2) status check (booked), (3) stay check, (4) let r0 be the current room of reservation i. If, for all dates d with a<=d<b, the night of d in r0 is free except for i, the room stays r0 (kept). Otherwise, search for a free room of the room type of r0 from a to b except for i, and if one is found, change to that room (reassigned). If there is no free room, "ERROR no availability <room type of r0> <a> <b>". On success, change reservation i's current room to the determined room r, its check-in date to a and its check-out date to b, and replace its night list with "room r priced from a to b" (nights on the same dates as before are also re-priced at the rates at this time). Then output "MODIFY <i> room <r> kept <a> -> <b> nights <b-a> total <lodging charge>". If the room was changed, write reassigned instead of kept.
- CANCEL <i>: if the waitlist has an entry with reservation ID i, remove that entry and output "CANCEL <i> waitlist removed" (this is a success). Otherwise, (1) reservation check, (2) status check (booked). On success, let k=(check-in date-today) (by the rules, k>=0). The cancellation fee is 0 if k>=7, floor(first night's rate/2) if 1<=k<=6, and the first night's rate if k=0. Set the fee and the amount paid to the cancellation fee, the status to cancelled and the posting date to today, and output "CANCEL <i> days <k> fee <cancellation fee> <deposit notation for the cancellation fee>".
- CHECKIN <i>: (1) reservation check, (2) status check (booked), (3) if the check-in date is not today, "ERROR not arrival date <i> <check-in date>", (4) if the current room r has an in-house guest, "ERROR room occupied <r> <reservation ID of the in-house guest>", (5) if the cleaning state of r is dirty, "ERROR room dirty <r>". On success, set the status to inhouse and output "CHECKIN <i> <guest name> room <r> until <check-out date>".
- MOVE <i> <r>: (1) reservation check, (2) status check (inhouse), (3) room check, (4) if r is the same as the current room of reservation i, "ERROR same room <r>", (5) let the "remaining nights" be the nights in the night list whose dates are today or later. If there are no remaining nights, "ERROR no remaining nights <i>", (6) if, among the dates e of the remaining nights, there is one for which the night of e in r is not free except for i, let d be the earliest such date and output "ERROR room unavailable <r> <d>", (7) if r has an in-house guest, "ERROR room occupied <r> <reservation ID of the in-house guest>", (8) if the cleaning state of r is dirty, "ERROR room dirty <r>". On success, change the room of each remaining night to r (the rates are not changed) and output "MOVE <i> <current room before the move> -> <r> nights <number of remaining nights>". Then set the cleaning state of the current room before the move to dirty, and set the current room to r.
- EXTEND <i> <b>: let a be the check-in date and c the check-out date of reservation i. (1) reservation check, (2) status check (inhouse), (3) if b is not valid, "ERROR invalid date <b>", (4) if b<=c, "ERROR invalid range <c> <b>", (5) if b-a>30, "ERROR too long <b-a>", (6) if, among the dates e with c<=e<b, there is one for which the night of e in the current room r is not free except for i, let d be the earliest such date and output "ERROR room unavailable <r> <d>". On success, append the nights of room r priced from c to b to the end of the night list, set the check-out date to b, and output "EXTEND <i> until <b> nights <number of nights in the night list> total <lodging charge>".
- CHARGE <i> <m> <s>: (1) reservation check, (2) status check (inhouse), (3) if m is 0.00, "ERROR invalid amount". On success, append (s, m) to the end of the list of extra charges and output "CHARGE <i> <s> <m> extras <sum of the amounts in the list of extra charges>".
- PAY <i> <m>: (1) reservation check, (2) if the status is neither booked nor inhouse, "ERROR invalid status <i> <status of that reservation>", (3) if m is 0.00, "ERROR invalid amount". On success, add m to the deposit and output "PAY <i> <m> deposit <deposit after adding>".
- CHECKOUT <i>: (1) reservation check, (2) status check (inhouse). On success, check out reservation i on today (by the rules, today<=check-out date).
- INVOICE <i>: (1) reservation check. On success, output "INVOICE <i> <guest name> room <current room> <room type> <status>" as the first line, followed by the following depending on the status.
  When the status is cancelled: (indented) "cancellation fee <fee>", (indented) "total <fee>", (indented) "<deposit notation for the fee>".
  When the status is noshow: (indented) "noshow fee <fee>", (indented) "total <fee>", (indented) "<deposit notation for the fee>".
  Otherwise: first output each night of the night list in order as (indented) "<date and weekday of that night> room <room of that night> <rate of that night>". For the bill's L, C, P, E, T, A, use the values recorded at checkout if the status is checkedout, or the values calculated at this time (with a penalty of 0) if the status is booked or inhouse. Then output (indented) "lodging <L>", (indented) "discount <C>", (indented) "penalty <date and weekday of the penalty night> <P>" if an early-departure penalty is recorded, each item of the list of extra charges in order as (indented) "extra <item name> <amount>", and then (indented) "tax <T>", (indented) "total <A>", (indented) "<deposit notation for A>".
- AVAIL <t> <a> <b>: (1) type check, (2) period check. On success, output "AVAIL <t> <a> -> <b>", then for each date d in the period a..b output (indented) "<date and weekday of d> free <k>". k is the number of rooms whose room type is t and whose night of d is free; at the end of this line, append the room numbers of those rooms in ascending order, each preceded by one ASCII space (append nothing if k=0).
- OCCUPANCY <d>: (1) if d is not valid, "ERROR invalid date <d>". For a group of rooms G, the "occupancy notation" is "rooms <number of rooms in G> blocked <number b of rooms in G whose night of d is blocked> occupied <number k of rooms in G whose night of d is occupied> rate <rate>". The rate is floor(k*100/s) followed by "%" if s=(number of rooms in G-b) is 1 or more, and "n/a" if s=0. On success, output "OCCUPANCY <date and weekday of d> <occupancy notation for all registered rooms>", then, in the order SINGLE, DOUBLE, SUITE, for each room type with at least one registered room, output (indented) "<room type> <occupancy notation for the rooms of that room type>".
- STATS <a> <b>: (1) period check. For a date d, let s be the number of rooms whose night of d is not blocked, k the number of rooms whose night of d is occupied, and v the sum of "the rate of the night of d in the night list of a reservation occupying the night of d". The "metrics notation" for s, k, v is "sold <k>/<s> rate <rate> revenue <v> adr <ADR> revpar <RevPAR>", where the rate is floor(k*100/s) followed by "%" if s>=1 and "n/a" if s=0, ADR is floor(v/k) as an amount if k>=1 and "n/a" if k=0, and RevPAR is floor(v/s) as an amount if s>=1 and "n/a" if s=0. On success, output "STATS <a> -> <b>", then for each date d in the period a..b output (indented) "<date and weekday of d> <metrics notation for s, k, v of d>", and finally output (indented) "total <metrics notation for the sum of s, the sum of k and the sum of v over the whole period>".
- ROOMS: output "ROOMS <date and weekday of today> rooms <number of registered rooms>" as the first line, then for each room r in ascending order of room number output (indented) "<r> <room type> base <base rate> tonight <rate of the night of today in r at this time> <cleaning state> <situation>". The situation is "occupied <reservation ID of the in-house guest>" if r has an in-house guest; otherwise "blocked" if the night of today in r is blocked; otherwise "vacant". In addition, if there are reservations whose status is booked and whose current room is r, then for the one among them with the earliest check-in date (by the rules, it is unique), append one ASCII space and "next <reservation ID> <check-in date>" to the end of the line.
- LIST <s>: sort the reservations whose status is s in ascending order of (check-in date, reservation ID). Output "LIST <s> <count>", then for each reservation output (indented) "<reservation ID> <guest name> room <current room> <check-in date> -> <check-out date>".
- QUEUE: output "QUEUE <number of waitlist entries>", then for each entry in order from the front output (indented) "<position> <reservation ID> <guest name> <room type> <check-in date> -> <check-out date>". The position of the front entry is 1.
- SCHEDULE <d>: (1) if d is not valid, "ERROR invalid date <d>". "Arrivals" are the reservations whose check-in date is d and whose status is anything other than cancelled; "departures" are the reservations whose check-out date is d and whose status is booked, inhouse or checkedout. On success, output "SCHEDULE <date and weekday of d> arrivals <number of arrivals> departures <number of departures>", then output the arrivals in ascending order of reservation ID as (indented) "arrival <reservation ID> <guest name> room <current room> <status>", and then the departures in ascending order of reservation ID as (indented) "departure <reservation ID> <guest name> room <current room> <status>".
- GUEST <g>: (1) if there is no reservation with guest name g, "ERROR unknown guest <g>" (waitlist entries are not counted). On success, output "GUEST <g>", then output the reservations with guest name g in the order they were created as (indented) "<reservation ID> room <current room> <room type> <check-in date> -> <check-out date> <status> <amount part>". The amount part is "deposit <deposit>" if the status is booked or inhouse, and "paid <amount paid>" otherwise. Finally output (indented) "total paid <total paid> points <points> tier <tier>".
- REVENUE <a> <b>: (1) if a is not valid, "ERROR invalid date <a>", (2) if b is not valid, "ERROR invalid date <b>", (3) if b<a, "ERROR invalid range <a> <b>". The target reservations are those whose posting date is a or later and b or earlier. On success, output "REVENUE <a> -> <b>", then the following 7 lines in order, (indented): "lodging <sum of L of the target checkedout reservations>", "discount <sum of C of the same>", "penalty <sum of P of the same>", "extras <sum of E of the same>", "tax <sum of T of the same>", "cancellation <sum of the fees of the target cancelled reservations>", "noshow <sum of the fees of the target noshow reservations>". Next output (indented) "total <lodging-discount+penalty+extras+tax+cancellation+noshow>", and finally (indented) "checkouts <number of target checkedout reservations> cancellations <number of target cancelled reservations> noshows <number of target noshow reservations>".

On an error, output exactly one of the following lines.

"ERROR same room <room number>"
"ERROR no remaining nights <reservation ID>"

"ERROR invalid amount"

The waitlist processing is performed even after a command that resulted in an error.

[Output format]
For each command, output the lines defined in [Processing] in order. Lines marked (indented) start with two ASCII spaces; all other lines have no leading whitespace. Tokens within a line are separated by a single ASCII space, with no trailing whitespace. No blank lines are output. All output consists of ASCII characters, and each line ends with a newline character (LF).
The output of each command line may be followed by (indented) lines output by the waitlist processing.

[Constraints]
- 1<=N<=1000.
- The input correctly follows the formats in [Input format] (there are no unknown commands, wrong numbers of tokens, or tokens that do not match their formats). The only errors to detect are those listed in [Processing].
- At most 50 rooms are registered, and there are at most 300 used reservation IDs.
- The rates of ROOM and RATE are from 1.00 to 9999.99. The amounts of CHARGE and PAY are from 0.00 to 99999.99.
- When calculated according to the rules above, the absolute value of every integer, including intermediate values, stays below 2 to the 53rd power (no overflow occurs when calculating with 64-bit signed integers).

The night of 2026-03-02 is priced at 150% of the base rate because of season 1, so A1's second night is 120.00 and each of A2's nights is 135.00. A2 is cancelled 1 day before check-in, so the cancellation fee is half of the first night's rate, 67.50, and the waitlisted A3 automatically gets room 102, which the cancellation freed. A1 departed early on 2026-03-02, so the rate of the night of 2026-03-02 that was not stayed, 120.00, becomes the penalty, and the tax is 21.25, 10% (rounded down) of the subtotal 80.00+120.00+12.50=212.50.
