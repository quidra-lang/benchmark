For 5 games of bowling, the number of pins knocked down by each roll is given below in the order rolled. Every game is a valid game completed through the 10th frame.
Game 1: 8, 1, 10, 7, 3, 9, 0, 10, 10, 2, 6, 6, 4, 0, 9, 10, 7, 2
Game 2: 3, 4, 5, 2, 7, 1, 0, 6, 8, 1, 4, 4, 2, 3, 6, 2, 9, 0, 1, 5
Game 3: 5, 5, 0, 10, 7, 3, 9, 1, 6, 4, 8, 2, 3, 7, 4, 6, 1, 9, 7, 3, 5
Game 4: 10, 10, 10, 10, 10, 10, 10, 10, 10, 10, 10, 10
Game 5: 10, 9, 1, 10, 10, 7, 2, 0, 0, 10, 8, 1, 10, 6, 4, 10
Compute the score of each frame with the following standard rules, and for each game compute the cumulative score from frame 1 through each frame, for frames 1 to 10.
Frames 1-9: If the first roll of a frame knocks down 10 pins, it is a strike; the frame ends after that one roll, and its score is 10 plus the pins knocked down by the next 2 rolls. Otherwise the frame consists of 2 rolls. If the 2 rolls total 10 pins, it is a spare, and the score is 10 plus the pins knocked down by the next 1 roll. If the 2 rolls total fewer than 10 pins, the score is the total of the 2 rolls. If the first roll knocks down 0 pins and the second roll knocks down 10 pins, it is a spare, not a strike.
Frame 10: If the first roll is a strike, or if the first 2 rolls total 10 pins, a third roll is made; otherwise the frame ends after 2 rolls. The score is the total number of pins knocked down by all rolls of the 10th frame (2 or 3 rolls).
The "next 2 rolls" and "next 1 roll" added to the score of a strike or a spare are counted in the order rolled over the rolls of the following frames (including the rolls of the 10th frame).

Output format:
Print 5 lines in total, one line per game, from game 1 onward. Each line consists of "game", one space, the game number (1-5), ":", and one space, followed by the 10 cumulative scores at the end of frames 1 to 10, in this order, separated by one space each. Write every number as a decimal integer. Do not put trailing whitespace at the end of any line, and end every line, including the last one, with a newline.

Example output (the numbers are illustrative and differ from the actual answer):
game 1: 9 28 37 56 65 73 97 114 121 130
game 2: 9 29 48 57 65 81 90 110 124 133
