The test scores of 10 students (integers from 0 to 100 inclusive) are 78, 90, 100, 59, 80, 85, 89, 70, 45 and 93. Write a program that grades each score by the following criteria: 90 or more is A, at least 80 and less than 90 is B, at least 70 and less than 80 is C, at least 60 and less than 70 is D, and less than 60 is F. Also count the number of students who received each of A, B, C, D and F.

Output format:
First, in the given order, output one line per score consisting of the score, a colon, a single space and the grade letter (an uppercase ASCII letter), in this order.
Then output 5 lines with the count for each grade in the form "A: n" (the grade letter, a colon, a single space and the count n), in the order A, B, C, D, F. Grades with a count of 0 are also output and not omitted.
Write every number as a decimal integer with no extra leading zeros. Output only these 15 lines, with no trailing whitespace on any line, and end every line with a newline. The example output below is for the case of 3 students with scores 72, 95 and 60.

Example output (the numbers are illustrative and differ from the actual answer):
72: C
95: A
60: D
A: 1
B: 0
C: 1
D: 1
F: 0
