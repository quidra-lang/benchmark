Write a program that divides the lowercase English words listen, stone, night, silent, notes, apple, thing, enlist, earth, onset, heart, tones (12 words, in this order) into groups of words that are anagrams of each other (words that use exactly the same letters with exactly the same counts).
All words are distinct. A word that is not an anagram of any other word forms a group consisting of that word alone.

Output format:
Print one group per line: the words belonging to the group, in the given order, separated by a single space.
Order the groups so that a group comes earlier when its first word (the word of the group that appears earliest in the given order) appears earlier in the given order. A group with only one word is also printed as one line.
Print exactly as many lines as there are groups, with no trailing whitespace on any line, and end every line with a newline.
The example output below is for grouping a different set of words care, lemon, race, acre, bird, melon (6 words, in this order).

Example output (the numbers are illustrative and differ from the actual answer):
care race acre
lemon melon
bird
