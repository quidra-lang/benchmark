Write a program that counts the occurrences of the words in the following English text (a single-line string), and finds the top 5 words by number of occurrences and the number of distinct words.
The cat sat on the mat. The dog sat on the rug, and the cat watched the dog. A bird sang on the fence, and the Dog barked at the bird. The cat slept in the sun, the bird flew away, and the dog sat by the door.
A word is a maximal run of consecutive English letters (A-Z, a-z). Spaces, "," and "." are not part of words. Case is ignored: convert everything to lowercase before counting (for example, The and the are the same word).
Sort the words in descending order of number of occurrences, and words with the same number of occurrences in alphabetical (ascending) order. The first 5 words in this order are the top 5 words (even if the 5th and 6th words have the same number of occurrences, take the first 5 words in this order).

Output format:
On lines 1 to 5, print the top 5 words in the order above, one word per line, in the format "w: c", where w is the lowercased word and c is its number of occurrences.
On line 6, print the number of distinct words N in the format "distinct words: N".
Put exactly one space after ":" and after "distinct". Write every number as a decimal integer without a sign or extra leading zeros.
Print only these 6 lines, with no trailing whitespace on any line, and end every line with a newline.

Example output (the numbers are illustrative and differ from the actual answer):
the: 9
a: 5
is: 3
it: 3
of: 3
distinct words: 31
