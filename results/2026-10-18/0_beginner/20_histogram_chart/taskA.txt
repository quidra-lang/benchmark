The counts for the day labels "Mon", "Tue", "Wed", "Thu", "Fri", "Sat" and "Sun" are 7, 12, 4, 15, 9, 18 and 11, respectively. Write a program that displays the count of each day as a horizontal bar chart made of "*" characters.

Output format:
Output 7 lines in total, one line per day, in the order of the days above. Each line consists of, in this order: the day label, one space, "|", one space, as many "*" characters as the count, one space, and the count (a decimal integer). Do not put trailing whitespace at the end of any line, and end every line, including the last one, with a newline.

Example output (the numbers are illustrative and differ from the actual answer):
Mon | *** 3
Tue | ****** 6
Wed | * 1
Thu | ********** 10
Fri | ** 2
Sat | ***** 5
Sun | ******** 8
