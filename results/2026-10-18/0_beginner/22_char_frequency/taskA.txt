Write a program that counts the occurrences of the letters in the English sentence "Peter Piper picked a peck of pickled peppers!", without distinguishing between uppercase and lowercase. The double quotes only mark the boundaries of the sentence and are not part of it. Characters other than letters (A-Z, a-z), such as spaces and symbols, are not counted.

Output format:
Output one line for each letter that occurs, in alphabetical order (from a to z). Each line consists of, in this order: the letter in lowercase, ":", one space, and the number of occurrences (a decimal integer). Do not output a line for a letter that never occurs. Do not put trailing whitespace at the end of any line, and end every line, including the last one, with a newline.

Example output (the numbers are illustrative and differ from the actual answer):
a: 2
b: 1
e: 4
h: 1
n: 3
