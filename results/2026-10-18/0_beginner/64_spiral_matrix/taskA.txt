Write the integers from 1 to 30 into a matrix with 5 rows and 6 columns in a clockwise spiral. Write 1 in the top-left corner cell, and from there, moving to the right, write 2, 3, ... one per cell in order. When the next cell to move to would be outside the matrix or a cell that has already been written, first turn the moving direction 90 degrees clockwise, then move (the direction changes in the order right, down, left, up, right, ...). Continue until 30 has been written.

Output format:
Print the completed matrix row by row from the top row, one output line per row, 5 lines in total. Each line consists of the 6 values of that row, from the left column, each right-aligned in a field 3 characters wide (padded on the left with spaces), concatenated with no separator. Therefore each line is 18 characters long. Write the numbers as decimal integers, and end every line, including the last one, with a newline. The example output below is for 3 rows and 4 columns.

Example output (the numbers are illustrative and differ from the actual answer):
  1  2  3  4
 10 11 12  5
  9  8  7  6
