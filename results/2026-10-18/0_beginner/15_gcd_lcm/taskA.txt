For the 5 pairs of positive integers (a, b) = (48, 180), (1071, 462), (7, 91), (13, 97) and (36288, 98280), write a program that computes, in this order, the greatest common divisor and the least common multiple of each pair. Compute the greatest common divisor with a function you define yourself that uses the Euclidean algorithm (do not use any function or operator for the greatest common divisor or least common multiple that is built into the language or provided by the standard library). Compute the least common multiple by dividing a by the greatest common divisor and then multiplying by b.

Output format:
For each pair, in the given order, output one line in the form "gcd(a, b) = G, lcm(a, b) = L". a and b are the two numbers of that pair written in the given order, G is the greatest common divisor and L is the least common multiple, all written as decimal integers with no extra leading zeros. All other parts are the ASCII characters exactly as written; put a single space after each "," and a single space before and after each "=", and put no whitespace anywhere else. Output only these 5 lines, with no trailing whitespace on any line, and end every line with a newline.

Example output (the numbers are illustrative and differ from the actual answer):
gcd(12, 30) = 6, lcm(12, 30) = 60
gcd(9, 28) = 1, lcm(9, 28) = 252
