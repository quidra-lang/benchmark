Express the following 8 integers (each between 0 and 999999 inclusive) in English words, in this order.
0, 13, 40, 100, 568, 71000, 300019, 999999
The words to use are as follows, all written in lowercase English letters.
0 to 9: zero, one, two, three, four, five, six, seven, eight, nine
10 to 19: ten, eleven, twelve, thirteen, fourteen, fifteen, sixteen, seventeen, eighteen, nineteen
20, 30, 40, 50, 60, 70, 80, 90: twenty, thirty, forty, fifty, sixty, seventy, eighty, ninety
Hundred and thousand: hundred, thousand
0 is "zero". An integer x from 1 to 999 inclusive is expressed by taking whichever of the following 2 parts are non-empty, in order, separated by one ASCII space.
First part: if the quotient h of x divided by 100 is 1 or more, the word for h and "hundred", separated by one ASCII space (e.g. five hundred). If h is 0, it is empty.
Second part: let y be the remainder of x divided by 100. If y is 20 or more, it is the word for the tens digit of y (one of twenty through ninety), and if the ones digit of y is 1 or more, an ASCII hyphen "-" and the word for the ones digit are attached after it (e.g. sixty, forty-two). If y is from 1 to 19 inclusive, it is the word for y, and if y is 0, it is empty.
An integer n from 1 to 999999 inclusive is expressed by taking whichever of the following 2 parts are non-empty, in order, separated by one ASCII space. The first part, when the quotient t of n divided by 1000 is 1 or more, is t expressed by the method above and "thousand", separated by one ASCII space (empty if t is 0). The second part, when the remainder of n divided by 1000 is 1 or more, is that remainder expressed by the method above (empty if the remainder is 0). The word "and" is not used.

Output format:
Print one line per integer, in the given order, in the form "n: WORDS" (8 lines in total). n is the integer written as a decimal integer with no extra leading zeros (and with no digit-group separators), and WORDS is its English expression by the method above. Put one ASCII space after ":", and separate words with one ASCII space (no spaces inside a hyphen-joined part). Print only these 8 lines, with no trailing whitespace on any line, and end every line with a newline. The example output below is for the different integers 7, 21, 205, 4300, 120015.

Example output (the numbers are illustrative and differ from the actual answer):
7: seven
21: twenty-one
205: two hundred five
4300: four thousand three hundred
120015: one hundred twenty thousand fifteen
