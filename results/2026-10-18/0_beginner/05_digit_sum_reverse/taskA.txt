For the array (6 elements) of positive integers whose values are 7, 98000, 4096, 50301, 2718281 and 123456789, find for each integer its number of decimal digits, the sum of its digits, and the integer formed by arranging its digits in reverse order. Zeros that end up at the front after reversing are removed (example: 1200 reversed gives 21).

Output format:
Print one line per integer, in array order, 6 lines in total.
Each line consists of, in this order: the original integer immediately followed by ":", one ASCII space, "digits=" immediately followed by the number of digits, one ASCII space, "sum=" immediately followed by the digit sum, one ASCII space, "reversed=" immediately followed by the reversed integer.
Write every number as a decimal integer with no extra leading zeros. Print only these 6 lines, with no trailing whitespace on any line, and end every line with a newline.

Example output (the numbers are illustrative and differ from the actual answer):
1200: digits=4 sum=3 reversed=21
305: digits=3 sum=8 reversed=503
