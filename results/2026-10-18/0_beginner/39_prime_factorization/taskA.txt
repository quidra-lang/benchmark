In trial division, increase the divisor d by 1 starting from 2, and while the square of d is less than or equal to the remaining number, keep dividing the remaining number by d as long as it is divisible by d. Finally, if the remaining number is greater than 1, it is also a prime factor.

Output format:

When n is prime, the line has the form "n = n". Write every number as a decimal integer without extra leading zeros. Output only these 8 lines, with no trailing whitespace on any line, and end every line with a newline.
