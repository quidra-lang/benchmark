Write a program that finds a minimum spanning tree with Kruskal's algorithm for a connected, undirected, edge-weighted graph with 8 vertices A, B, C, D, E, F, G, H and 13 edges. Writing each edge as (endpoint, endpoint, weight), the 13 edges are (A, B, 7), (C, A, 4), (B, C, 9), (B, D, 14), (D, C, 6), (C, E, 3), (E, D, 5), (F, D, 11), (E, G, 16), (G, F, 8), (H, F, 12), (G, H, 2), (H, E, 10). All edge weights are distinct positive integers.
First, sort the edges in increasing order of weight. Then, while managing the connected components of the vertices with Union-Find (a disjoint-set data structure), examine the edges one at a time in increasing order of weight. If the two endpoints of the examined edge belong to different connected components, accept the edge and merge the two connected components into one. If both endpoints already belong to the same connected component, do not accept the edge; discard it. Stop examining as soon as the 7th edge (the (number of vertices - 1)-th edge) has been accepted; the remaining edges are not examined.

Output format:
Output the accepted edges, one per line, in the order they were accepted, in the format "U-V w". U and V are the names of the two endpoints of the edge, where U is the one that comes first in alphabetical order (for example, an edge (F, B, 13) is written as "B-F 13"). w is the weight of the edge.
Next, output the sum W of the weights of the 7 accepted edges in the format "total weight: W".
Finally, output the number k of edges that were examined and discarded before examining stopped, in the format "skipped: k".
Put no space before or after "-", and put exactly one ASCII space after V, after "total", and after ":". Write every number as a decimal integer without a sign or extra leading zeros.
Output only these 9 lines; no line has trailing spaces, and every line ends with a newline. The example output below is for a different graph with 4 vertices.

Example output (the numbers are illustrative and differ from the actual answer):
B-C 1
A-B 2
C-D 4
total weight: 7
skipped: 1
