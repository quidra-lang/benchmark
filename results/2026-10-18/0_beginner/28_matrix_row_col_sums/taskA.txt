Write a program that finds, for the following integer matrix with 4 rows and 5 columns, the sum of the elements of each row (row sum), the sum of the elements of each column (column sum), and the sum of all the elements (grand total).
Row 1: 125, 340, 78, 912, 456
Row 2: 603, 27, 819, 150, 384
Row 3: 0, 765, 231, 498, 62
Row 4: 947, 113, 586, 39, 720

Output format:
Output 5 lines in total. Each line contains 6 integers, each right-aligned in a field 6 characters wide (padded with spaces on the left), placed one after another with no separator. Therefore each line is 36 characters long.
Lines 1-4: for each row of the matrix, the 5 elements of that row from left to right, followed by the row sum of that row
Line 5: the column sums of columns 1 through 5 in order, followed by the grand total
End every line, including the last one, with a newline.

Example output (the numbers are illustrative and differ from the actual answer):
     5   210    33   480    92   820
   671     8   145   300    57  1181
    44   903   260    19   388  1614
   130    75   612     8   999  1824
   850  1196  1050   807  1536  5439
