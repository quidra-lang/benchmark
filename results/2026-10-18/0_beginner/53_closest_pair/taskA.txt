Given 10 points P0 to P9 in the plane with the (integer) coordinates P0 (12, -7), P1 (-33, 25), P2 (41, 18), P3 (-8, -44), P4 (27, 39), P5 (-46, -12), P6 (5, 14), P7 (36, -29), P8 (-19, 47), P9 (-2, 3), write a program that finds the closest pair of points and the farthest pair of points. For every pair (Pi, Pj) with i < j (45 pairs), let the coordinates of Pi be (xi, yi) and those of Pj be (xj, yj), compute the squared distance (xi - xj) * (xi - xj) + (yi - yj) * (yi - yj) as an integer, and compare pairs by this value. In this data, the pair with the smallest squared distance and the pair with the largest squared distance are each unique (if several pairs had the same value, the pair with the smaller i would be chosen, and if i were also equal, the pair with the smaller j).

Output format:
Print the closest pair on the first line in the format "closest: Pi Pj d", and the farthest pair on the second line in the format "farthest: Pi Pj d". Pi and Pj are the names of the two points of the pair (an ASCII uppercase P immediately followed by the number without extra leading zeros), with the point with the smaller number written first. d is the distance between the two points (the square root of the squared distance) rounded half up at the 5th decimal place and written to 4 decimal places; always write exactly 4 digits after the decimal point. All other parts are ASCII characters exactly as written, with one ASCII space after ":" and between items. Print only these 2 lines, with no trailing whitespace at the end of any line, and end every line with a newline.

Example output (the numbers are illustrative and differ from the actual answer):
closest: P2 P4 12.3693
farthest: P0 P5 87.0919
