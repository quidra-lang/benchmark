Write a program that displays the 7 integers 0, 7, 999, 1000, -456789, 3141592 and 9876543210 with thousands separators. With thousands separators, the digits are split into groups of 3 starting from the right end (the ones place), and an ASCII comma "," is inserted between the groups. A negative number is written as its absolute value with thousands separators, preceded by an ASCII minus sign "-".

Output format:
For each value, in the given order, output one line containing only the number with thousands separators. Put no comma at the beginning of the digit sequence (including right after the minus sign) or at its end, and add no extra zeros in front of the digits and no "+" sign for positive numbers. Numbers with 3 or fewer digits contain no comma. Output only these 7 lines, with no trailing whitespace on any line, and end every line with a newline.

Example output (the numbers are illustrative and differ from the actual answer):
-12,345
500
3,000,000
