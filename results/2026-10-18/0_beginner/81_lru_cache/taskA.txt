Simulate the behavior of an LRU (Least Recently Used) cache with capacity 3 when the 15 accesses with keys B, A, C, B, D, A, B, E, A, D, D, C, A, E, B are made in this order.
Every key is a single uppercase ASCII letter. The cache is initially empty and is managed as a list of the keys it contains, ordered by how recently they were used.
Process each access as follows.
- When the accessed key is in the cache (hit): remove that key from the list and move it to the front of the list (the most recently used position).
- When the accessed key is not in the cache (miss): if the cache already holds 3 keys, first remove the key at the end of the list (the least recently used position) (eviction), and then add the accessed key to the front of the list. When it holds fewer than 3 keys, add the accessed key to the front of the list without evicting anything.

Output format:
For each access, in the given order, print one line in one of the following formats.
On a hit: "K: hit [LIST]"
On a miss without eviction: "K: miss [LIST]"
On a miss with eviction: "K: miss, evict E [LIST]"
K is the accessed key and E is the evicted key. LIST is the contents of the cache after processing that access: the keys in order from the most recently used to the least recently used, separated by single ASCII spaces.
Finally, print one line in the format "hits: H misses: M", where H is the number of hits and M is the number of misses.
"hit", "miss", "evict", "hits" and "misses" are lowercase ASCII letters, and "[" and "]" are ASCII square brackets, with no whitespace right after "[" or right before "]". Put one ASCII space each after ":" and ",", between "hit", "miss" or E and "[", between "evict" and E, and between H and "misses". H and M are decimal integers without extra leading zeros. Print only these 16 lines, with no trailing whitespace on any line, and end every line with a newline. The example output below is for a different sequence of 5 accesses with keys P, Q, P, R, S.

Example output (the numbers are illustrative and differ from the actual answer):
P: miss [P]
Q: miss [Q P]
P: hit [P Q]
R: miss [R P Q]
S: miss, evict Q [S R P]
hits: 1 misses: 4
