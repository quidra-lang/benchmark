Write a program that finds all prime numbers less than or equal to 200 using the sieve of Eratosthenes. Prepare an array of boolean values that indicates, for each integer from 0 to 200, whether it is prime, and find the primes by going through the numbers in increasing order starting from 2 and crossing out, as not prime, the multiples (other than the number itself) of each number that still remains marked as prime.

Output format:
Line 1 consists of, in this order: "count:", one space, and the number of primes less than or equal to 200 (a decimal integer).
From line 2 on, output the primes in increasing order, 10 per line, separated by one space. If the number of primes is not a multiple of 10, the last line contains only the remaining primes.
Do not put trailing whitespace at the end of any line, and end every line, including the last one, with a newline. The example output below shows the primes greater than 1000 and less than or equal to 1100 (16 primes) output in the same format.

Example output (the numbers are illustrative and differ from the actual answer):
count: 16
1009 1013 1019 1021 1031 1033 1039 1049 1051 1061
1063 1069 1087 1091 1093 1097
