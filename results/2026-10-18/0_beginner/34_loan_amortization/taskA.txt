A loan with a principal of 1000000 yen is repaid with a monthly payment of 50000 yen. The monthly interest rate is 1%, and each month's interest is the quotient of the balance at the start of that month divided by 100 (fractional part truncated). Starting with a balance of 1000000 yen, perform the following computation every month, in order from month 1. First, let the interest be "the quotient of the balance divided by 100 (truncated)". Next, let the payment be the smaller of "50000" and "balance + interest". Next, let the principal portion be "payment - interest". Finally, let the new balance be the balance minus the principal portion. Repeat this computation until the month in which the new balance becomes 0. All computations are done in integers.

Output format:
Output one line per month, in order from month 1, in the format "month m: interest=I principal=P balance=B". m is the month number (month 1 is 1), I is that month's interest, P is that month's principal portion, and B is the balance after that month's payment. After the line for the month in which the balance becomes 0, output the line "total interest: T" and then the line "total paid: S", in this order. T is the sum of the interest over the whole period, and S is the sum of the payments over the whole period. All other parts are ASCII lowercase exactly as written; put exactly one ASCII space between "month" and m, after ":", before "principal" and "balance", and between "total" and the next word, and put no spaces before or after "=". Write every number as a decimal integer without digit separators, units or extra leading zeros. Output only these lines, with no trailing whitespace on any line, and end every line with a newline. The example output below is for a principal of 100000 yen and a monthly payment of 30000 yen.

Example output (the numbers are illustrative and differ from the actual answer):
month 1: interest=1000 principal=29000 balance=71000
month 2: interest=710 principal=29290 balance=41710
month 3: interest=417 principal=29583 balance=12127
month 4: interest=121 principal=12127 balance=0
total interest: 2248
total paid: 102248
