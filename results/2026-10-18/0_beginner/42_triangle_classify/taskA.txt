Write a program that, for the 8 sets of three side lengths (positive integers) (3, 8, 5), (6, 6, 6), (5, 8, 5), (13, 5, 12), (2, 9, 4), (7, 4, 7), (8, 6, 7), (4, 9, 6), determines whether they form a triangle and, if they do, determines its type by sides and by angles.
Let a, b, c (a <= b <= c) be the three values of a set sorted in ascending order.
- If a + b <= c, they do not form a triangle (they do not form a triangle when a + b = c either).
- Otherwise they form a triangle. The type by sides is equilateral (equilateral triangle) if all three sides are equal, isosceles (isosceles triangle) if exactly two sides are equal, and scalene (scalene triangle) if no two sides are equal.
- The type by angles is determined by comparing the integers c^2 and a^2 + b^2: acute (acute triangle) if c^2 < a^2 + b^2, right (right triangle) if c^2 = a^2 + b^2, and obtuse (obtuse triangle) if c^2 > a^2 + b^2.

Output format:
Print one line per set, in the given order.
If they do not form a triangle, use the format "x y z: not a triangle"; if they form a triangle, use the format "x y z: SIDE ANGLE".
x y z are the given three side lengths in their original given order (before sorting), written as decimal integers separated by a single space. SIDE is one of equilateral, isosceles, scalene, and ANGLE is one of acute, right, obtuse, all written in lowercase ASCII letters.
Put exactly one space after ":" and between SIDE and ANGLE.
Print only these 8 lines, with no trailing whitespace on any line, and end every line with a newline.

Example output (the numbers are illustrative and differ from the actual answer):
10 4 5: not a triangle
5 6 5: isosceles acute
4 2 3: scalene obtuse
