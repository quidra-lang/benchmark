Write a program that determines the category of each of the following 8 poker hands (each line is a string representing one hand) and lists the hands from strongest to weakest.
9C 9D 5H QH 2C
4D AH 3C 5S 2H
KS 8S 2S JS 6S
7H 7D KC 7S KD
3H 6C 4S 2D 5C
JD TC 4H 8C AS
QC 6D QS 3S 6H
9H KH 9S 8H 3D
Each hand is 5 cards separated by ASCII spaces, and each card is written as 2 characters, a "rank" and a "suit". There are 13 ranks, from weakest to strongest 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9, T (10), J, Q, K, A, and 4 suits, S, H, D, C. All 40 cards in the 8 hands are distinct.
There are the following 9 categories, from strongest to weakest; the category of each hand is the strongest one that applies.
straight flush: it is a straight and also a flush.
four of a kind: there are 4 cards of the same rank.
full house: there are 3 cards of the same rank, and the remaining 2 cards also have the same rank as each other.
flush: all 5 cards have the same suit.
straight: the ranks of the 5 cards are consecutive. A can be used both after K and before 2, so T-J-Q-K-A and A-2-3-4-5 are both straights, but a sequence that wraps around A, such as Q-K-A-2-3, is not a straight.
three of a kind: there are 3 cards of the same rank, and the remaining 2 cards have ranks different from each other.
two pair: there are 2 pairs of 2 cards of the same rank.
one pair: there is exactly 1 pair of 2 cards of the same rank.
high card: none of the above applies.
Between hands of different categories, the hand with the stronger category is placed higher. Hands of the same category are compared as follows. Build a sequence listing each rank in the hand once, with ranks that have more cards of that rank first, and among ranks with equal card counts, stronger ranks first (for example, the two pair J J 4 4 9 gives the sequence J, 4, 9). Compare the sequences of the two hands from the beginning; the hand with the stronger rank at the first position where they differ is placed higher. However, in the straight A-2-3-4-5 (and the straight flush A-2-3-4-5), A is treated as a rank weaker than 2, so its sequence is 5, 4, 3, 2, A. In this data, no two hands have completely equal sequences.

Output format:
Output one line per hand from strongest to weakest, in the format "k. HAND: category". k is the position starting from 1, HAND is the hand string written exactly as given (without changing the order of the cards), and category is the English name of the category from the list above (one of straight flush, four of a kind, full house, flush, straight, three of a kind, two pair, one pair, high card, all in ASCII lowercase).
Put exactly one ASCII space after "k." and after ":", and separate the words of the category's English name by exactly one ASCII space.
Output only these 8 lines; no line has trailing spaces, and every line ends with a newline. The example output below is for 3 different hands.

Example output (the numbers are illustrative and differ from the actual answer):
1. 8D 8S 8C 4H JD: three of a kind
2. KH 2D KC 7S 2S: two pair
3. 3C QD 9H 6S TD: high card
