The results of a round-robin league of 6 teams, Lions, Tigers, Eagles, Sharks, Wolves, Bears (one match for each pair of teams, 15 matches in total), are given as the following 15 strings (each line represents one match).
Lions 0-3 Bears
Tigers 2-2 Wolves
Eagles 4-0 Sharks
Bears 0-1 Sharks
Wolves 2-1 Eagles
Lions 0-1 Tigers
Tigers 3-3 Bears
Eagles 2-2 Lions
Sharks 2-2 Wolves
Bears 3-1 Wolves
Lions 2-0 Sharks
Tigers 2-1 Eagles
Eagles 1-0 Bears
Sharks 2-0 Tigers
Wolves 2-1 Lions
Each string has the form "Home H-A Away" and consists of 3 parts separated by ASCII spaces. Home is the name of the home team, Away is the name of the away team, and H and A are the goals scored by the home team and the away team respectively (integers >= 0); H and A are separated by an ASCII "-". All team names are distinct, and each is a single word whose first letter is an uppercase English letter and whose remaining letters are lowercase English letters.
Write a program that, from these results, tallies for each team the number of matches played, wins, draws, losses, goals for, goals against, goal difference (goals for - goals against), and points, and produces a standings table. A win is worth 3 points, a draw 1 point, and a loss 0 points.
Order the teams by points in descending order; teams with equal points are ordered by goal difference in descending order, then, if still equal, by goals for in descending order, then, if still equal, by team name in alphabetical (ascending) order.

Output format:
Output one team per line in the order above, in the format "pos. Team P=played W=w D=d L=l GF=gf GA=ga GD=gd Pts=pts". pos is the position in this ordering (a sequential number starting from 1; teams with identical records are not given the same position), Team is the team name, played is the number of matches played, w is the number of wins, d is the number of draws, l is the number of losses, gf is the goals for, ga is the goals against, gd is the goal difference, and pts is the points.
Write gd with a leading "+" when it is positive and a leading "-" when it is negative, and as "0" with no sign when it is 0. Write all other numbers as decimal integers without a sign or extra leading zeros.
Put exactly one ASCII space after "pos." and between items, and put no space before or after "=".
Output only these 6 lines; no line has trailing spaces, and every line ends with a newline.

Example output (the numbers are illustrative and differ from the actual answer):
1. Falcons P=5 W=4 D=0 L=1 GF=11 GA=4 GD=+7 Pts=12
2. Rovers P=5 W=3 D=1 L=1 GF=8 GA=6 GD=+2 Pts=10
3. Hawks P=5 W=2 D=1 L=2 GF=7 GA=7 GD=0 Pts=7
4. Bulls P=5 W=1 D=2 L=2 GF=6 GA=8 GD=-2 Pts=5
5. Pumas P=5 W=1 D=1 L=3 GF=4 GA=7 GD=-3 Pts=4
6. Owls P=5 W=1 D=1 L=3 GF=5 GA=9 GD=-4 Pts=4
