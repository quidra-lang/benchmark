For the 6 non-negative integers 0, 10, 255, 1000, 43981 and 65535, write a program that finds their representations in binary, octal and hexadecimal.

Output format:
For each value, in the given order, output one line in the form "n: bin=B oct=O hex=H". n is the original value in decimal, and B, O and H are its binary, octal and hexadecimal representations respectively. No representation has a prefix such as "0b" or "0x" or any extra leading zeros (when the value is 0, write "0"), and hexadecimal digits 10 to 15 are written as the uppercase ASCII letters A to F. All other parts are the ASCII characters exactly as written; put a single space after ":", before "oct" and before "hex", and put no whitespace before or after "=". Output only these 6 lines, with no trailing whitespace on any line, and end every line with a newline.

Example output (the numbers are illustrative and differ from the actual answer):
18: bin=10010 oct=22 hex=12
3054: bin=101111101110 oct=5756 hex=BEE
