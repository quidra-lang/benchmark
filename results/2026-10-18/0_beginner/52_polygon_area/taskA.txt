Write a program that computes the area and the perimeter of the following 3 simple polygons (polygons whose edges do not intersect each other). The vertices of each polygon are given as integer coordinates (x, y); they are connected by edges in the given order, and the last vertex is connected to the first vertex.
Polygon 1: the 4 vertices (0, 0), (5, 0), (6, 4), (1, 3)
Polygon 2: the 5 vertices (2, 1), (0, 4), (3, 7), (7, 6), (8, 2)
Polygon 3: the 7 vertices (0, 0), (8, 0), (8, 6), (5, 6), (4, 3), (3, 6), (0, 6)
Let the vertices in the given order be v1, v2, ..., vn, and regard v1 as the vertex following vn. Compute the area with the shoelace formula (Gauss's area formula): for i = 1, 2, ..., n, take the product of the x coordinate of vi and the y coordinate of the next vertex, subtract the product of the x coordinate of the next vertex and the y coordinate of vi, and add up these values as integers; the absolute value of the sum divided by 2 is the area (when the vertices are in clockwise order the sum is negative, but the area is a positive value). The perimeter is the sum of the lengths of the edges (the Euclidean distance between two vertices, that is, the square root of the sum of the square of the difference of the x coordinates and the square of the difference of the y coordinates), added up as real numbers (double-precision floating point) in the order of the edges v1-v2, v2-v3, ..., vn-v1.

Output format:
Print one line per polygon, in the given order, in the format "polygon k: area=A perimeter=P". k is the number of the polygon (an integer starting at 1), A is the area written to 1 decimal place (the area is always an integer or an integer plus 0.5), and P is the perimeter rounded half up at the 4th decimal place and written to 3 decimal places; always write exactly 1 digit after the decimal point for A and exactly 3 digits for P. All other parts are ASCII characters exactly as written, with one ASCII space after "polygon", after ":" and before "perimeter", and no whitespace before or after "=". Print only these 3 lines, with no trailing whitespace at the end of any line, and end every line with a newline.

Example output (the numbers are illustrative and differ from the actual answer):
polygon 1: area=12.0 perimeter=14.000
polygon 2: area=7.5 perimeter=13.831
