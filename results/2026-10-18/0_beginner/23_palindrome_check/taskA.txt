Write a program that determines whether each of the following 6 phrases is a palindrome. The double quotes only mark the boundaries of the phrases and are not part of them.
"Never odd or even", "Race a car", "Was it a car or a cat I saw?", "Hello, World!", "Step on no pets.", "No lemon, no melon"
A phrase is a palindrome when the string obtained by removing every character other than letters (A-Z, a-z) from the phrase and converting all the remaining letters to lowercase reads the same forwards and backwards.

Output format:
Output 6 lines in total, one line per phrase, in the order of the phrases above. Each line consists of, in this order: the phrase (exactly as given), one space, "->", one space, and "yes" if the phrase is a palindrome or "no" otherwise. Do not put trailing whitespace at the end of any line, and end every line, including the last one, with a newline. The example output below is for the case of the 2 phrases "Top spot!" and "Good morning".

Example output (the numbers are illustrative and differ from the actual answer):
Top spot! -> yes
Good morning -> no
