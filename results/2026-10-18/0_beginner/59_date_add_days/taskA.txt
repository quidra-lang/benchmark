Write a program that, for the 5 pairs of a date and a number of days to add, (1999-12-25, 10), (2000-02-27, 3), (2021-02-20, 14), (2023-11-15, 120), (2026-10-17, 1500), computes in the given order the date obtained by adding that number of days to the date. Dates are written in the form "year-month-day", where the year is the year AD. Do not use date libraries or date functions; compute using the number of days in each month (January has 31 days, February has 28 days in a common year and 29 days in a leap year, March 31, April 30, May 31, June 30, July 31, August 31, September 30, October 31, November 30, December 31) and the Gregorian leap year rule (a year divisible by 4 is a leap year, except that a year divisible by 100 is a common year, except that a year divisible by 400 is a leap year).

Output format:
Print one line per pair, in the given order, in the format "YYYY-MM-DD + n days = YYYY-MM-DD". The YYYY-MM-DD on the left is the original date, n is the number of days to add (a decimal integer without extra leading zeros), and the YYYY-MM-DD on the right is the resulting date. A date is written with the year as 4 digits and the month and day as 2 digits each (with a leading 0 when it has 1 digit), joined by ASCII "-". All other parts are ASCII characters exactly as written, with one ASCII space before and after "+" and "=" and between n and "days". Print only these 5 lines, with no trailing whitespace at the end of any line, and end every line with a newline.

Example output (the numbers are illustrative and differ from the actual answer):
2010-03-28 + 7 days = 2010-04-04
2015-12-30 + 400 days = 2017-02-02
