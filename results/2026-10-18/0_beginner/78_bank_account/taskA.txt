Process the following 10 transactions on 3 bank accounts, in order from top to bottom. The account holders and their initial balances are Nina with 12000, Leo with 3500 and Emma with 800. Each holder's name is a single word consisting of one uppercase English letter followed by lowercase English letters, and all names are distinct. Each line is one transaction.
deposit Emma 1200
withdraw Leo 4000
transfer Nina Leo 5000
withdraw Leo 4000
transfer Emma Nina 2500
transfer Emma Nina 2000
withdraw Emma 300
deposit Nina 750
transfer Leo Emma 3200
withdraw Nina 9800
"deposit NAME AMOUNT" is a deposit: it adds the amount to the balance of that account. A deposit always succeeds. "withdraw NAME AMOUNT" is a withdrawal: it subtracts the amount from the balance of that account. "transfer FROM TO AMOUNT" is a transfer: it subtracts the amount from the balance of the source account FROM and adds the amount to the balance of the destination account TO. A withdrawal or transfer fails if the amount is greater than the balance of the account the money is taken from, and in that case no account balance changes. If the amount equals the balance, it succeeds. All amounts are positive integers, and the source and destination of a transfer are different accounts.

Output format:
Print one line per transaction, in the order processed: "op k: OPERATION ok" if it succeeded, or "op k: OPERATION failed (insufficient funds)" if it failed. k is the transaction number (the first transaction is 1), and OPERATION is the transaction written in the same notation as the list above ("deposit NAME AMOUNT", "withdraw NAME AMOUNT", "transfer FROM TO AMOUNT"). After all transactions, print the final balance of each account, one line per account, in ascending order of holder names (lexicographic order comparing one character at a time by character code, smaller first), in the form "name: balance" (the name, ":", one ASCII space, the balance) (13 lines in total). Put one ASCII space between words, names and numbers, and after ":", and write "(insufficient funds)" exactly as shown. Write every number as a decimal integer with no extra leading zeros. Print only these 13 lines, with no trailing whitespace on any line, and end every line with a newline. The example output below is for 3 transactions on two different accounts (Ken with 4000 and Amy with 2000).

Example output (the numbers are illustrative and differ from the actual answer):
op 1: deposit Ken 500 ok
op 2: transfer Ken Amy 9000 failed (insufficient funds)
op 3: withdraw Amy 250 ok
Amy: 1750
Ken: 4500
