Write a program that removes the duplicate elements from an array of integers (15 elements) whose values are 12, 5, 12, 8, 3, 5, 20, 8, 8, 15, 3, 7, 20, 12 and 9, respectively. For each value, keep only its first occurrence and remove its second and later occurrences. Arrange the remaining values in the order in which they first appear in the original array.

Output format:
Output the following 2 lines.
Line 1: the values remaining after the duplicates are removed, in order of first appearance, separated by one space
Line 2: "removed:", one space, and the number of removed elements (a decimal integer), in this order
Do not put trailing whitespace at the end of any line, and end every line, including the last one, with a newline.

Example output (the numbers are illustrative and differ from the actual answer):
6 2 9 4
removed: 5
