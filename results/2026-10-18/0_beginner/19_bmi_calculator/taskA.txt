For 5 people whose name, height and weight are Aiko, 158 cm, 45 kg; Kenji, 172 cm, 68 kg; Mika, 165 cm, 70 kg; Ryo, 180 cm, 101 kg; and Yuki, 150 cm, 52 kg, write a program that computes each person's BMI and classifies their degree of obesity. BMI is computed as weight (kg) * 10000 / (height (cm) * height (cm)). Compute weight * 10000 and height * height each as integers, then divide the two as real numbers with a single division (this equals the weight divided by the square of the height converted to meters). Classify using the BMI before rounding: less than 18.5 is underweight, at least 18.5 and less than 25 is normal, at least 25 and less than 30 is overweight, and 30 or more is obese.

Output format:
In the given order, output one line per person in the form "name: BMI x.x (category)". name is the name, x.x is the BMI rounded half up at the second decimal place to 1 decimal place (the integer part has no extra leading zeros, and there is always exactly 1 digit after the decimal point), and category is the English word of the classification result (all lowercase ASCII). All other parts are the ASCII characters exactly as written; put a single space after ":", after "BMI" and after the number (before "("), and put no whitespace inside the parentheses. Output only these 5 lines, with no trailing whitespace on any line, and end every line with a newline.

Example output (the numbers are illustrative and differ from the actual answer):
Taro: BMI 21.4 (normal)
Hana: BMI 17.9 (underweight)
