Write a program that inserts 12 distinct integer keys with the values 50, 27, 74, 13, 39, 62, 88, 6, 31, 45, 70, 34, in this order, into an empty binary search tree (no balancing is done). To insert a key, start at the root; if the key being inserted is smaller than the key of the current node, move to the left child, and if it is larger, move to the right child; if there is no child in the direction of the move, place the key there as a new node (the first key becomes the root).
For the tree after all insertions, list the keys in the following 4 orders and find the height of the tree. Inorder visits the left subtree, the node itself, then the right subtree; preorder visits the node itself, the left subtree, then the right subtree; postorder visits the left subtree, the right subtree, then the node itself; each recursively. Levelorder visits nodes starting from those closest to the root (shallowest), and nodes at the same depth from left to right. The height of the tree is the number of nodes on the longest path from the root to a leaf (a tree consisting only of the root has height 1).
Next, delete key 6 and then key 27, in this order, from this tree, and find the preorder and the height of the tree after the deletions. To delete a node, find it by searching from the root, then do the following. If it has no children, simply remove the node. If it has exactly one child, connect that child (the subtree rooted at it) in the node's position. If it has two children, find the node holding the smallest key in the node's right subtree (the key that comes right after the node in inorder), replace the key of the node being deleted with that smallest key, and then remove the node that held the smallest key. That node has no left child, so if it has a right child, connect that right child (the subtree rooted at it) in the position of the removed node. In this data, the node with key 6 has no children, and the node with key 27 has two children.

Output format:
Output the following 7 lines.
Line 1, in the format "inorder: K1 K2 ...": the keys in inorder.
Line 2, in the format "preorder: K1 K2 ...": the keys in preorder.
Line 3, in the format "postorder: K1 K2 ...": the keys in postorder.
Line 4, in the format "levelorder: K1 K2 ...": the keys in levelorder.
Line 5, in the format "height: h": the height h of the tree.
Line 6, in the format "preorder after deletions: K1 K2 ...": the keys of the tree after the deletions, in preorder.
Line 7, in the format "height after deletions: h": the height h of the tree after the deletions.
K1 K2 ... is the keys in order, separated by exactly one ASCII space. Put exactly one ASCII space after ":" and between the English words on lines 6 and 7 (before and after "after"), and put no space before ":". Write every number as a decimal integer without a sign or extra leading zeros.
Output only these 7 lines; no line has trailing spaces, and every line ends with a newline. The example output below is for inserting the keys 20, 9, 31, 4, 15, 26, 12, 40 in this order and then deleting key 26 and key 9 in this order.

Example output (the numbers are illustrative and differ from the actual answer):
inorder: 4 9 12 15 20 26 31 40
preorder: 20 9 4 15 12 31 26 40
postorder: 4 12 15 9 26 40 31 20
levelorder: 20 9 31 4 15 26 40 12
height: 4
preorder after deletions: 20 12 4 15 31 40
height after deletions: 3
