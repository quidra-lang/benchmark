There is a grid of 6 rows and 7 columns. Writing each row, from top to bottom, as a 7-character string from its leftmost cell to its rightmost cell, the grid is as follows. "." is a passable cell and "#" is an impassable cell (an obstacle); the top-left and bottom-right cells are passable.
.......
..#....
....#..
.#.....
...#.#.
.......
Write a program that uses dynamic programming to count the paths that start at the top-left cell and reach the bottom-right cell by repeatedly moving only to the cell immediately to the right or immediately below. Count them in 2 cases: when every cell is regarded as passable (ignoring the obstacles), and when the obstacle cells are not passed through.

Output format:
Print "without obstacles: N" on the first line and "with obstacles: M" on the second line. N is the number of paths when the obstacles are ignored and M is the number of paths that do not pass through any obstacle cell; both are decimal integers without extra leading zeros. All other parts are ASCII characters exactly as written, with one ASCII space between words and after ":". Print only these 2 lines, with no trailing whitespace at the end of any line, and end every line with a newline.

Example output (the numbers are illustrative and differ from the actual answer):
without obstacles: 210
with obstacles: 17
