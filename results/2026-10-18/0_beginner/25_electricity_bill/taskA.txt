The names of 6 households and their monthly electricity usage (in kWh, integers) are as follows.
Sato: 85 kWh
Suzuki: 120 kWh
Takahashi: 254 kWh
Tanaka: 300 kWh
Ito: 438 kWh
Watanabe: 176 kWh
The electricity bill of each household (in yen) is a basic charge of 900 yen plus the energy charge. The energy charge is calculated at 30 yen per kWh for the part of the usage up to the first 120 kWh, 36 yen per kWh for the part above 120 kWh up to 300 kWh, and 40 yen per kWh for the part above 300 kWh. Write a program that finds the electricity bill of each household and the total of the electricity bills of the 6 households.

Output format:
First, output 6 lines, one line per household, in the order of the households above. Each line consists of, in this order: the household name, ":", one space, the usage, one space, "kWh,", one space, the electricity bill, one space, and "yen".
The 7th and last line consists of, in this order: "total:", one space, the total of the electricity bills of the 6 households, one space, and "yen".
Write every number as a decimal integer without digit-group separators. Do not put trailing whitespace at the end of any line, and end every line, including the last one, with a newline.

Example output (the numbers are illustrative and differ from the actual answer):
Sato: 50 kWh, 2400 yen
Suzuki: 200 kWh, 7380 yen
Takahashi: 90 kWh, 3600 yen
Tanaka: 310 kWh, 11380 yen
Ito: 0 kWh, 900 yen
Watanabe: 150 kWh, 5580 yen
total: 31240 yen
