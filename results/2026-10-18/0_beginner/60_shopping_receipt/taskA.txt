The name, unit price (yen), quantity and tax category of each of the 6 items in a shopping cart are as follows.
milk: 198 yen, 2 pieces, food
notebook: 165 yen, 3 pieces, general
bread: 248 yen, 1 piece, food
apple: 128 yen, 4 pieces, food
detergent: 437 yen, 1 piece, general
eggs: 268 yen, 1 piece, food
Compute the subtotal of each item (unit price * quantity). Next, for each tax category, compute the sum of the subtotals of the items in that category (the category total) and the consumption tax amount. The tax rate is 8% for food and 10% for general; the consumption tax amount is the category total multiplied by the rate number (8 or 10), divided by 100, with the fractional part truncated, as an integer. Finally, compute the total payment, which is the sum of all 6 item subtotals and the consumption tax amounts of the 2 categories.

Output format:
Print 9 lines in total.
Lines 1-6: one line per item, in the order of the items above, consisting of, in this order: the item name, one space, the unit price, one space, a lowercase "x", one space, the quantity, one space, "=", one space, and the subtotal.
Line 7: in this order: "food:", one space, the category total of food, one space, "yen,", one space, "tax", one space, the consumption tax amount of food, one space, and "yen".
Line 8: the same format as line 7, with "food:" replaced by "general:", giving the category total and the consumption tax amount of general.
Line 9: in this order: "total:", one space, the total payment, one space, and "yen".
Write every number as a decimal integer without digit grouping separators. Do not put trailing whitespace at the end of any line, and end every line, including the last one, with a newline.

Example output (the numbers are illustrative and differ from the actual answer):
milk 150 x 3 = 450
notebook 120 x 2 = 240
bread 210 x 1 = 210
apple 98 x 5 = 490
detergent 398 x 1 = 398
eggs 230 x 2 = 460
food: 1610 yen, tax 128 yen
general: 638 yen, tax 63 yen
total: 2439 yen
