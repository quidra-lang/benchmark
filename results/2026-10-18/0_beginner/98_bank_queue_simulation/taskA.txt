Write a program that uses the random number generator MINSTD to simulate 20 customers visiting, one after another, a bank with 2 tellers. MINSTD keeps an integer x as its state and repeatedly updates it by taking the remainder of x multiplied by 48271, divided by 2147483647, as the new x. The initial value (seed) of x is 42. Each time a random number is used, update x once and use the updated x (the first value used is the seed 42 updated once). Note that the product of 48271 and x can greatly exceed 2^31, so compute it without overflow or rounding error.
For customer 1, customer 2, ..., customer 20 in this order, use 2 random numbers per customer. From the 1st random number x, compute the interarrival time "x mod 5 + 1", and from the 2nd random number x, compute the service time at the teller "x mod 7 + 2". Times are integers starting from 0; the arrival time of customer 1 is time 0 plus the interarrival time of customer 1, and the arrival time of customer k (k >= 2) is the arrival time of customer k-1 plus the interarrival time of customer k.
Teller 1 and teller 2 each have a "free time", both initially 0. Customers are assigned to a teller one at a time in the order they arrive. The assigned teller is the one with the smaller free time at that moment; when the free times are equal, it is teller 1. The start time of service is the larger of the customer's arrival time and the assigned teller's free time, the end time is the start time plus the service time, and the assigned teller's free time is updated to this end time. The wait time is the start time minus the arrival time.

Output format:
Output one line per customer, starting from customer 1, in the format "customer k: arrive=a start=s end=e teller=t wait=w". k is the customer number, a is the arrival time, s is the start time, e is the end time, t is the number of the assigned teller (1 or 2), and w is the wait time.
Next, output in the format "average wait: x.xx" the average wait time of the 20 customers (the sum of the wait times divided by 20) with exactly 2 digits after the decimal point. Because it is divided by 20, this value terminates within 2 decimal places, and no rounding occurs.
Then output the maximum wait time m in the format "max wait: m", and finally output the latest of the 20 customers' end times, d, in the format "last departure: d".
Put exactly one ASCII space after "customer", after ":", between items, and after each of "average", "max", "last", and put no space before or after "=". Write every number other than the average as a decimal integer without a sign or extra leading zeros.
Output only these 23 lines; no line has trailing spaces, and every line ends with a newline. The example output below is for 4 customers (the average is the sum of the wait times divided by 4).

Example output (the numbers are illustrative and differ from the actual answer):
customer 1: arrive=2 start=2 end=7 teller=1 wait=0
customer 2: arrive=4 start=4 end=10 teller=2 wait=0
customer 3: arrive=5 start=7 end=9 teller=1 wait=2
customer 4: arrive=6 start=9 end=12 teller=1 wait=3
average wait: 1.25
max wait: 3
last departure: 12
