Write a program that converts 6 durations, given in seconds as 0, 59, 3725, 45296, 86400 and 312047, into hours, minutes and seconds. The hours do not wrap around at 24 and are the total number of hours (every value is less than 100 hours); the minutes and the seconds are each between 0 and 59 inclusive.

Output format:
For the 6 values above, in the given order, output one line each in the form "S seconds = HH:MM:SS". S is the original number of seconds, written as a decimal integer with no extra leading zeros. HH, MM and SS are the hours, minutes and seconds respectively, each always written with exactly 2 digits, padded with a leading 0 when it has only 1 digit. All other parts are the ASCII characters exactly as written; S, "seconds", "=" and HH:MM:SS are separated from each other by single spaces, and there is no whitespace before or after ":". Output only these 6 lines, with no trailing whitespace on any line, and end every line with a newline.

Example output (the numbers are illustrative and differ from the actual answer):
100 seconds = 00:01:40
93784 seconds = 26:03:04
