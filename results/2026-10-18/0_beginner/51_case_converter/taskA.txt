Write a program that rewrites the 6 identifiers user_name, maxRetryCount, ShoppingCartItem, background-color, get_last_login_time, isEmailAddressValid, in the given order, in the 4 naming conventions snake_case, camelCase, PascalCase and kebab-case. Every identifier consists only of ASCII letters and "_" and "-", and is made of 2 to 4 words. No abbreviations are included, so only the first character of each word can be uppercase.
First split the identifier into words. Split at the positions of "_" and "-" and immediately before every uppercase letter ("_" and "-" themselves are discarded), discard any parts that are empty after splitting (in a PascalCase identifier, splitting immediately before the leading uppercase letter produces an empty part), and convert each word entirely to lowercase. From the resulting words, build the following 4 forms. snake joins all words with "_"; camel takes the first word as is and the second and later words with only their first character uppercased, joined without separators; pascal takes all words with only their first character uppercased, joined without separators; kebab joins all words with "-".

Output format:
Print one line per identifier, in the given order, in the format "ID: snake=S, camel=C, pascal=P, kebab=K". ID is the original identifier itself, and S, C, P, K are the identifier rewritten in the snake, camel, pascal and kebab forms respectively. All other parts are ASCII characters exactly as written, with one ASCII space after each ":" and ",", and no whitespace before or after "=". Print only these 6 lines, with no trailing whitespace at the end of any line, and end every line with a newline.

Example output (the numbers are illustrative and differ from the actual answer):
fileName: snake=file_name, camel=fileName, pascal=FileName, kebab=file-name
last-update-date: snake=last_update_date, camel=lastUpdateDate, pascal=LastUpdateDate, kebab=last-update-date
