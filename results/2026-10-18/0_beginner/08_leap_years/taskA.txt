For the array (8 elements) of years in the Common Era (AD) whose values are 1900, 1988, 2000, 2019, 2024, 2026, 2032 and 2100, determine whether each year is a leap year or a common year. Use the rules of the Gregorian calendar. A year divisible by 4 is a leap year, and a year not divisible by 4 is a common year. However, a year that is divisible by 100 but not divisible by 400 is a common year.

Output format:
Print one line per year, in array order, 8 lines in total.
Each line consists of, in this order: the year (a 4-digit decimal integer) immediately followed by ":", one ASCII space, and the result. The result is "leap year" for a leap year and "common year" for a common year (in both, the two words are separated by one ASCII space).
Print only these 8 lines, with no trailing whitespace on any line, and end every line with a newline.

Example output (the numbers are illustrative and differ from the actual answer):
1996: leap year
2023: common year
