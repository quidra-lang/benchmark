For each of 5 amounts, 3780 yen, 15420 yen, 99999 yen, 506 yen and 48263 yen, compute the breakdown of the number of bills and coins when the amount is split into bills and coins. There are 9 denominations: 10000 yen, 5000 yen, 1000 yen, 500 yen, 100 yen, 50 yen, 10 yen, 5 yen and 1 yen. Going from the largest denomination down, the number of pieces of a denomination is the quotient of the remaining amount divided by that denomination's face value, and the remainder of that division is carried over to the next denomination (this uses as many pieces of each denomination as possible).

Output format:
Output one line per amount, in the given order (5 lines in total). Each line starts with the amount A, one ASCII space, and the ASCII "yen:". Then list only the denominations with 1 or more pieces, in decreasing order of face value, each in the form "<face value>x<count>". x is the ASCII lowercase letter x, and the face value, x and the count are joined with no spaces (for example, six 100-yen coins are "100x6"). Finally, append the total number N of bills and coins used for that amount in the form "(N pieces)". Put exactly one ASCII space after "yen:", between adjacent "<face value>x<count>" items, and before "(", and also exactly one ASCII space between "N" and "pieces". Put no spaces anywhere else. Write every number as a decimal integer without extra leading zeros. Output only these 5 lines, with no trailing whitespace on any line, and end every line with a newline.

Example output (the numbers are illustrative and differ from the actual answer):
2650 yen: 1000x2 500x1 100x1 50x1 (5 pieces)
70013 yen: 10000x7 10x1 1x3 (11 pieces)
