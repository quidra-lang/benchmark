Line 3 is the difference: "difference:" followed by its elements.
Line 4 is the symmetric difference: "symmetric difference:" followed by its elements.
On each line, list each value only once, in increasing order. Put exactly one ASCII space after the ":" of the label and between adjacent elements, and also exactly one ASCII space between "symmetric" and "difference". Write every number as a decimal integer without extra leading zeros. Output only these 4 lines, with no trailing whitespace on any line, and end every line with a newline.
