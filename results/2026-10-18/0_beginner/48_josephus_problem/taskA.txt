Write a program that, for the 3 cases where the number of people n and the count k are (7, 3), (10, 2), (41, 3), solves the Josephus problem and finds the order in which people are removed and the person who remains last.
People numbered 1 to n stand in a circle in numerical order (the person after person n is person 1). Starting from person 1, count 1, 2, 3, ..., and remove from the circle the person at whom the count reaches k. Then, starting again from 1 with the next remaining person after the removed person, remove the person at whom the count reaches k. Repeat this until everyone has been removed. Thus the first person removed is person k, and the last person removed is the person who remains last (the survivor).

Output format:
Print two lines per case, in the given order.
The first line has the format "n=N k=K: P1 P2 ... Pn", where N and K are n and k of that case, and P1 P2 ... Pn are the numbers of the removed people in the order they were removed, all n of them up to the last person removed.
The second line has the format "survivor: S", where S is the number of the survivor.
Put exactly one space between "n=N" and "k=K", after ":", and between consecutive numbers. Write every number as a decimal integer without a sign or extra leading zeros.
Print only these 6 lines, with no trailing whitespace on any line, and end every line with a newline.

Example output (the numbers are illustrative and differ from the actual answer):
n=5 k=2: 2 4 1 5 3
survivor: 3
