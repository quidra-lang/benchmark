For the undirected graph with the 7 vertices A to G and the 11 weighted edges A-B (7), A-C (3), A-D (9), B-C (2), C-E (6), B-D (3), D-E (4), D-F (5), E-F (3), E-G (8), F-G (4), find the shortest distance and the shortest path from vertex A to each vertex using Dijkstra's algorithm.
An edge "X-Y (w)" is an undirected edge connecting vertex X and vertex Y, and w is its weight (a positive integer). The distance of a path is the sum of the weights of the edges it passes through. In this data, every vertex is reachable from A, and the shortest path from A to each vertex is unique.
Dijkstra's algorithm proceeds as follows. Initially, the distance of A is 0 and the distance of every other vertex is infinity (unreached). Choose one vertex u with the smallest distance among the vertices not yet finalized and finalize it (if several vertices share the smallest distance, choose the one among them whose name comes first in alphabetical order); then, for each vertex v connected to u by an edge, if the distance of u plus the weight of edge u-v is smaller than the current distance of v, update the distance of v to that value and set the predecessor of v to u. Repeat this until all vertices are finalized.
The shortest path to each vertex is obtained by following predecessors from that vertex until A is reached and then listing the vertices in reverse order.

Output format:
For each vertex, in the order A, B, C, D, E, F, G, print one line in the format "X: distance=D path=P".
X is the vertex name, and D is the shortest distance from A to X, written as a decimal integer without extra leading zeros. P is the names of the vertices on the shortest path from A to X, in the order in which they are passed from A to X, joined by ASCII hyphens "-". The line for A itself is "A: distance=0 path=A".
"distance" and "path" are lowercase ASCII letters. Put one ASCII space after ":" and between D and "path", and no whitespace before or after "=" and "-". Print only these 7 lines, with no trailing whitespace on any line, and end every line with a newline.

Example output (the numbers are illustrative and differ from the actual answer):
A: distance=0 path=A
B: distance=4 path=A-B
C: distance=6 path=A-B-C
D: distance=5 path=A-D
E: distance=7 path=A-D-E
F: distance=10 path=A-B-C-F
G: distance=13 path=A-D-E-G
