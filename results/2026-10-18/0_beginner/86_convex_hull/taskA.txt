Given 15 points in the plane whose integer coordinates are (3, 2), (-6, 1), (8, 4), (0, 0), (-3, 7), (4, -3), (1, -5), (-2, 1), (5, 6), (-6, -2), (2, -2), (7, -1), (-3, 4), (2, 8), (-6, 3), find the convex hull of these points using Andrew's monotone chain algorithm, and compute its vertices and its area. Each point is written as (x, y), with the x-coordinate first and the y-coordinate second. No two points have the same coordinates.
The vertices of the convex hull are only those points on the boundary of the hull that are corners; points in the middle of a boundary edge (points on the segment connecting two adjacent vertices, other than its endpoints) are not included as vertices.
For three points O, A, B with coordinates (Ox, Oy), (Ax, Ay), (Bx, By), let cross(O, A, B) = (Ax - Ox) * (By - Oy) - (Ay - Oy) * (Bx - Ox) (computed with integers).
The procedure is as follows.
- Sort the points by x-coordinate in increasing order, and points with equal x-coordinates by y-coordinate in increasing order.
- Lower chain: start with an empty sequence and look at the points one at a time in sorted order. Let B be the current point; while the sequence has 2 or more points and cross(O, A, B) is 0 or less, where O is the second-to-last point of the sequence and A is its last point, remove the last point of the sequence. Then append B to the end of the sequence.
- Upper chain: look at the points in the reverse of the sorted order, and build a separate sequence with the same processing as for the lower chain.
- Remove the last point from each of the lower chain and the upper chain, and concatenate the upper chain after the lower chain. This gives the vertices of the convex hull in counterclockwise order, and the first vertex is the point with the smallest y-coordinate among the points with the smallest x-coordinate.
The area S is computed with the shoelace formula: with the vertices in this order written as (x1, y1), (x2, y2), ..., (xn, yn), S = ((x1 * y2 - x2 * y1) + (x2 * y3 - x3 * y2) + ... + (xn * y1 - x1 * yn)) / 2. The value before the division is an integer, so S is either an integer or a value whose fractional part is exactly 0.5.

Output format:
On line 1, print the number of vertices H of the convex hull in the format "hull: H points".
Then print the vertices of the convex hull, one per line, in the format "(x, y)", in the order obtained by the procedure above (counterclockwise, starting from the point with the smallest y-coordinate among the points with the smallest x-coordinate).
On the last line, print "area: A", where A is the area S written with exactly 1 digit after the decimal point (S is an integer or has fractional part 0.5, so no rounding occurs).
Write numbers in decimal without extra leading zeros, with a leading "-" for negative numbers. "(", ")" and "," are ASCII characters. Put one ASCII space each after ",", after the ":" of "hull:" and of "area:", and between H and "points", and no whitespace anywhere else. Print only 1 + H + 1 lines, with no trailing whitespace on any line, and end every line with a newline.

Example output (the numbers are illustrative and differ from the actual answer):
hull: 4 points
(-2, 0)
(3, -4)
(5, 1)
(0, 6)
area: 36.5
