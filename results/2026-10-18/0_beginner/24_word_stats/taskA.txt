The English sentence "we planted beans and carrots in the garden behind our old house" consists of 12 words made only of lowercase letters, separated by one space each. The double quotes only mark the boundaries of the sentence and are not part of it. Write a program that finds, for this sentence, the number of words, the longest word, the shortest word, and the average word length.
If there are several longest words or several shortest words, choose the one among them that appears first in the sentence. The average word length is the total number of characters of all the words divided by the number of words; perform this division as real-number division that keeps the fractional part, not as truncating integer division.

Output format:
Output the following 4 lines.
Line 1: "words:", one space, the number of words (a decimal integer)
Line 2: "longest:", one space, the longest word
Line 3: "shortest:", one space, the shortest word
Line 4: "average length:", one space, the average word length (rounded half up at the third decimal place and written with exactly 2 digits after the decimal point)
Do not put trailing whitespace at the end of any line, and end every line, including the last one, with a newline.

Example output (the numbers are illustrative and differ from the actual answer):
words: 9
longest: example
shortest: an
average length: 4.56
