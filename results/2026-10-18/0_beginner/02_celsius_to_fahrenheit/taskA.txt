For the array (7 elements) of Celsius temperatures (in degrees Celsius) whose values are -40, -15, -3, 0, 22, 37 and 100, convert each temperature to Fahrenheit. The Fahrenheit temperature is the Celsius temperature multiplied by 9, divided by 5, plus 32. Perform the division by 5 as real-number division that keeps the fractional part, not as truncating integer division.

Output format:
Print one line per Celsius temperature, in array order, 7 lines in total.
Each line consists of, in this order: the Celsius temperature, one ASCII space, "C", one ASCII space, "=", one ASCII space, the Fahrenheit temperature, one ASCII space, "F".
Write the Celsius temperature unchanged as a decimal integer, and write the Fahrenheit temperature rounded half up to 1 decimal place (rounding at the second decimal place), with exactly 1 digit after the decimal point (append ".0" even when the fractional part is 0). Prefix negative numbers with "-". Print only these 7 lines, with no trailing whitespace on any line, and end every line with a newline.

Example output (the numbers are illustrative and differ from the actual answer):
25 C = 77.0 F
-8 C = 17.6 F
