Write a program that, for 6 students whose names and scores in Japanese, Mathematics and English (integers from 0 to 100) are Tanaka (78, 85, 94), Suzuki (88, 74, 82), Yamada (65, 90, 71), Ito (80, 79, 85), Kato (92, 88, 95), Mori (70, 62, 78), computes each student's total score and average score and produces a ranked grade list.
The scores in parentheses are in the order Japanese, Mathematics, English. All names are distinct, and each is a single word whose first letter is an uppercase English letter and the rest are lowercase English letters.
The total score is the sum of the three subject scores, and the average score is the total score divided by 3 (this division is real-number division computed including the fractional part, not truncating integer division).
Sort the students in descending order of total score, and students with equal total scores in alphabetical (ascending) order of name.
Ranks are determined by total score: students with equal total scores get the same rank, and the rank of the next student skips as many ranks as there are tied students (for example, if the 2nd and 3rd students from the top have equal total scores, the ranks of the first 4 students are 1, 2, 2, 4).

Output format:
Print one student per line, in the order above, in the format "R. NAME total=T average=A".
R is the rank, NAME is the name, and T is the total score; write R and T as decimal integers without a sign or extra leading zeros. A is the average score written with exactly 2 digits after the decimal point (rounding half up at the third digit).
Put exactly one space after "R.", after NAME, and after T, and put no whitespace before or after "=".
Print only these 6 lines, with no trailing whitespace on any line, and end every line with a newline.

Example output (the numbers are illustrative and differ from the actual answer):
1. Sato total=270 average=90.00
2. Abe total=251 average=83.67
2. Ueda total=251 average=83.67
4. Ono total=199 average=66.33
