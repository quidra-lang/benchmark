Print the multiplication table with 9 rows and 9 columns. Row i of the table (i from 1 to 9) contains the 9 products i*1, i*2, ..., i*9 in this order.

Output format:
Print rows 1 through 9 in order, 9 lines in total.
Each line is the 9 products of that row, each right-aligned in a field 3 characters wide (padded on the left with ASCII spaces when it has fewer than 3 digits), concatenated with no separator. Therefore every line starts with an ASCII space.
Write numbers as decimal integers. Print only these 9 lines, with no trailing whitespace on any line, and end every line with a newline. The example output below is for a table with 4 rows and 4 columns.

Example output (the numbers are illustrative and differ from the actual answer):
  1  2  3  4
  2  4  6  8
  3  6  9 12
  4  8 12 16
