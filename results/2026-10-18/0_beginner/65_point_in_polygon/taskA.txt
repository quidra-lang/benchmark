On the coordinate plane there is a polygon formed by connecting the following 8 vertices with line segments in this order and closing it by returning from the last vertex to the first vertex. This polygon is a non-convex polygon whose edges do not cross each other.
(1, 1), (11, 2), (13, 9), (9, 7), (7, 12), (5, 6), (2, 10), (3, 5)
For each of the following 6 points, determine whether it is inside or outside the polygon.
(4, 8), (6, 4), (10, 8), (7, 11), (14, 3), (12, 8)
For the determination, use the ray casting method with the even-odd rule. That is, draw a horizontal ray from the point (px, py) in the positive direction of the x-axis; if the number of polygon edges that the ray crosses is odd, the point is inside, and if it is even, the point is outside. An edge with endpoints (x1, y1) and (x2, y2) crosses the ray when one of y1 and y2 is greater than py and the other is less than py, and the x-coordinate of the intersection of that edge with the line y = py, x1 + (x2 - x1) * (py - y1) / (y2 - y1), is greater than px. The "/" in this formula is real-number division that does not truncate the fractional part. Take care that the quotient is not truncated by integer division. No point lies on an edge of the polygon, and every point has a y-coordinate different from that of every vertex, so the ray never passes through a vertex and the x-coordinate of an intersection is never equal to px.

Output format:
Print 6 lines in total, one line per point, in the order of the points above. Each line consists of, in this order: "(", the x-coordinate of the point, ",", one space, the y-coordinate of the point, "):", one space, and the result. The result is "inside" if the point is inside and "outside" if it is outside (both in lowercase ASCII letters). Write coordinates as decimal integers. Do not put trailing whitespace at the end of any line, and end every line, including the last one, with a newline.

Example output (the numbers are illustrative and differ from the actual answer):
(8, 3): inside
(0, 4): outside
(5, 11): outside
