Summarize the following sales data in CSV format, which consists of 13 lines. The first line is the header line, and the 12 lines from the second line on are data lines. In the program, prepare this text as-is as an array of strings with one line per element, and read each line by splitting it at the commas ",".
date,region,product,quantity,unit_price
2026-04-01,north,notebook,10,200
2026-04-01,east,marker,6,150
2026-04-01,south,eraser,25,80
2026-04-02,west,stapler,4,600
2026-04-02,north,marker,8,150
2026-04-02,east,notebook,9,200
2026-04-03,south,stapler,3,600
2026-04-03,west,eraser,15,80
2026-04-03,north,eraser,20,80
2026-04-04,east,stapler,5,600
2026-04-04,south,notebook,6,200
2026-04-04,west,marker,8,150
Each data line contains the date (in YYYY-MM-DD format), the region name, the product name, the quantity and the unit price, in this order, separated by commas. Region names and product names are single words consisting only of lowercase ASCII letters, and the quantity and the unit price are positive integers. The sales amount of each data line is quantity * unit price.
Find the total sales amount over all data lines, the total sales amount for each region, the total quantity for each product, and the day with the largest total sales amount per date.
Alphabetical order compares two names character by character from the beginning, and the first pair of differing characters decides the order by their alphabetical order (in this data, no name is a prefix of another name).

Output format:
Print the following lines in this order.
Line 1 is "total: T", where T is the total sales amount over all data lines.
Next, print one line per region in the format "REGION: R", where REGION is the region name and R is the total sales amount of that region. Order the regions by R in decreasing order, and regions with equal R in alphabetical order of region name.
Next, print one line per product in the format "PRODUCT: Q", where PRODUCT is the product name and Q is the total quantity of that product. Order the products in alphabetical order of product name.
The last line is "best day: DATE (S)", where DATE is the date of the day with the largest total sales amount per date, written in the same YYYY-MM-DD format as in the data, and S is the total sales amount of that day. If several days share the largest total, choose the earliest date among them.
Write every number as a decimal integer without extra leading zeros and without thousands separators. Put one ASCII space each after ":", between "best" and "day:", and between DATE and "(". "(" and ")" are ASCII parentheses, with no whitespace between them and S. Print only 1 + (number of regions) + (number of products) + 1 lines, with no trailing whitespace on any line, and end every line with a newline.

Example output (the numbers are illustrative and differ from the actual answer):
total: 15600
west: 6200
east: 4700
north: 4700
apple: 30
banana: 14
best day: 2026-05-03 (6100)
