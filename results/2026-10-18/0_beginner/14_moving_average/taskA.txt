The sales (integers) of a shop over 10 days are, from day 1 onward, 120, 135, 98, 143, 160, 152, 171, 149, 188 and 176. Write a program that computes the 3-day moving average. For each day d = 3, 4, ..., 10, the moving average of day d is the sum of the sales of the 3 days d-2, d-1 and d divided by 3 (a real number).

Output format:
Output one line per day, in day order from d = 3 to d = 10, in the form "day d: A". d is the day number (an integer, with day 1 being 1), and A is that day's moving average rounded half up at the third decimal place to 2 decimal places, always written with exactly 2 digits after the decimal point. All other parts are the ASCII characters exactly as written; there is a single space between "day" and d, and a single space between ":" and A. Output only these 8 lines, with no trailing whitespace on any line, and end every line with a newline.

Example output (the numbers are illustrative and differ from the actual answer):
day 3: 101.67
day 4: 98.33
day 5: 104.00
