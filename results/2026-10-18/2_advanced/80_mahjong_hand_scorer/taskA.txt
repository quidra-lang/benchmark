[Overview]
Write a program that analyzes wins (agari) in riichi mahjong and computes their scores. The program reads the rule settings input/rules.txt, the list of winning situations input/hands.txt, and the list of 13-tile hands input/waits.txt.
For each winning situation, after checking the input for errors, the program enumerates by backtracking every way of splitting the hand into four sets and one pair, and also checks the seven-pairs and thirteen-orphans forms. For each combination of a split and a position of the winning tile (a candidate), it determines the yaku (including yakuman), dora, fu, han count and base points, selects the highest candidate, and computes the payments and the point transfer. It also detects furiten and the absence of yaku (no yaku).
For each 13-tile hand, it computes the shanten numbers for the standard form, seven pairs and thirteen orphans, the tiles that advance the shanten number (acceptance tiles) with their remaining counts, and, for hands that are tenpai, the ron and tsumo points for each waiting tile and furiten.
The results are written to output/scores.txt, output/waits.txt and output/summary.csv. Nothing is printed to standard output. No random numbers and no real numbers are used; every computation is done with integers.

[Input files]
There are three input files: input/rules.txt, input/hands.txt and input/waits.txt. Every file consists only of ASCII characters, and every line (including the last line) ends with a line feed (LF). There are no empty lines. The items within a line are separated by single ASCII spaces, and there are no spaces at the start or end of a line. Integers are written in decimal without extra leading zeros.
Each line of hands.txt and waits.txt consists of a label followed by items of the form "<key>=<value>" in a fixed order. Values contain no spaces and no "=".

* Tile strings
A tile is written as a number (one digit) and a suit (one character, one of m, p, s, z) (the meaning is explained in [Processing] section 1). Several tiles are written as a "tile string", defined as follows.
- A tile string is a string made by concatenating one or more parts, each of the form "one or more digits (0 to 9) followed by one suit character". Each digit of a part represents one tile with that number and that suit. For example, "123m406p11z" is the 8 tiles 1m, 2m, 3m, 4p, 0p, 6p, 1z, 1z. The digits within a part may be in any order, and parts with the same suit may appear any number of times.
- The digits of a part whose suit is z must be 1 to 7.
- A string that does not have this form (the empty string, a string containing a character other than digits and suit characters, a string with a suit character not immediately preceded by a digit, a string ending with a digit, a string with 0, 8 or 9 in a z part) is an invalid tile string.
- For some items, the value "-" means that there are no tiles at all (an empty tile string). This is stated in the description of each such item. For any other item, "-" is an invalid tile string.

(1) input/rules.txt (rule settings)
Each line describes one rule setting. Each line has the following format.
  <name> open_tanyao=<yes|no> red_fives=<yes|no> double_yakuman=<yes|no> kazoe_yakuman=<yes|no> kiriage=<yes|no> honba=<integer>
- <name> is one uppercase English letter and differs on every line. The order of the file is called "rule order".
- open_tanyao: if yes, all simples (tanyao) is allowed even for a hand with calls (section 5).
- red_fives: if yes, red fives (0m, 0p, 0s) are used. When no, the appearance of a red five is an error (section 2).
- double_yakuman: if yes, some yakuman count as double yakuman (multiplier 2) (section 6).
- kazoe_yakuman: if yes, 13 han or more counts as counted yakuman (kazoe yakuman) (section 9).
- kiriage: if yes, rounded-up mangan (kiriage mangan) is applied (section 9).
- honba: the points per honba (a positive integer that is a multiple of 3).

(2) input/hands.txt (winning situations)
Each line describes one winning situation. Each line has the following format.
  <label> rules=<rule> hand=<hand> melds=<melds> win=<winning tile> by=<win method> seat=<seat wind> from=<discarder> round=<round wind> flags=<flags> dora=<dora indicators> ura=<ura-dora indicators> discards=<discards> honba=<honba count> sticks=<stick count>
- <label> is a string of uppercase English letters and digits and differs on every line. The order of the file is called "situation order".
- rules: the name of a rule setting in rules.txt.
- hand: the tile string of the tiles of the hand that are not part of melds, excluding the winning tile.
- melds: "-" if there are no melds; otherwise the melds joined with ",". Each meld is a one-character type letter followed by a tile string. The type letter should be one of C (chi), P (pon), K (open kan) and A (closed kan), but it may be wrong (section 2). Each meld is at least one character long.
- win: the tile string of the winning tile (it should be one tile).
- by: ron for a ron win, tsumo for a tsumo win.
- seat: the player's own wind (seat wind), one of E, S, W, N. The player with E is the dealer.
- from: for a ron win, the wind of the discarder (the player who discarded the winning tile) (one of E, S, W, N); for a tsumo win it should be "-". It may be wrong (section 2).
- round: the round wind, one of E, S, W, N.
- flags: "-" if there are no flags; otherwise the flags joined with ",". Each flag is one of riichi, double_riichi, ippatsu, haitei, houtei, rinshan, chankan, tenhou, chiihou, and the same flag does not appear twice. They mean, respectively, that the situation is one in which riichi, double riichi, ippatsu, haitei (win on the last drawn tile), houtei (win on the last discard), rinshan (win on the replacement tile after a kan), chankan (robbing a kan), tenhou and chiihou hold.
- dora: the tile string of the dora indicators.
- ura: the tile string of the ura-dora indicators; "-" means there are no ura-dora indicators.
- discards: the tile string of the player's own discards; "-" means there are no discards.
- honba: the honba count (an integer >= 0).
- sticks: the number of riichi sticks deposited on the table (an integer >= 0), including those of the winner.

(3) input/waits.txt (13-tile hands)
Each line describes one hand. Each line has the following format.
  <label> rules=<rule> hand=<hand> seat=<seat wind> round=<round wind> riichi=<yes|no> dora=<dora indicators> discards=<discards> visible=<visible tiles>
- <label> is a string of uppercase English letters and digits and differs on every line.
- rules, seat and round are the same as in hands.txt.
- hand: the tile string of a hand without melds (it should be 13 tiles).
- riichi: yes means that the player has declared riichi (or intends to declare it).
- dora: the tile string of the dora indicators.
- discards: the tile string of the player's own discards; "-" means there are no discards.
- visible: the tile string of the other visible tiles, such as other players' discards and melds; "-" means there are no such tiles.

[Processing]
Process according to the following sections 1 to 13. Sections 1 to 11 process the situations of hands.txt, and sections 12 to 13 process the hands of waits.txt.

* 1. Tiles and terms
(1) There are the following 34 kinds of tiles.
  - Number tiles: each of the three suits characters (m), circles (p) and bamboo (s) has the numbers 1 to 9, giving the 27 kinds 1m, 2m, ..., 9s.
  - Honor tiles: the 7 kinds 1z (East), 2z (South), 3z (West), 4z (North), 5z (White), 6z (Green), 7z (Red). 1z to 4z are called wind tiles, and 5z to 7z are called dragon tiles. The tiles of the winds E, S, W, N are 1z, 2z, 3z, 4z respectively.
  The red fives 0m, 0p, 0s are tiles of the same kinds as 5m, 5p, 5s respectively. The only difference between a red five and an ordinary 5 is that a red five is counted as red dora (section 7). Below, the "kind" of a tile is used in this sense, and when counting tiles, a red five is also counted as the kind of its 5.
(2) The order of kinds is 1m, 2m, ..., 9m, 1p, ..., 9p, 1s, ..., 9s, 1z, 2z, ..., 7z. This order is called "kind order".
(3) Tile name: a kind written as one digit followed by one suit character (e.g. 1m, 7z). The tile names of the kinds of the red fives are 5m, 5p, 5s.
(4) Yaochuu tiles are the number tiles whose number is 1 or 9 and the honor tiles; simple tiles are the number tiles whose number is 2 to 8. The number tiles whose number is 1 or 9 are called terminal tiles. The green tiles are 2s, 3s, 4s, 6s, 8s, 6z.
(5) Block notation: a group of 2 to 4 tiles of the same suit, written as the digits of their kinds in increasing order followed by the suit character (e.g. "44p", "345p", "777z", "2222z"). A red five is written as 5.
(6) Terms for a situation (n is the number of melds).
  - Among the melds, those of type C, P or K are called calls. A hand without calls (with no melds, or with only closed kans) is called a closed hand.
  - Concealed part: the 14 - 3 * n tiles made of the hand and the winning tile together.
  - All tiles: the concealed part together with the tiles of all melds (all 4 tiles of a kan are included).
  - Riichi situation: a situation whose flags include riichi or double_riichi.
  - The dealer is the player whose seat wind is E; non-dealers are the other players.

* 2. Checking a situation
For each situation, check the following (a) to (j) in order, and the <message> of the first one that applies is the error of that situation. For a situation with an error, the only things done are writing the error to scores.txt and summary.csv and counting it in the statistics ([Output files]).
  (a) Any of the values of hand, win, dora, ura, discards, or the part of a meld after its type letter, is an invalid tile string ("-" for ura and discards is a correct value with no tiles), or the number of tiles of win is not 1: bad tile notation
  (b) There are 5 or more melds, or there is a meld that does not satisfy the following condition: bad meld
    - C: 3 tiles, one each of the kinds of three consecutive numbers of the same number suit (e.g. 3m, 4m, 5m; the order of the kinds does not matter).
    - P: 3 tiles of the same kind.
    - K, A: 4 tiles of the same kind.
    - A meld whose type letter is none of C, P, K, A does not satisfy this condition.
  (c) The number of tiles of the hand is not 13 - 3 * n: wrong tile count
  (d) For all tiles of the hand, the winning tile, the melds, the dora indicators, the ura-dora indicators and the discards together, check the following (d1) to (d3) in order.
    (d1) The rule's red_fives is no and there is at least one red five: red five not allowed
    (d2) There is a kind with 5 or more tiles: too many copies of <tile name> (the first such kind in kind order)
    (d3) There are 2 or more red fives of the same suit: too many copies of <one of 0m, 0p, 0s> (the first that applies in the order m, p, s)
  (e) by is tsumo and from is not "-", by is ron and from is "-", or by is ron and from is the same as seat: invalid discarder
  (f) Check the flags in the order riichi, double_riichi, ippatsu, haitei, houtei, rinshan, chankan, tenhou, chiihou; the first flag present in the situation that does not satisfy the following condition: invalid flag <flag>
    - riichi: there are no calls.
    - double_riichi: there are no calls, and the flags do not include riichi.
    - ippatsu: the flags include riichi or double_riichi, and do not include rinshan.
    - haitei: the win is by tsumo, and the flags do not include rinshan.
    - houtei: the win is by ron, and the flags do not include chankan.
    - rinshan: the win is by tsumo, and there is at least one meld of type K or A.
    - chankan: the win is by ron.
    - tenhou: the seat wind is E, the win is by tsumo, there are no melds, and this is the only flag.
    - chiihou: the seat wind is not E, the win is by tsumo, there are no melds, and this is the only flag.
  (g) The number of dora indicators is not 1 + (the number of melds of type K or A): invalid dora indicators
  (h) In a riichi situation, the number of ura-dora indicators differs from the number of dora indicators, or there are ura-dora indicators although the situation is not a riichi situation: invalid ura indicators
  (i) In a riichi situation, sticks is 0: invalid sticks
  (j) The concealed part is not a winning form (section 3 (5)): not a winning hand

* 3. Winning forms and waiting tiles
(1) A set is a triplet (3 tiles of the same kind) or a sequence (one tile each of three consecutive numbers of the same number suit, e.g. 3p, 4p, 5p). There are no sequences of honor tiles. A pair is 2 tiles of the same kind.
(2) Standard decomposition: a way of splitting the concealed part exactly into 1 pair and 4 - n sets. Two splits with the same kind of pair and the same collection of sets (including the multiplicity when the same set appears more than once) are regarded as the same decomposition.
(3) Seven-pairs form: there are no melds, and the 14 tiles of the concealed part consist of 2 tiles each of 7 mutually different kinds (4 tiles of the same kind are not regarded as two pairs).
(4) Thirteen-orphans form: there are no melds, and the 14 tiles of the concealed part include all 13 kinds of yaochuu tiles and include no tile other than yaochuu tiles.
(5) Winning form: at least one of the following holds: there is at least one standard decomposition, it is a seven-pairs form, or it is a thirteen-orphans form.
(6) Waiting tiles: for each kind t, if the number of tiles of kind t in the hand and the melds is 3 or less, and the hand plus one tile of t, regarded as the concealed part, is a winning form, then t is a waiting tile.

* 4. Candidates and form strings
(1) Candidates: for each situation, make the following candidates.
  - Standard candidates: for each standard decomposition, the combination of that decomposition with one chosen block among its blocks (the pair and the sets of the concealed part) that contains the kind of the winning tile. The chosen block is called the winning block. When blocks of the same shape made of the same kinds (for example, two 234m) both contain the winning tile, only one candidate is made from them.
  - Seven-pairs candidate: one is made if the concealed part is a seven-pairs form.
  - Thirteen-orphans candidate: one is made if the concealed part is a thirteen-orphans form.
  One situation may have candidates of more than one type (for example, standard candidates and a seven-pairs candidate).
(2) Form string: for each candidate, the following strings listed with single spaces between them.
  - Standard candidate: "standard", the notation of the pair, the notations of the sets of the concealed part (the sets are sorted in kind order of the smallest kind of each set, and if that is the same, a triplet comes before a sequence), and the notation of each meld (in the order written in melds). The notation of a meld is the type letter followed by the block notation (e.g. "C456p", "K2222z"). A "*" is appended immediately after the notation of the winning block. When there are several blocks with the same notation, the "*" is appended to the leftmost of them.
  - Seven-pairs candidate: "chiitoitsu" and the notations of the 7 kinds of pairs of the concealed part (in kind order). A "*" is appended immediately after the notation of the pair of the winning tile's kind.
  - Thirteen-orphans candidate: "kokushi" and the notations of the 13 kinds of yaochuu tiles in the concealed part (in kind order). A kind with 2 tiles is written with its digit twice (e.g. "99s"), and the others with a single digit (e.g. "1m"). A "*" is appended immediately after the notation of the winning tile's kind.
  Examples: "standard 55m 234m 345p 456s 678s*", "standard 11m 789p 222s* P555z C123s", "kokushi 1m 9m 11p* 9p 1s 9s 1z 2z 3z 4z 5z 6z 7z".
(3) Candidate order: the candidates sorted in form-string order. String order is the lexicographic order by ASCII code (comparing from the start, the string whose first differing character has the smaller code comes first; if one string is a prefix of the other, the shorter one comes first). A space comes before "*". The form strings of the candidates of one situation are all different from each other.
(4) Wait shape: for each candidate, determined as follows.
  - Standard candidate: tanki if the winning block is the pair, shanpon if it is a triplet. If it is a sequence: kanchan if the winning tile is the middle number of that sequence; penchan if the sequence is 1, 2, 3 and the winning tile is 3, or the sequence is 7, 8, 9 and the winning tile is 7; otherwise ryanmen.
  - Seven-pairs candidate: tanki.
  - Thirteen-orphans candidate: thirteen if the hand (the 13 tiles excluding the winning tile) consists of one each of the 13 kinds of yaochuu tiles; otherwise single.

* 5. Yaku
For each candidate, determine the yaku that hold. The names of the yaku and their han (the han for a closed hand, the han for a hand with calls) are given in the following table. "-" means that the yaku does not hold for that hand. The order of this table is called "yaku order".
  riichi 1 -
  double_riichi 2 -
  ippatsu 1 -
  menzen_tsumo 1 -
  pinfu 1 -
  tanyao 1 1
  iipeikou 1 -
  haku 1 1
  hatsu 1 1
  chun 1 1
  jikaze 1 1
  bakaze 1 1
  haitei 1 1
  houtei 1 1
  rinshan 1 1
  chankan 1 1
  chiitoitsu 2 -
  toitoi 2 2
  sanankou 2 2
  sanshoku_doujun 2 1
  sanshoku_doukou 2 2
  ittsu 2 1
  chanta 2 1
  sankantsu 2 2
  shousangen 2 2
  honroutou 2 2
  honitsu 3 2
  junchan 3 2
  ryanpeikou 3 -
  chinitsu 6 5
(1) Terms (standard candidates).
  - The blocks of a candidate are the pair, the sets of the concealed part, and the melds. A meld of type C is regarded as a sequence, P as a triplet, and K and A as kans. Triplets and kans together are called triplet-like blocks, and the sequences of the concealed part together with the C melds are called sequence-like blocks.
  - Concealed triplet: the following triplet-like blocks. A triplet of the concealed part that is not the winning block. A triplet of the concealed part that is the winning block, when the win is by tsumo. A meld of type A. (Melds of type P and K, and a triplet that is the winning block of a ron win, are not concealed triplets.)
  - Yakuhai pair: the condition that the pair is a dragon tile, the seat wind tile or the round wind tile.
(2) For every candidate, the following yaku hold under the following conditions.
  - riichi, double_riichi, ippatsu, haitei, houtei, rinshan, chankan: there is a flag with the same name.
  - menzen_tsumo: the hand is closed, and the win is by tsumo.
  - tanyao: all tiles are simple tiles, and the hand is closed or the rule's open_tanyao is yes.
  - honroutou: all tiles are yaochuu tiles.
  - honitsu: the number tiles among all tiles are all of the same suit, and there is at least one honor tile (it does not hold if there is no number tile at all).
  - chinitsu: all tiles are number tiles of the same suit.
(3) For a seven-pairs candidate, chiitoitsu also holds.
(4) For a standard candidate, the following yaku also hold under the following conditions.
  - pinfu: the hand is closed, all sets of the concealed part are sequences and there are no melds, the pair is not a yakuhai pair, and the wait shape is ryanmen.
  - ryanpeikou: the hand is closed, and the sets of the concealed part are 4 sequences that can be divided into 2 groups of two identical sequences.
  - iipeikou: the hand is closed, ryanpeikou does not hold, and the concealed part has 2 or more identical sequences.
  - haku, hatsu, chun: there is a triplet-like block of 5z, 6z, 7z respectively.
  - jikaze: there is a triplet-like block of the seat wind tile. bakaze: there is a triplet-like block of the round wind tile (if the seat wind and the round wind are the same, both hold).
  - toitoi: there are 4 triplet-like blocks.
  - sanankou: there are exactly 3 concealed triplets.
  - sanshoku_doujun: there are sequence-like blocks of m, p and s with the same numbers (e.g. 234m, 234p, 234s).
  - sanshoku_doukou: there are triplet-like blocks of m, p and s with the same number.
  - ittsu: there are sequence-like blocks 1, 2, 3 and 4, 5, 6 and 7, 8, 9 of the same suit.
  - chanta, junchan: there is at least one sequence-like block, and every block contains a yaochuu tile. If all tiles include an honor tile, chanta holds; otherwise junchan holds.
  - sankantsu: there are exactly 3 kans.
  - shousangen: there are exactly 2 triplet-like blocks of dragon tiles, and the pair is a dragon tile.
(5) Sort the yaku that hold in yaku order, and let the han of each yaku be its closed-hand han for a closed hand and its with-calls han for a hand with calls. For a hand with calls, yaku whose han is "-" are removed (with the conditions of this table, such a yaku does not hold for a hand with calls anyway).

* 6. Yakuman
The names and multipliers of the yakuman are given in the following table. The multiplier is the value when the rule's double_yakuman is yes; when it is no, all multipliers are 1. The order of this table is called "yakuman order".
  tenhou 1
  chiihou 1
  kokushi 1
  kokushi_13 2
  suuankou 1
  suuankou_tanki 2
  daisangen 1
  shousuushii 1
  daisuushii 2
  tsuuiisou 1
  chinroutou 1
  ryuuiisou 1
  chuuren 1
  junsei_chuuren 2
  suukantsu 1
(1) For every candidate, the following yakuman hold under the following conditions.
  - tenhou, chiihou: there is a flag with the same name.
  - tsuuiisou: all tiles are honor tiles.
  - chinroutou: all tiles are terminal tiles.
  - ryuuiisou: all tiles are green tiles.
  - chuuren, junsei_chuuren: there are no melds, all tiles are number tiles of the same suit, and there are at least 3 tiles each of numbers 1 and 9 and at least 1 tile each of numbers 2 to 8 of that suit. If the numbers of tiles of numbers 1 to 9 in the hand (the 13 tiles excluding the winning tile) are, in this order, 3, 1, 1, 1, 1, 1, 1, 1, 3, junsei_chuuren holds; otherwise chuuren holds.
(2) For a thirteen-orphans candidate, kokushi_13 holds if the wait shape is thirteen, and kokushi holds if it is single.
(3) For a standard candidate, the following yakuman also hold under the following conditions (using the terms of section 5 (1)).
  - suuankou, suuankou_tanki: there are 4 concealed triplets. If the wait shape is tanki, suuankou_tanki holds; otherwise suuankou.
  - daisangen: there are 3 triplet-like blocks of dragon tiles.
  - daisuushii: there are 4 triplet-like blocks of wind tiles.
  - shousuushii: there are exactly 3 triplet-like blocks of wind tiles, and the pair is a wind tile.
  - suukantsu: there are 4 kans.
(4) A candidate for which at least one yakuman holds is called a yakuman candidate. For a yakuman candidate, the yakuman that hold are sorted in yakuman order, and the sum of their multipliers is called the "yakuman multiplier" (even when double_yakuman is no, the multipliers of several yakuman are added together). For a yakuman candidate, ordinary yaku and dora are not counted.

* 7. Dora
(1) The next tile of a dora indicator: for a number tile, the next number of the same suit (after 9 comes 1); for a wind tile, the next kind in the order 1z -> 2z -> 3z -> 4z -> 1z; for a dragon tile, the next kind in the order 5z -> 6z -> 7z -> 5z. An indicator that is a red five is treated as a 5.
(2) Dora count: for each dora indicator tile, count the tiles among all tiles that are of the same kind as the next tile of that indicator, and sum this over all indicators (if there are two indicators of the same kind, it is counted twice).
(3) Ura-dora count: counted in the same way as (2) for the ura-dora indicators.
(4) Red dora count: the number of red fives among all tiles.

* 8. Fu
The fu of a candidate that is not a yakuman candidate is determined as follows.
(1) For a standard candidate, the fu breakdown is the following items 1) to 7) listed in this order. An item whose value would be 0 (an item whose condition does not apply) is not listed.
  1) "base": 20
  2) "closed_ron": 10 if the hand is closed and the win is by ron
  3) "tsumo": 2 if the win is by tsumo and pinfu does not hold
  4) "pair <notation of the pair>": the sum of 2 if the pair is a dragon tile, 2 if it is the seat wind tile, and 2 if it is the round wind tile (a pair of a wind that is both the seat wind and the round wind gives 4)
  5) For each triplet-like block, in the order of the sets of the concealed part (in form-string order) followed by the melds (in the order of melds), "<type> <block notation>": the type is closed_triplet for a triplet that is a concealed triplet and open_triplet otherwise, and for a kan, closed_kan if it is a concealed triplet (a closed kan) and open_kan otherwise. The value is open_triplet 2, closed_triplet 4, open_kan 8, closed_kan 16 for a triplet-like block of simple tiles, and twice that (4, 8, 16, 32) for a triplet-like block of yaochuu tiles.
  6) "wait <wait shape>": 2 if the wait shape is one of kanchan, penchan, tanki
  7) "open_pinfu": 10 if the hand is not closed and the sum of the values of 1) to 6) is exactly 20
  The sum of the values of the breakdown is the fu total, and the fu total rounded up to a multiple of 10 (unchanged if it is already a multiple of 10) is the fu.
(2) For a seven-pairs candidate, the fu breakdown is the single item "chiitoitsu": 25, and both the fu total and the fu are 25 (no rounding up).

* 9. Evaluating a candidate
(1) Yakuman candidate: the category is yakuman, and the base points are 8000 * (yakuman multiplier).
(2) A candidate that is not a yakuman candidate and has none of the yaku of section 5: it is a no-yaku candidate, and its base points are 0.
(3) Any other candidate: the han count is the sum of the han of the yaku + the dora count + the red dora count + the ura-dora count. From the han count h and the fu f, the category and the base points are determined by checking the following in order.
  - h >= 13 and the rule's kazoe_yakuman is yes: category kazoe_yakuman, base points 8000
  - h >= 11: category sanbaiman, base points 6000
  - h >= 8: category baiman, base points 4000
  - h >= 6: category haneman, base points 3000
  - Otherwise, let b = f * 2^(h + 2); if any one of h = 5, b >= 2000, or the rule's kiriage is yes and b = 1920, holds, the category is mangan with base points 2000; otherwise the category is none with base points b.
The category order is none, mangan, haneman, baiman, sanbaiman, kazoe_yakuman, yakuman.

* 10. Result of a situation
For a situation without errors, the result is determined in the following order.
(1) Furiten: if the win is by ron and any kind in the discards is a waiting tile (section 3 (6)), the result is furiten (regardless of whether there are yaku).
(2) No yaku: if (1) does not apply and there is no candidate whose base points are 1 or more, the result is no yaku.
(3) Win: otherwise, the best candidate is chosen from the candidates whose base points are 1 or more by comparing in the following order, and the result is a win.
  1) larger base points
  2) a yakuman candidate is preferred over a candidate that is not a yakuman candidate
  3) larger han count
  4) larger fu
  5) form string earlier in string order
  3) and 4) are used only when comparing two candidates that are both not yakuman candidates.

* 11. Payments and point transfer
For a situation whose result is a win, let B be the base points of the best candidate. Rounding a value x up to a multiple of 100 means finding the smallest multiple of 100 that is >= x.
(1) Payments
  - Ron win: the discarder pays 6 * B for a dealer win, or 4 * B for a non-dealer win, rounded up to a multiple of 100.
  - Dealer tsumo win: each of the three non-dealers pays 2 * B rounded up to a multiple of 100.
  - Non-dealer tsumo win: the dealer pays 2 * B and each of the other two non-dealers pays B, each rounded up to a multiple of 100.
  The total of the payments is called the win points.
(2) Honba and deposits: let the honba points H be (honba count) * (the rule's honba), and the stick points S be (stick count) * 1000. In a ron win the discarder pays H, and in a tsumo win each of the three payers pays H / 3, in addition to the payments of (1). The winner receives all the points paid and S.
(3) Point transfer: the increase or decrease of points for each of the players with winds E, S, W, N. A paying player is given the total points paid as a negative value, the winner is given the total points received (win points + H + S) as a positive value, and every other player is given 0.

* 12. Shanten number
For a collection of 13 or 14 tiles without melds, the following three shanten numbers are defined.
(1) Standard shanten number: take some blocks of the following types out of the collection so that no two blocks share a tile (there may be tiles that are not used in any block).
  - Set: a triplet or a sequence (section 3 (1))
  - Pair: 2 tiles of the same kind. At most one can be taken.
  - Partial set: 2 tiles of the same kind, or 2 tiles of the same number suit whose numbers differ by 1 or 2 (e.g. 34m, 35m).
  With m, t and h the numbers of sets, partial sets and pairs taken, the value of that way of taking is 8 - 2 * m - min(t, 4 - m) - h. The minimum of the values over all ways of taking is the standard shanten number.
(2) Seven-pairs shanten number: 6 - (the number of kinds with 2 or more tiles) + max(0, 7 - (the number of kinds with 1 or more tiles)).
(3) Thirteen-orphans shanten number: 13 - (the number of kinds of yaochuu tiles with 1 or more tiles), minus a further 1 if there is a kind of yaochuu tile with 2 or more tiles.
(4) The shanten number of a collection of tiles is the minimum of (1) to (3). A collection of 14 tiles has shanten number -1 if and only if it is a winning form when regarded as the concealed part.

* 13. Analyzing a hand
For each hand of waits.txt, do the following.
(1) Check: check the following (a) to (c) in order, and the <message> of the first one that applies is the error of that hand. For a hand with an error, the only things done are writing the error to waits.txt and counting it in the statistics.
  (a) Any of the values of hand, dora, discards, visible is an invalid tile string ("-" for discards and visible is a correct value with no tiles): bad tile notation
  (b) The number of tiles of the hand is not 13: wrong tile count
  (c) For all tiles of the hand, the dora indicators, the discards and the visible tiles together, check (d1) to (d3) of section 2 (d) in order; the <message> of the first one that applies
(2) Shanten numbers: compute the standard, seven-pairs and thirteen-orphans shanten numbers of the hand and the shanten number s of the hand (section 12).
(3) Acceptance: in kind order, for each kind t, if the number of tiles of kind t in the hand is 3 or less and the shanten number of the 14 tiles made of the hand plus one tile of t is smaller than s, t is an acceptance tile. The remaining count of an acceptance tile t is 4 - (the total number of tiles of kind t in the hand, the dora indicators, the discards and the visible tiles).
(4) A hand with s = 0 is called a tenpai hand. The acceptance tiles of a tenpai hand are the tiles that make a winning form when added to the hand, and they are called waiting tiles. If any kind of the waiting tiles is in the discards, the hand is furiten.
(5) For a tenpai hand, compute the points of a ron win and of a tsumo win for each waiting tile.
  - For a furiten hand, the points of a ron win are not computed; it is furiten instead.
  - Otherwise, make and evaluate the candidates as in sections 3 to 9 for the following situation; if there is no candidate whose base points are 1 or more, it is no yaku; otherwise choose the best candidate in the order of section 10 (3) and compute the win points of section 11 (1). The situation is: the rules are the line's rules, the hand is the line's hand, there are no melds, the winning tile is one tile of that waiting tile's kind (a tile that is not a red five), the win method is a ron win or a tsumo win, the seat wind and the round wind are the line's seat and round, the flags are only riichi if riichi is yes and none if it is no, the dora indicators are the line's dora, and there are no ura-dora indicators. The checks of section 2 and the furiten decision of section 10 (1) are not performed.
  - For each of the ron win and the tsumo win, among the waiting tiles whose win points were computed (those that are neither furiten nor no yaku), the one with the largest points (if equal, the first in kind order) is the best waiting tile for that win method.

[Output files]
Write the following three files to output/. Every file consists only of ASCII characters, every line ends with a line feed (LF), and no line has trailing spaces. No empty lines are written. Text inside double quotes is written exactly as shown, and each part in < > is replaced by its value. Every separator is a single space. Integers are written in decimal without extra leading zeros and without "+" (only the values of the point transfer follow the rule given below).

* output/scores.txt (results of the winning situations)
For each situation of hands.txt, in situation order, output the following lines, and at the end output the statistics lines.
(1) "HAND <label>"
(2) For a situation with an error, output "ERROR <message>" and end the output of that situation.
(3) "WAITS <the tile names of the waiting tiles in kind order, separated by single spaces>"
(4) For each candidate, in candidate order, "CANDIDATE <form string> = <wait shape> <evaluation>". <evaluation> is as follows.
  - Yakuman candidate: "yakuman <yakuman multiplier> base <base points> yaku <the names of the yakuman that hold, in yakuman order, joined with ",">"
  - No-yaku candidate: "no_yaku"
  - Otherwise: "han <han count> fu <fu> base <base points> yaku <the names of the yaku that hold, in yaku order, joined with ",">"
(5) If the result is furiten, output "RESULT furiten", and if it is no yaku, output "RESULT no_yaku", and end the output of that situation.
(6) If the result is a win, output the following lines for the best candidate.
  - "FORM <form string>"
  - "WAIT <wait shape>"
  - For a yakuman candidate, for each yakuman in yakuman order, "YAKUMAN <name> <multiplier>", followed by "SCORE yakuman <yakuman multiplier> base <base points>"
  - For a candidate that is not a yakuman candidate, for each yaku in yaku order, "YAKU <name> <han>", followed by "DORA dora <dora count> aka <red dora count> ura <ura-dora count>", then for each item of the fu breakdown in order "FU <item> <value>", then "FU total <fu total> <fu>", then "SCORE han <han count> fu <fu> limit <category> base <base points>"
  - Payment: for a ron win "PAY ron <payment of the discarder>", for a dealer tsumo win "PAY tsumo all <payment of one player>", for a non-dealer tsumo win "PAY tsumo dealer <payment of the dealer> nondealer <payment of one non-dealer>" (none of these include the honba points)
  - "BONUS honba <H> sticks <S>"
  - "TOTAL points <win points> gain <win points + H + S>"
  - "DELTA E <change of E> S <change of S> W <change of W> N <change of N>". A positive change is written with "+", a negative change with "-", and 0 is written as "0".
(7) Statistics lines: after all situations, output the following lines in this order. Below, a "winning situation" is a situation whose result is a win, and it is counted by its best candidate.
  - "STATS hands <number of situations> wins <number of winning situations> furiten <number of furiten situations> no_yaku <number of no-yaku situations> errors <number of situations with errors>"
  - "WINS dealer_ron <count> dealer_tsumo <count> nondealer_ron <count> nondealer_tsumo <count>": the numbers of winning situations divided by dealer or non-dealer and by ron or tsumo.
  - "WAIT_COUNT ryanmen <count> kanchan <count> penchan <count> shanpon <count> tanki <count> single <count> thirteen <count>": the numbers of winning situations for each wait shape.
  - For each han count that appears among the winning situations that are not yakuman candidates, in increasing order, "HAN_COUNT <han count> <number of winning situations with that han count>"
  - For each fu that appears among the winning situations that are not yakuman candidates, in increasing order, "FU_COUNT <fu> <number of winning situations with that fu>"
  - For each category, in category order (7 lines), "LIMIT <category> <number of winning situations of that category>"
  - For each yaku in yaku order, followed by each yakuman in yakuman order, if there is at least one winning situation in which it holds, "YAKU_COUNT <name> <number of winning situations in which it holds>" (count what appears in the YAKU and YAKUMAN lines)
  - "POINTS total <sum of the win points> max <maximum of the win points> hand <label of the situation with the maximum>": if several situations have the maximum, the first in situation order; if there is no winning situation, "max 0 hand -".
  - For each rule setting, in rule order, "RULE <name> hands <number of situations with that rule> wins <number of winning situations with that rule> points <sum of the win points of that rule>". The number of situations includes the situations with errors.

* output/waits.txt (analysis of the hands)
For each hand of waits.txt, in file order, output the following lines, and at the end output the statistics lines.
(1) "HAND <label>"
(2) For a hand with an error, output "ERROR <message>" and end the output of that hand.
(3) "SHANTEN standard <standard shanten number> chiitoitsu <seven-pairs shanten number> kokushi <thirteen-orphans shanten number>"
(4) For a hand that is not tenpai (s >= 1), output one line made of "ACCEPT kinds <number of kinds of acceptance tiles> tiles <total of the remaining counts>" followed by " <tile name>:<remaining count>" for each acceptance tile in kind order, and end the output of that hand.
(5) For a tenpai hand, output the following lines.
  - "TENPAI kinds <number of kinds of waiting tiles> tiles <total of the remaining counts> furiten <yes if furiten, otherwise no>"
  - For each waiting tile, in kind order, "WAIT <tile name> left <remaining count> ron <ron> tsumo <tsumo>". <ron> and <tsumo> are the win points, or "no_yaku" if no yaku; <ron> of a furiten hand is "furiten".
  - "BEST ron <ron> tsumo <tsumo>". Each is "<tile name> <win points>" if there is a best waiting tile, otherwise "none".
(6) Statistics lines: after all hands, output the following lines in this order.
  - "STATS hands <number of hands> errors <number of hands with errors> tenpai <number of tenpai hands> furiten <number of furiten hands>"
  - For k = 0, 1, ..., 6 in this order (7 lines), "SHANTEN_COUNT <k> <number of hands without errors whose shanten number s is k>"

* output/summary.csv (list)
The first line is "hand,han,fu,points,yaku". It is followed by one line for each situation of hands.txt, in situation order, as follows.
  - Situation with an error: "<label>,-,-,0,ERROR"
  - Furiten: "<label>,-,-,0,FURITEN"
  - No yaku: "<label>,-,-,0,NO_YAKU"
  - Win with a yakuman candidate: "<label>,Y<yakuman multiplier>,-,<win points>,<the names of the yakuman in yakuman order joined with ";">"
  - Any other win: "<label>,<han count>,<fu>,<win points>,<the names of the yaku in yaku order joined with ";">"

[Source files]
Split the source code into the following 6 files (file names and extensions follow the conventions of each language).
- main: handles the overall flow and the core of the processing, and holds the largest share of the logic. It reads the three input files, checks the situations ((a) to (j) of section 2), creates the candidates and their form strings, determines the yaku and yakuman of each candidate (counting concealed triplets, triplet-like blocks and sequence-like blocks, and the condition of each yaku), counts the dora, evaluates the candidates, decides furiten, no yaku and the best candidate, computes the payments, honba, deposits and point transfer, runs the flow of the hand analysis (checking, acceptance, remaining counts, evaluation of each waiting tile of a tenpai hand, best waiting tiles), computes the statistics, and builds and writes every line of the three output files.
- tiles: handles parsing tile strings, conversion to kinds, building tile names and block notations, deciding yaochuu tiles, terminal tiles and honor tiles, the next tile of a dora indicator, and counting the tiles of each kind.
- decomposition: handles enumerating standard decompositions by backtracking, deciding the seven-pairs and thirteen-orphans forms, deciding winning forms, enumerating waiting tiles, and deciding the wait shape.
- yaku: handles the yaku table (names and closed/with-calls han), the yakuman table (names and multipliers), the list of flags, the list of category names, and building the sequences of (name, han) or (name, multiplier) from a collection of yaku that hold.
- scoring: handles the fu breakdown and its rounding up, the fu of triplet-like blocks, the category and base points from the han count and fu, rounding up to a multiple of 100, and computing the payments.
- shanten: handles computing the standard, seven-pairs and thirteen-orphans shanten numbers.

[Constraints]
- input/rules.txt has at least 1 and at most 26 lines. honba in rules.txt is a multiple of 3, at least 3 and at most 3000.
- input/hands.txt and input/waits.txt each have at least 1 and at most 1000 lines. Both are written exactly in the format of [Input files] (the number and order of items, the key names, rules being the name of a rule setting, the values of by, seat, from, round and riichi being one of the values given in the descriptions, the flags in flags being in the list and not repeated, each meld being at least one character long, and the labels differing on every line). However, the values written as tile strings and the type letters of melds may be wrong, and the combinations of items (the relationship between by, from and seat, the flags, the numbers of dora indicators and ura-dora indicators, sticks, etc.) may also be wrong (they are checked in section 2 and section 13 (1)).
- honba (the honba count) and sticks (the stick count) in hands.txt are at least 0 and at most 99.
- All values are integers, and in every computation (including the sums in the statistics) the absolute value is at most 10^8.
