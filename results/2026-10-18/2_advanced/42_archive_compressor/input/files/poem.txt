The river keeps its own slow time,
the river keeps its own slow rhyme;
it carries leaves, it carries light,
it carries morning into night.

The river keeps its own slow time,
the river keeps its own slow rhyme;
it carries stones, it carries sand,
it carries sky across the land.

And when the winter locks the hills,
the river waits, the river stills;
but under ice it hums the line:
the river keeps its own slow time.
