[Overview]
Write a program that performs a static analysis of a plane truss (a framework whose members are connected to each other by pins) by the direct stiffness method (stiffness matrix method). The program builds the stiffness matrix of the whole structure by adding up the stiffness matrices of the members, solves the stiffness equations, partitioned into the degrees of freedom fixed at the supports and the other degrees of freedom, by Cholesky factorization, and obtains, for each load case and each load combination, the nodal displacements, the member axial forces and stresses, and the support reactions. The nodes are renumbered by the reverse Cuthill-McKee method to reduce the bandwidth of the matrix, and when a pivot is too small during the factorization, the structure is judged to be unstable.
In addition, the program performs member checks against stress and buckling, a design iteration that reselects the member sections from a catalogue of sections, a deflection check and a verification by the principle of virtual work, a stability study in which members are removed one at a time, an evaluation of the maximum and minimum axial forces and of the load rating using influence lines and a moving load (a vehicle), a computation of natural frequencies and vibration modes by inverse iteration, and the creation of a deformed-shape drawing and a utilization map (diagrams drawn with characters).
The program reads 7 files in the input/ folder (nodes, members, section catalogue, materials, supports, loads, calculation settings) and writes 8 files to the output/ folder. It writes nothing to standard output. The only operations used in the calculation are the four arithmetic operations and the square root, and no random numbers are used.

[Input files]
The input files are the 7 files input/nodes.csv, input/members.csv, input/sections.csv, input/materials.csv, input/supports.csv, input/loads.txt and input/params.txt. Every file consists of ASCII characters only, and every line (including the last line) ends with a line feed (LF). There are no spaces at the beginning or end of a line, and there are no empty lines. The values within a line are separated by one comma in the files with the extension .csv (below, the CSV files), and by one space in loads.txt and params.txt. The first line of a CSV file is a header line, which is exactly the string shown below. The description below uses the following terms.
- Integer: a decimal integer of 0 or more without superfluous leading zeros.
- Real: a decimal number made of "-" (present only for a negative number), a string of one or more digits, and "." followed by a string of one or more digits (this part may be absent), in this order (examples: 0.000012, -8.0, 205000, 1.35).
- Name: a string of 1 to 12 characters consisting of letters and digits. Uppercase and lowercase letters are distinguished.
The units are mm for lengths and coordinates, N for forces (but the forces in loads.txt are in kN), and N/mm^2 for stresses and Young's modulus.

input/nodes.csv (nodes)
The header line is id,x,y. Each line from the second line on represents one node and consists of the node number (a positive integer), the x coordinate and the y coordinate (both integers, in mm), in this order. The x axis points to the right and the y axis points upward. The lines are in increasing order of node number. This line order is called the "node order", and the "original number" of the k-th node in node order (counted from 0) is k.

input/members.csv (members)
The header line is id,node_i,node_j,section,material,group. Each line represents one member and consists of the member number (a positive integer), the node number of the start node, the node number of the end node, the name of the section used initially, the name of the material and the name of the group, in this order. The lines are in increasing order of member number. This line order is called the "member order". In addition, the order of the group names by their first appearance in members.csv is called the "group order". In the design of section 10, the same section is chosen for the members of the same group.

input/sections.csv (section catalogue)
The header line is name,area,second_moment. Each line represents one section and consists of the section name, the cross-sectional area (a positive real, in mm^2) and the second moment of area (a positive real, in mm^4), in this order. This line order is called the "catalogue order".

input/materials.csv (materials)
The header line is name,young_modulus,yield_stress,density,expansion. Each line represents one material and consists of the material name, Young's modulus E (in N/mm^2), the yield stress fy (in N/mm^2), the density rho (in kg/m^3) and the coefficient of linear thermal expansion alpha (in 1/K), in this order. E, fy, rho and alpha are positive reals.

input/supports.csv (supports)
The header line is node,fix_x,fix_y,prescribed_dx,prescribed_dy. Each line represents one support and consists of the node number of the support, whether the x direction is fixed (1 means fixed, 0 means not fixed), whether the y direction is fixed (likewise 1 or 0), the prescribed displacement in the x direction and the prescribed displacement in the y direction (both reals, in mm), in this order. The prescribed displacement of a direction that is not fixed is 0.0. The lines are in increasing order of node number. This line order is called the "support order".

input/loads.txt (loads)
One or more load case blocks come first, followed by one or more COMBINATION lines.
A load case block starts with a line "LOADCASE <name>", continues with one or more load lines, and ends with a line "END". Each load line is one of the following 4 kinds.
  FORCE <node number> Fx Fy: the x component Fx and the y component Fy of a force applied to that node (both reals, in kN).
  TEMPERATURE <member number> dT: the temperature change dT of that member (a real, in K).
  SELFWEIGHT: the self weight of all members.
  SETTLEMENT: apply the prescribed displacements of supports.csv.
A COMBINATION line has the form "COMBINATION <name> <case name 1> <factor 1> <case name 2> <factor 2> ..." and represents a load combination. One or more pairs of a case name and a factor (a real) follow the name.
The order of the blocks is called the "load case order", and the order of the COMBINATION lines is called the "combination order". Load cases and combinations are collectively called "results", and the order that lists the load cases in load case order followed by the combinations in combination order is called the "result order".

input/params.txt (calculation settings)
The following lines appear in this order. There are one or more influence lines and one or more drawing lines, and lines of the same kind are consecutive.
- deck n_1 n_2 ... n_q: the node numbers n_1, ..., n_q (q >= 2) of the deck nodes (the deck is the line on which vehicles and loads are placed). They are in increasing order of x coordinate. This order is called the "deck order", and the x coordinate of n_k is written X_k.
- influence <member number>: a member whose influence line is written. The order of these lines is called the "influence line order".
- vehicle dp o_1 P_1 o_2 P_2 ... o_a P_a: the vehicle settings. dp is the interval by which the vehicle is moved (a positive integer, in mm). o_k is the distance from the first axle to the k-th axle (an integer, in mm), with o_1 = 0 < o_2 < ... < o_a. P_k is the load of that axle (a positive real, in kN). The order of this sequence is called the "axle order".
- rating <name> gammaD gammaL: the name of the result used as the dead load in the load rating, the dead load factor gammaD and the vehicle load factor gammaL (both positive reals).
- slenderness lambda_c lambda_t: the slenderness limits: lambda_c for a member under compression and lambda_t for the other members (both positive reals).
- deflection <name> r: the name of the combination used in the deflection check, and the ratio r that determines the deflection limit (a positive integer).
- vibration <name> nm T: the name of the load case converted into masses in the natural vibration calculation, the number nm of vibration modes to obtain, and the number T of iterations of the inverse iteration (both positive integers).
- removal <name>: the name of the combination used in the member removal study.
- drawing <name> mu wc hc: the name of the result whose deformed shape is drawn, the displacement magnification mu (a positive integer), and the horizontal length wc and the vertical length hc represented by one character of the drawing (both positive integers, in mm). The order of these lines is called the "drawing order".
- utilization_map wc hc: the horizontal length wc and the vertical length hc represented by one character of the utilization map (both positive integers, in mm).

[Processing]
Process as described in sections 0 to 17 below.

0. Calculation rules
- Evaluate every expression exactly as written. Parentheses are evaluated first, * and / are evaluated before + and -, and operations of the same precedence are evaluated from left to right. For example, rho * 9.81 * A * L / 1000000000 is evaluated by computing ((rho * 9.81) * A) * L and then dividing it by 1000000000. Even in operations between integers, division and operations involving a real are carried out in real arithmetic.
- Unless stated otherwise, a "sum" is computed by adding the terms one at a time from the left, in the order listed. That is, the second term is added to the first term, the third term is added to that result, and so on. A sum with no terms is 0. "Subtract b_1, b_2, ... from w in order" means computing w - b_1, then subtracting b_2 from that result, and so on.
- sqrt is the square root, |x| is the absolute value of x, floor(x) is the largest integer not greater than x (also when x is negative), and min and max are the minimum and the maximum. The circle ratio is pi = 3.141592653589793, and the gravitational acceleration is 9.81 (in m/s^2).
- Choosing the largest: when the largest (or the smallest) of several values is chosen, the values are compared after rounding each of them to 4 decimal places (the same values as those written in the output), and if several of them have the same rounded value, the first of them in the order stated at each place is chosen. From here on, every choice of "the largest" or "the smallest" follows this rule (the only exceptions are min and max in formulas, the larger of the two terms of the utilization in section 9, and the normalization of the mode shapes in section 16, which compare unrounded values). When the value of the chosen item is written out or used in later calculations or decisions, the value before rounding is used.

1. Member length and direction
Let (xi, yi) be the coordinates of the start node of a member and (xj, yj) the coordinates of its end node, let dx = xj - xi and dy = yj - yi (integers), and let the member length be L = sqrt(dx * dx + dy * dy) and the direction cosines be c = dx / L and s = dy / L (dx * dx + dy * dy may be computed as an integer).

2. Node renumbering (reverse Cuthill-McKee method)
- Two nodes connected by one member are said to be "adjacent" to each other. The degree of a node is the number of nodes adjacent to it.
- Build a sequence Q of nodes by the following procedure.
  (1) Let Q be the sequence consisting only of the node with the smallest degree (if there are several, the one with the smallest node number among them).
  (2) Examine the elements of Q one at a time from the front. For the node being examined, append its adjacent nodes that are not yet in Q to the end of Q, in increasing order of degree (in increasing order of node number for equal degrees). Finish after examining the last element of Q (the elements appended during the examination are also examined). In the given data, all nodes are in Q at this point.
  (3) Let S be the sequence Q in reverse order; the "new number" of the k-th node in S (counted from 0) is k.
- Bandwidth: for a numbering of the nodes, the maximum over all members of the absolute value of the difference between the numbers of the two end nodes of the member.
- Profile: for a numbering of the nodes, the total over all nodes v of the number of v minus the minimum of the numbers of v and of the nodes adjacent to v.
The bandwidth and the profile are computed both for the original numbers and for the new numbers.

3. Degrees of freedom
- The x-direction degree of freedom of the node with new number k has the number 2k, and its y-direction degree of freedom has the number 2k + 1. The total number of degrees of freedom is nd = 2 * (number of nodes). Below, the components of a vector of length nd and the rows and columns of a matrix with nd rows and nd columns are indexed by the degree-of-freedom numbers 0 to nd - 1.
- The degrees of freedom in the directions fixed at supports (the directions whose fix_x or fix_y is 1) are called "restrained degrees of freedom", and the other degrees of freedom are called "free degrees of freedom". Let r_1, r_2, ..., r_nr be the restrained degrees of freedom in increasing order of degree-of-freedom number, and f_1, f_2, ..., f_nf the free degrees of freedom in the same order.
- The degree of static indeterminacy is (number of members) + nr - 2 * (number of nodes).

4. Member properties and stiffness matrices
- The cross-sectional area and the second moment of area of the section of a member (initially the section in members.csv; it is reselected in section 10) are that member's A and I. The E, fy, rho and alpha of the member's material are that member's E, fy, rho and alpha.
- The axial stiffness of a member is k = E * A / L.
- Let a_1, a_2, a_3, a_4 be the degree-of-freedom numbers of the x direction and the y direction of the start node and of the x direction and the y direction of the end node of the member, in this order, and let t = (t_1, t_2, t_3, t_4) = (-c, -s, c, s).
- With kxx = k * (c * c), kxy = k * (c * s) and kyy = k * (s * s), the member stiffness matrix is the following matrix with 4 rows and 4 columns (-kxx and so on are the values kxx and so on with only the sign changed).
     kxx   kxy  -kxx  -kxy
     kxy   kyy  -kxy  -kyy
    -kxx  -kxy   kxx   kxy
    -kxy  -kyy   kxy   kyy
- The global stiffness matrix K (nd rows, nd columns) is built by setting all its elements to 0 and then, in member order, adding the element in row m, column n of each member's stiffness matrix to K[a_m][a_n] (m, n = 1 to 4).
- Kff is the matrix with nf rows and nf columns obtained by taking only the rows and columns of K for the free degrees of freedom: Kff[i][j] = K[f_i][f_j] (i, j = 1 to nf).

5. Load vectors
For each load case, start with vectors Fe (external forces) and Ft (forces equivalent to the temperature changes) of length nd whose components are all 0, and process the load lines of the block from top to bottom as follows.
- FORCE: add Fx * 1000 to Fe of the x-direction degree of freedom of that node, and then add Fy * 1000 to Fe of its y-direction degree of freedom (converting kN to N). If there are several FORCE lines for the same node, the values are added each time.
- TEMPERATURE: for that member, let P0 = E * A * alpha * dT, and add P0 * t_m to Ft[a_m] for m = 1, 2, 3, 4 in this order. This P0 is the "temperature force" of that member in that load case. The temperature force of a member without a TEMPERATURE line is 0.
- SELFWEIGHT: in member order, compute the weight of each member W = rho * 9.81 * A * L / 1000000000 (in N), subtract W / 2 from Fe of the y-direction degree of freedom of the start node, and then subtract W / 2 from Fe of the y-direction degree of freedom of the end node.
- SETTLEMENT: the load case becomes a "case with prescribed displacements".
After all lines have been processed, let F[d] = Fe[d] + Ft[d] for each degree of freedom d.

6. Solving the system of linear equations
- Cholesky factorization: factor Kff into a lower triangular matrix G. For i = 1, 2, ..., nf in this order, and for each i for j = 1, 2, ..., i in this order, do the following.
  Let w be the value obtained by subtracting G[i][h] * G[j][h] (h = 1, 2, ..., j - 1) from Kff[i][j] in increasing order of h.
  If j < i, let G[i][j] = w / G[j][j].
  If j = i: when w <= 0.000000001 * Kff[i][i], judge the structure "unstable", take the degree of freedom f_i as the unstable degree of freedom, and stop the factorization. Otherwise let G[i][i] = sqrt(w).
  (Omitting the terms whose G[i][h] is 0, for example outside the band, does not change the result.)
- Solution for b: for a vector b of length nf, for i = 1, 2, ..., nf in this order, let y_i be the value obtained by subtracting G[i][h] * y_h (h = 1, ..., i - 1) from b_i in increasing order of h and then dividing by G[i][i]. Next, for i = nf, nf - 1, ..., 1 in this order, let x_i be the value obtained by subtracting G[h][i] * x_h (h = i + 1, ..., nf) from y_i in increasing order of h and then dividing by G[i][i]. This x is called the solution for b.
- Displacement calculation: given a vector F of length nd and the displacements g_1, g_2, ..., g_nr of the restrained degrees of freedom, the displacement vector D (length nd, in mm) is obtained as follows. First let D[r_j] = g_j (j = 1 to nr). Next, let b_i be the value obtained by subtracting K[f_i][r_j] * g_j (j = 1, ..., nr) from F[f_i] in increasing order of j, and, for the solution x for b, let D[f_i] = x_i (i = 1 to nf).
- Reaction calculation: the reaction of a restrained degree of freedom r is the sum of K[r][d] * D[d] added for d = 0, 1, ..., nd - 1 in this order, minus F[r] (in N; it is the force that the support applies to the structure). The reaction vector R (length nd) has this value at the restrained degrees of freedom and 0 at the free degrees of freedom.
- Member axial force: for each member, let e = (D[a_3] - D[a_1]) * c + (D[a_4] - D[a_2]) * s, and let the axial force be N = k * e - P0 (P0 is the temperature force of that member) (in N; tension is positive).

7. Load case analysis
Once the set of sections (the sections of all members) is fixed, build K and Kff of section 4 and factor Kff by Cholesky factorization (in the given data, the structure with no member removed is never unstable). For each load case, obtain the following values.
- Displacements D: using F of section 5, with g_j equal to the prescribed displacement of the restrained degree of freedom r_j (the value in supports.csv) for a case with prescribed displacements, and g_j = 0 for the other cases, compute D as in section 6.
- Axial forces N and reaction vector R: compute them from that D and F as in section 6.
- Stress: for each member, sigma = N / A (in N/mm^2).
- External force vector: Fe of section 5.

8. Load combinations
The displacements D, the axial forces N, the reaction vector R and the external force vector Fe of a combination are obtained by superposing the same values of the load cases. For the pairs (case name 1, factor 1), (case name 2, factor 2), ... written on the COMBINATION line, each component is factor 1 * (value of case name 1) + factor 2 * (value of case name 2) + ..., added in the written order. The stress of a combination is also sigma = N / A. Below, for both load cases and combinations, these values are called the displacements, axial forces, stresses, reactions and external forces of that result.

9. Member checks
- Euler buckling load: for a second moment of area I', Pcr(I') = pi * pi * E * I' / (L * L) (in N), and Pcr = Pcr(I).
- Utilization: the utilization of a member with axial force N for a cross-sectional area A' and a second moment of area I' is the larger of |N / A'| / fy and max(0, -N) / Pcr(I'). The utilization with A' = A and I' = I is simply called the utilization of the member.
- Slenderness: the slenderness for A' and I' is L / sqrt(I' / A'). The slenderness with A' = A and I' = I is simply called the slenderness of the member.
- Slenderness limit: lambda_c if at least one of the member's axial forces for the combinations is negative (compression), and lambda_t if none of them is (decided without rounding).

10. Section design
Repeat the following for round = 1, 2, ..., 10 in this order. The set of sections for round = 1 is the sections in members.csv.
(1) Perform the calculations of sections 7 and 8 with the current set of sections.
(2) For each group in group order, examine the sections in catalogue order and choose the first section that satisfies the following condition. If no section satisfies the condition, choose the last section of the catalogue.
   Condition: for every member of the group, the slenderness for the section's cross-sectional area A' and second moment of area I' is at most that member's slenderness limit, and, for every combination, the utilization of that member's axial force in that combination for A' and I' is at most 1.
(3) A group whose chosen section differs from its current section is a "changed group". If there is no changed group, the iteration has "converged" and ends. If round = 10, the iteration ends even if there are changed groups (the chosen sections are not used in that case). Otherwise, replace the sections of the members of the changed groups by the chosen sections and proceed to the next round.
The set of sections when the iteration ends is called the "final sections", and the values obtained in (1) of the last round are called the "final results". Sections 11 to 17 all use the final sections and the final results.
In each round, also obtain the following values.
- Weight: the sum in member order of the member weights W (the formula of section 5) (in N).
- Maximum utilization: list, in member order and for each member in combination order, the utilization of that member in that combination; the largest of these, together with its member and combination.
- For each changed group: the current section, the chosen section, and the maximum of the utilizations of all members of the group in all combinations.

11. Summary of the final results
- Governing utilization: for each member, the largest of its utilizations for the combinations (chosen with the combinations listed in combination order), and that combination (the governing combination).
- Member verdict: ok if the governing utilization is at most 1 and the slenderness is at most the slenderness limit, and over otherwise.
- For each group: the number of members, and the largest of the governing utilizations of the members of the group (chosen with the members listed in member order) and that member.
- Ranking: the first 5 members (all members if there are fewer than 5) when the members are sorted in decreasing order of the governing utilization rounded to 4 decimal places (in member order when these are equal).
- Axial force range: for each member, the largest and the smallest of its axial forces for the combinations divided by 1000 (in kN) (chosen with the combinations listed in combination order), and the respective combinations.
- Section usage: in catalogue order, the number of members that use the section, and the sum in member order of the lengths L of those members.
- Summary per result: in result order, obtain the following values for each result.
  - Maximum displacement: for each node, compute the displacement magnitude sqrt(ux * ux + uy * uy) (ux and uy are the displacements of the x-direction and y-direction degrees of freedom of the node); the largest of these (chosen with the nodes listed in node order), and that node.
  - Strain energy: compute N * N * L / (2 * E * A) for each member, add these in member order, and divide the sum by 1000 (in J).
  - Total reactions: the value obtained by adding R of the x-direction degrees of freedom of the nodes in node order and dividing by 1000, and the value obtained in the same way for the y direction (in kN). The total external forces are obtained in the same way from Fe.
  - Equilibrium check: for each degree of freedom d let z_d = Fe[d] + R[d]; then, in member order, for each member add N * t'_m to z_(a_m) for m = 1, 2, 3, 4 in this order. Here t' = (c, s, -c, -s), and N * t'_m is the force that the member exerts on the node. The result is ok if the maximum of |z_d| is at most 0.000001 * max(1, maximum of |Fe[d]|, maximum of |R[d]|), and failed otherwise.
- Reaction range: in support order, for each support in the order x direction, y direction, for each fixed direction: the largest and the smallest of the reactions for the combinations (R of that degree of freedom divided by 1000, in kN) (chosen with the combinations listed in combination order), and the respective combinations. In the y direction, there is uplift if at least one of the reactions for the combinations is negative, and there is no uplift if none of them is (decided without rounding).
- Displacement range: for each node, the largest of its displacement magnitudes for the combinations (chosen with the combinations listed in combination order) and that combination.

12. Deflection check and virtual work verification
Use the displacements of the combination of the deflection line (in this section simply called the combination). List the deck nodes that are nodes of supports fixing the y direction, in deck order, and do the following for each pair of two adjacent nodes n_a, n_b in that list (n_a comes first in deck order). The part from n_a to n_b is called a "span".
- Deflection: for each deck node n_k that lies between n_a and n_b in deck order, compute delta_k = |v_k - (v_a + (v_b - v_a) * (X_k - X_a) / (X_b - X_a))|. v is the y-direction displacement of the node in the combination. The largest delta_k (chosen with the nodes listed in deck order) is the maximum deflection, and its node is nt.
- Limit: (X_b - X_a) / r. The span is ok if the maximum deflection (the unrounded value of the chosen delta_k) is at most the limit, and exceeded otherwise.
- Virtual work verification: with F the vector whose component at the y-direction degree of freedom of nt is -1000 and whose other components are 0, and with g_j = 0, compute the displacements and the axial force n of each member as in section 6 (with all temperature forces 0). Let delta_vw be the sum in member order of N * (n / 1000) * L / (E * A) computed for each member (N is the axial force in the combination), and let delta_st = -(the y-direction displacement of nt in the combination). The result is ok if |delta_vw - delta_st| <= 0.000001 * max(1, |delta_st|), and failed otherwise (since the load cases of the combination have no temperature changes and no prescribed displacements, delta_vw is the downward displacement of nt obtained by the unit load method, and it equals delta_st).

13. Study with one member removed
For each member in member order, consider the structure with that member removed (the node and degree-of-freedom numbers are not changed). Build K and Kff of section 4 without that member, and factor Kff by Cholesky factorization.
- If it is judged unstable, record the node of the unstable degree of freedom and its direction (x or y).
- Otherwise, compute the combination of the removal line and the load cases written in that combination as in sections 7 and 8, as if that member did not exist (the TEMPERATURE lines of the removed member are ignored, and SELFWEIGHT is processed only for the remaining members). Then obtain the largest of the utilizations of the remaining members in the combination (chosen with the members listed in member order) and that member, and the largest of the displacement magnitudes of the nodes in the combination (chosen with the nodes listed in node order) and that node.

14. Influence lines
For each deck node n_k in deck order, with F the vector whose component at the y-direction degree of freedom of n_k is -1000 (a downward force of 1 kN) and whose other components are 0, and with g_j = 0, compute the displacements, the axial force of each member (with all temperature forces 0) and the reaction vector as in section 6. The axial forces and the reactions, each divided by 1000, are called the "ordinates" of the axial forces and of the reactions for a downward unit load placed at n_k (in kN/kN). The ordinates of the axial force of a member are written eta_1, eta_2, ..., eta_q (eta_k is the value for the load placed at n_k). The ordinates of a reaction are written in the same way.
For each member of the influence lines, obtain the following values.
- The ordinates eta_1, ..., eta_q.
- The largest and the smallest eta (chosen with the nodes listed in deck order), and the respective nodes.
- The sum in deck order of the positive eta, and the sum in deck order of the negative eta (the sign is decided without rounding).
- Sign change positions: for k = 1, 2, ..., q - 1 in this order, if one of eta_k and eta_(k+1) is positive and the other is negative (decided without rounding; 0 is neither positive nor negative), compute X_k + (X_(k+1) - X_k) * eta_k / (eta_k - eta_(k+1)).

15. Vehicle effects and load rating
- Ordinate at position x: for the ordinates eta_1, ..., eta_q of the axial force of a member (or of the reaction of a restrained degree of freedom), the ordinate at position x is 0 if x < X_1 or x > X_q, eta_q if x = X_q, and otherwise eta_k + (eta_(k+1) - eta_k) * (x - X_k) / (X_(k+1) - X_k) for the k that satisfies X_k <= x < X_(k+1).
- Vehicle positions: let p = X_1 + j * dp for j = 0, 1, 2, ...; the vehicle positions are all such p that satisfy p <= X_q + o_a, in increasing order. When the vehicle is at position p, the k-th axle is at x = p - o_k.
- Effect: the effect on an axial force (or a reaction) when the vehicle is at position p is the sum of P_k * (ordinate at position p - o_k), added in axle order (in kN).
- For each member, obtain the largest and the smallest of the axial force effects over all vehicle positions (chosen with the positions listed in increasing order) and the respective positions. In support order, for each support in the order x direction, y direction, for each fixed direction, obtain the same for the reaction effects.
- Load rating: for each member, let Nd be the axial force of the result of the rating line divided by 1000, let T be the largest and C the smallest of the vehicle axial force effects, and let Pt = A * fy / 1000 and Pc = min(A * fy, Pcr) / 1000. If T rounded to 4 decimal places is positive, the tension rating factor is (Pt - gammaD * Nd) / (gammaL * T). If C rounded to 4 decimal places is negative, the compression rating factor is (Pc + gammaD * Nd) / (gammaL * (-C)). Otherwise, that rating factor does not exist.
- Minimum rating factor: list the existing rating factors in member order, for each member in the order tension, compression; the smallest of these, together with its member and its kind (tension or compression).

16. Natural vibration
- Mass: from the external forces Fe of the load case of the vibration line, for each node let m = -(Fe of the y-direction degree of freedom of the node) / 9.81 (in kg). The mass M_i of a free degree of freedom f_i is the m of the node of f_i. The total mass is the sum of m in node order.
- Inverse iteration: for the mode numbers 1, 2, ..., nm in this order, do the following for each mode. Set all components of a vector x of length nf to 1, and repeat the following (1) to (3) T times.
  (1) For each mode shape phi already obtained (in mode number order; phi is the unnormalized x defined below, not the normalized shape), let beta be the sum in order of i of phi_i * M_i * x_i, and replace x_i by x_i - beta * phi_i for each i.
  (2) Let b_i = M_i * x_i for each i, and let y be the solution for b (section 6).
  (3) Let nu be the square root of the sum in order of i of M_i * y_i * y_i, and let x_i = y_i / nu for each i.
  The x after the T repetitions is the mode shape phi of that mode.
- Frequency: with x the mode shape, let (Kx)_i be the sum in order of j of Kff[i][j] * x_j for each i, and let lambda = (sum in order of i of x_i * (Kx)_i) / (sum in order of i of M_i * x_i * x_i). The frequency is sqrt(1000 * lambda) / (2 * pi) (in Hz), and the period is 1 / frequency (in s).
- Mode shape normalization: let i* be the i with the largest |phi_i| (compared without rounding; in the given data it is unique); the component of a free degree of freedom f_i is phi_i / phi_(i*), and the component of a restrained degree of freedom is 0.

17. Character drawings
- Let wc and hc be the horizontal and vertical lengths of one character of the drawing, X0 and X1 the minimum and the maximum of the x coordinates of the nodes, and Y0 and Y1 the minimum and the maximum of their y coordinates.
- A drawing is a grid of characters with W = floor((X1 - X0) / wc + 0.5) + 5 characters across and H = floor((Y1 - Y0) / hc + 0.5) + 5 rows down, initially all spaces. The columns are numbered 0, 1, ..., W - 1 from the left, and the rows 0, 1, ..., H - 1 from the top.
- The position of a point (x, y) is the place at column floor((x - X0) / wc + 0.5) + 2 and row floor((Y1 - y) / hc + 0.5) + 2.
- To write a character at a position: if the position is inside the grid (0 <= column < W and 0 <= row < H), replace the character at that place; if it is outside the grid, do nothing.
- Line segment: a line segment from position (column c0, row r0) to position (column c1, row r1) is written by the following procedure (Bresenham's method). Let dc = |c1 - c0|, dr = -|r1 - r0|, sc = 1 if c0 < c1 and sc = -1 otherwise, sr = 1 if r0 < r1 and sr = -1 otherwise, and err = dc + dr, and, starting from (c, r) = (c0, r0), repeat the following. Write the character at (c, r). If (c, r) = (c1, r1), finish. Let e2 = 2 * err; if e2 >= dr, add dr to err and add sc to c; then (with the same e2), if e2 <= dc, add dc to err and add sr to r.
- Deformed-shape drawing: for each drawing line, using the displacements of that result, draw the following (1) to (4) in order. The position of a node is the point at the coordinates of the node, and the deformed position of a node is the point (x + mu * ux, y + mu * uy) (x and y are the coordinates of the node, and ux and uy are the x-direction and y-direction displacements of the node).
  (1) In member order, draw a line segment of . from the position of the start node to the position of the end node.
  (2) In node order, write + at the position of each node.
  (3) In member order, draw a line segment of * from the deformed position of the start node to the deformed position of the end node.
  (4) In node order, write o at the deformed position of each node.
- Utilization map: in member order, draw a line segment from the position of the start node to the position of the end node, using # if the governing utilization u of the member (the unrounded value) is 1 or more, and otherwise using the single digit that represents floor(10 * u). After that, in node order, write o at the position of each node.

[Source files]
Split the source code into the following 6 files. The names are language-neutral role names; follow the conventions of each language for the file names and extensions.
- main: handles the overall flow and contains most of the processing. It performs the node renumbering and the bandwidth and profile (section 2), the numbering of the degrees of freedom (section 3), the procedure of the analysis for a set of sections (calling the Cholesky factorization, the displacement calculation including prescribed displacements, and the calculation of the load cases and combinations: the displacement calculation of section 6, section 7 and section 8), the section design iteration (section 10), the summary of the final results (section 11), the deflection check and the virtual work verification (section 12), the member removal study (section 13), the influence lines, the vehicle effects and the load rating (sections 14 and 15), the natural vibration (section 16), the character drawings (section 17), the formatting of reals (including the handling of - for values whose rounded result is 0) and the "choosing the largest" comparison, and the assembly and writing of the lines of all output files.
- model_io: reads the 7 input files and converts them into the data of nodes, members, sections, materials, supports, load cases, combinations and calculation settings.
- assembly: holds the member lengths and direction cosines (section 1), the axial stiffness and the member stiffness matrix, the assembly of the global stiffness matrix (with the option of specifying a member to remove; section 4), and the assembly of the load vectors and the member weights (section 5).
- solver: holds the Cholesky factorization with the instability check, and the forward and backward substitution (the solution for b of section 6).
- postprocess: holds the member axial forces, the reactions, the maximum of z used in the equilibrium check, and the superposition for combinations (the reactions and axial forces of section 6, section 8, and the equilibrium check of section 11).
- design: holds the Euler buckling load, the utilization, the slenderness, the slenderness limit and the selection of a section for a group (section 9 and (2) of section 10).

[Constraints]
- There are 3 to 40 nodes and 3 to 80 members. Node numbers and member numbers are 1 to 999, and the node numbers are mutually distinct, as are the member numbers. 0 <= x <= 30000 and 0 <= y <= 30000, and no two nodes have the same coordinates.
- The two end nodes of a member are different and exist in nodes.csv. There is only one member connecting the same two nodes. All nodes are connected through members.
- There are 1 to 20 sections and 1 to 5 materials; the section names are mutually distinct, as are the material names. The cross-sectional areas of the catalogue increase in catalogue order. The sections and materials in members.csv exist in the catalogue and in materials.csv, and the members of the same group have the same initial section. There are 1 to 20 groups.
- There are 1 to 10 supports, and the support nodes exist in nodes.csv. For every support, at least one of fix_x and fix_y is 1.
- There are 1 to 10 load cases and 1 to 10 combinations, and the names of the load cases and of the combinations are all mutually distinct. A block has 1 to 100 load lines, and the nodes of the FORCE lines and the members of the TEMPERATURE lines exist. A block contains at most one TEMPERATURE line for the same member, at most one SELFWEIGHT line and at most one SETTLEMENT line. The case names in a COMBINATION line are names of load cases, and the same case name appears at most once in a line.
- The nodes of the deck line are mutually distinct, and X_1 < X_2 < ... < X_q. n_1 and n_q are nodes of supports fixing the y direction, and between any two nodes of supports fixing the y direction that are adjacent in deck order there is at least one deck node. The members of the influence lines exist. 1 <= a <= 6, o_a <= 30000, and the number of vehicle positions is at most 1000.
- The name of the rating line is the name of a result, the names of the deflection line and the removal line are names of combinations, the name of the vibration line is the name of a load case, and the names of the drawing lines are names of results. The load cases written in the combination of the deflection line have no TEMPERATURE lines and no SETTLEMENT lines. In the load case of the vibration line, m is positive for every node. 1 <= nm <= 3, nm <= nf, 1 <= T <= 500, and 1 <= mu <= 1000.
- The input files strictly follow the formats above. There is no need to check for input errors.
- The absolute values of all values that appear during integer calculations (coordinate differences, sums of their squares, vehicle positions and so on) are less than 2^31.
- The given input data satisfy the following.
  - In the check at each i of the Cholesky factorization, either w is greater than 0.000001 * Kff[i][i], or |w| is at most 0.000000000001 * Kff[i][i].
  - In the comparisons made without rounding (slenderness and limit, utilization and 1, axial force and 0, reaction and 0, deflection and limit, ordinate and 0, the |phi_i| with each other in the mode shape normalization, and the value inside floor(...) and integers), the difference between the two compared values is at least 10^-4 times the larger of their absolute values (in a comparison with 0, the absolute value of the value is at least 10^-4, and the value inside floor(...) is at least 10^-4 away from every integer). This excludes the cases in which a value is exactly 0 by the formula, such as the ordinates at support nodes on which no load is placed. In addition, in the equilibrium check and the virtual work verification, the maximum of |z_d| and |delta_vw - delta_st| are at most 1/1000 of their respective limits.
  - For every real written to the output, and for every value rounded in a "choosing the largest" comparison, the difference between the value and a rounding boundary (the value exactly midway between two adjacent numbers at the number of digits written) is at least 10^-9.
  Therefore, differences in the order of additions, or differences in the last digit caused by fused multiply-add, do not change the results. Nevertheless, perform the calculations with the formulas and in the order defined in this statement.
