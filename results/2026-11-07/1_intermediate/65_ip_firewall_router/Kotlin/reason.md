This two-UDP-packet case meets the shared question with fewer state-transition and configuration paths: UDP needs no SYN/FIN state machine; NAT PAT supplies the outside role; direct DEV routing needs no next-hop resolution. The selected post-ACL allocation/reuse and timestamp write sites prove both no-create and no-refresh even though the concrete table is empty.
