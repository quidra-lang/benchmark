## Evidence and language semantics

The change is confined to fare eligibility state while traversing the already-selected journey. Keep a distinct integer-minute first-boarding value that is initially absent. On the first ride, remember its boarding time and apply no discount. On every later ride, compare that ride's boarding time with the same first-boarding value using <= W. Do not update the anchor to an alighting time, a later boarding time, or the end of a walk/stay. Non-ride legs remain free and do not initialize or reset it. Do not treat minute zero as absence; retain an explicit presence test or a sentinel outside the valid time range. The earliest-arrival search, timetable readiness, direction ordering, strict label improvement and tie resolution remain unchanged; fares are computed after the route has been selected. The distinct-zone count includes both endpoints and intervening stops, and tariff lookup still caps at the final price. Child price and child discount cap continue to use ceil(amount / 20) * 10 separately; the discount remains min(cap, price), and pay remains price minus discount. Keep time values separate from fare amounts even where the host uses the same numeric type. For a concrete valid source trace, register A/B/C/D/E in zones 1/2/2/3/3 with their default zero transfer times; register L1 from A to B in 5 minutes with first=last=08:00, L2 from C to D in 2 minutes with first=last=08:10, and L3 from D to E in 5 minutes with first=last=08:15 (headway 1 for each), plus WALK B C 2 and TARIFF 50 10 200 250. FARE A E 08:00 ADULT and FARE A E 08:00 CHILD select L1 08:00-08:05, walk B-C 08:05-08:07, L2 08:10-08:12 and L3 08:15-08:20. The second boarding is exactly W=10 minutes after the first and qualifies; the third is 15 minutes after the first and does not, although its gap from the previous alighting is only 3 minutes. Zone counts are 2, 2, 1. The proposed adult tickets have prices 250/250/200, discounts 0/50/0 and payments 250/200/200, total 650; child prices are 130/130/100, discounts 0/30/0 and payments 130/100/100, total 330. The current previous-alighting rule also discounts the third ride, giving totals 600 and 300. A valid walk-only FARE B C 08:05 ADULT produces zero tickets and total zero under either rule; the absent first ride must never be dereferenced. These are deductions from the selected source, not executed tests or a modified implementation.

Ride and Walk inherit Leg, and the isa Ride filter excludes non-rides before any anchor access. Label.journey and Direction/traintime establish the ordered timestamps.
