3. Establish the tick order: expiry/abandonment precedes same-tick input events, which precede car actions. At deadline 1 only the waiting person is lost, its assigned call is removed, and WHERE still sees the riding portion.
4. Explain why E2 cannot board at the deadline despite being at the landing, and why the E1 passenger remains aboard and is delivered at tick 3. Follow door, direction, movement and termination through END at tick 4.
5. Derive the event log, per-group/per-car/floor/summary output and the single scenario ranking/policy aggregate, distinguishing fully boarded/delivered group fields from delivered-passenger averages and abandonment counts.

## Evidence and language semantics

The shared concrete input is 1; SCENARIO deadline; FLOORS 3; CARS 2; CAPACITY 1; DOOR 1; START 1 2; POLICY ROUND; PATIENCE 1; 0 CALL 1 3 2; 1 WHERE 1; END (one item per line). At tick 0 E1 receives G1, opens and boards one person, logs LEFT x1, and reassigns the remainder to E2; E2 moves from floor 2 to 1. Tick 1 begins with ABANDON G1 x1, before WHERE and either car action. WHERE therefore reports waiting=0 car=- riding=E1x1 delivered=0 lost=1; E2 logs IDLE 1 instead of opening. E1 closes/moves at ticks 1 and 2, opens/alights at tick 3 and becomes idle at tick 4, then END is logged. G1 has delivered=1 lost=1 wait=- travel=- cars=1. E1 has distance=2 stops=2 boarded=1 busy=4 util=80.0% energy=12; E2 has distance=1 stops=0 boarded=0 busy=1 util=20.0% energy=1. F1 has groups=1 passengers=2 delivered=1 avg_wait=0.00; summary has delivered=1 abandoned=1 evacuated=0 avg_wait=0.00 avg_travel=3.00 max_wait=0 end=4. The scenario is ranked first and ROUND has one run with the same averages. Go zero values establish empty slices, zero counters and inactive fire/home fields; pointers in groups and cars preserve shared mutable state. Passenger stores a numeric group index and independent count/boarding time. Removing a waiting call by replacing a calls slice therefore cannot remove a passenger. assign excludes the original car after a partial board, and ordered car slices with the ROUND cursor select E1 then E2 without evaluating cost. The boarding IDs are materialized before assignment mutates calls. The selected map-to-sorted-keys helper and numeric floor loop make output order deterministic; integer division and fmt fields establish delivered-only statistics.

## Minimization and alternatives

The same WHERE scenario is used in every language. WHERE was compared with CANCEL: it directly exposes the riding/waiting distinction and avoids the additional cancellation-success/error path. ROUND avoids cost, ETA and nearest-policy dependencies. Two cars with capacity one are necessary here to make a partial group and a physically available second car at the deadline. Select only the reached configuration/event arms and their bounds, the tick scheduler, waiting-call removal, partial reassignment, ordinary car movement/boarding/delivery, and output aggregations. OUT/IN/HOLD/FIRE/RESET/CANCEL/STATUS, parking movement, comparison reruns, period reports and other policy implementations are not needed. Their defaults/guards establish that they are inactive; ordinary state is retained where a guard depends on it. For a single ranked run the comparator implementation adds no ordering fact. Each retained line supplies a state, validation, call relationship, transition, formatting or aggregation fact; empty lines and standalone closing delimiters supply no additional fact. The final excerpt alone was independently checked against all five common requirements, including preservation of the rider and delivered-only average denominators.
