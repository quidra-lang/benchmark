## Evidence and language semantics

The shared concrete input is 1; SCENARIO deadline; FLOORS 3; CARS 2; CAPACITY 1; DOOR 1; START 1 2; POLICY ROUND; PATIENCE 1; 0 CALL 1 3 2; 1 WHERE 1; END (one item per line). At tick 0 E1 receives G1, opens and boards one person, logs LEFT x1, and reassigns the remainder to E2; E2 moves from floor 2 to 1. Tick 1 begins with ABANDON G1 x1, before WHERE and either car action. WHERE therefore reports waiting=0 car=- riding=E1x1 delivered=0 lost=1; E2 logs IDLE 1 instead of opening. E1 closes/moves at ticks 1 and 2, opens/alights at tick 3 and becomes idle at tick 4, then END is logged. G1 has delivered=1 lost=1 wait=- travel=- cars=1. E1 has distance=2 stops=2 boarded=1 busy=4 util=80.0% energy=12; E2 has distance=1 stops=0 boarded=0 busy=1 util=20.0% energy=1. F1 has groups=1 passengers=2 delivered=1 avg_wait=0.00; summary has delivered=1 abandoned=1 evacuated=0 avg_wait=0.00 avg_travel=3.00 max_wait=0 end=4. The scenario is ranked first and ROUND has one run with the same averages. TypeScript structural interfaces are erased and do not freeze objects; actual object initializers and numeric group indices provide the state evidence. The complete JS/TS source correspondence was inspected, including every typed replacement, then the TypeScript excerpt was reviewed independently. The passenger count/time records stay in car.passengers while remove updates calls/group.car and abandon updates only the waiting/lost counters. The selected cost early return is relevant to the unconditionally evaluated chosenCost for initially idle cars, proving its read-only behavior. Static number annotations, Array/Set/Map semantics, ordered forEach, and Math.floor justify indexing, reassignment and statistics; redundant interface declarations add no behavior beyond the selected initializers.

## Minimization and alternatives

The same WHERE scenario is used in every language. WHERE was compared with CANCEL: it directly exposes the riding/waiting distinction and avoids the additional cancellation-success/error path. ROUND avoids cost, ETA and nearest-policy dependencies. Two cars with capacity one are necessary here to make a partial group and a physically available second car at the deadline. Select only the reached configuration/event arms and their bounds, the tick scheduler, waiting-call removal, partial reassignment, ordinary car movement/boarding/delivery, and output aggregations. OUT/IN/HOLD/FIRE/RESET/CANCEL/STATUS, parking movement, comparison reruns, period reports and other policy implementations are not needed. Their defaults/guards establish that they are inactive; ordinary state is retained where a guard depends on it. For a single ranked run the comparator implementation adds no ordering fact. Each retained line supplies a state, validation, call relationship, transition, formatting or aggregation fact; necessary internal control and callback boundaries are retained, while redundant outer declaration frames and blank lines are omitted. The final excerpt alone was independently checked against all five common requirements, including preservation of the rider and delivered-only average denominators. Internal loop, branch, switch-arm and callback endings explicitly separate expiry from same-tick events, car actions from termination, and repeated contributions from later state/output effects. Where a closing line also begins an adjacent arm, the complete original line and its necessary following boundary are retained. These boundaries do not add a new scenario, command implementation, or answer requirement. Local helper-definition endings are retained where subsequent simulation, validation or report statements execute in the enclosing routine. This prevents those statements from appearing to belong to the helper body; unrelated outer method/class framing is still omitted.
