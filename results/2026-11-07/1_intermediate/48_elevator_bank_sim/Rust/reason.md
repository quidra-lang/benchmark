## Evidence and language semantics

The shared concrete input is 1; SCENARIO deadline; FLOORS 3; CARS 2; CAPACITY 1; DOOR 1; START 1 2; POLICY ROUND; PATIENCE 1; 0 CALL 1 3 2; 1 WHERE 1; END (one item per line). At tick 0 E1 receives G1, opens and boards one person, logs LEFT x1, and reassigns the remainder to E2; E2 moves from floor 2 to 1. Tick 1 begins with ABANDON G1 x1, before WHERE and either car action. WHERE therefore reports waiting=0 car=- riding=E1x1 delivered=0 lost=1; E2 logs IDLE 1 instead of opening. E1 closes/moves at ticks 1 and 2, opens/alights at tick 3 and becomes idle at tick 4, then END is logged. G1 has delivered=1 lost=1 wait=- travel=- cars=1. E1 has distance=2 stops=2 boarded=1 busy=4 util=80.0% energy=12; E2 has distance=1 stops=0 boarded=0 busy=1 util=20.0% energy=1. F1 has groups=1 passengers=2 delivered=1 avg_wait=0.00; summary has delivered=1 abandoned=1 evacuated=0 avg_wait=0.00 avg_travel=3.00 max_wait=0 end=4. The scenario is ranked first and ROUND has one run with the same averages. Owned Rust structs separate Group, Car.calls IDs and Passenger records; Option explicitly distinguishes no waiting assignment from a car. retain operates only on the calls/pending vector selected by walk; the separately owned passenger vector and its count/time survive. mem::take moves passenger records out only during door service, reinserting those not delivered or evacuated. An immutable boarding Vec snapshot permits later mutable reassignment; ROUND selects ordered indices and does not evaluate cost. Config/event clones are owned copies, and BTreeSet iteration supplies deterministic sorted car/floor output. Integer arithmetic and the actual format operations establish the totals. With one ranked scenario, sort comparison details cannot affect output.

## Minimization and alternatives

The same WHERE scenario is used in every language. WHERE was compared with CANCEL: it directly exposes the riding/waiting distinction and avoids the additional cancellation-success/error path. ROUND avoids cost, ETA and nearest-policy dependencies. Two cars with capacity one are necessary here to make a partial group and a physically available second car at the deadline. Select only the reached configuration/event arms and their bounds, the tick scheduler, waiting-call removal, partial reassignment, ordinary car movement/boarding/delivery, and output aggregations. OUT/IN/HOLD/FIRE/RESET/CANCEL/STATUS, parking movement, comparison reruns, period reports and other policy implementations are not needed. Their defaults/guards establish that they are inactive; ordinary state is retained where a guard depends on it. For a single ranked run the comparator implementation adds no ordering fact. Each retained line supplies a state, validation, call relationship, transition, formatting or aggregation fact; empty lines and standalone closing delimiters supply no additional fact. The final excerpt alone was independently checked against all five common requirements, including preservation of the rider and delivered-only average denominators.
