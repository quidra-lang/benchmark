## Evidence and language semantics

matchEntire enforces the complete number-pair syntax. Nullable capture defaults only an omitted count to1;0 remains0. Header supplies old/new LineRange values and fresh body. parsePatch independently counts both sides and separates header rejection, missing required body, invalid/overflowing body and no changes. A range X means startX,count1; X,0 means startX,count0 and consumes no line on that side. Thus0 alone is invalid, while0,0 is allowed by the range parser. Space-prefixed context contributes to both counts, minus only old and plus only new; parsing continues until both declared counts are met. EOF while either remains produces truncated hunk at the original header line; a malformed range/delimiter produces bad hunk header at that header, and an encountered invalid or overflowing body line produces bad hunk line at its own line. A new @@ line encountered before the body counts are met is a bad hunk line rather than EOF truncation. A body with no additions/deletions, including both counts zero, fails no changes. Hunk application, matching offsets/fuzz, document mutation and diff generation are outside this parse operation and were excluded.
