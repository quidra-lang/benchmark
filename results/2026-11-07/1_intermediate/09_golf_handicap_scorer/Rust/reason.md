## Evidence and language semantics

The input loop and split_whitespace dispatcher route the selected commands. number parses i32; Option and explicit empty initialization establish the index/calculation missing states. Selected derived Clone structs expose value snapshots: player/course getters clone their records, ROUND constructs a Round containing previous_index, previous_records and a clone of previous_calculation, then gets the real player mutably for append/update. counting copies eligible values before sorting by (adjusted,-round). Historical low maps (day,index) to (index,day) before min. REMOVE pops the actual latest round, truncates records and restores the owned optional calculation. Known Rust ownership/Clone, arrays, Option/Result, iterator sum/min and formatting semantics supply these relationships; no hypothetical compilation investigation is part of this selection.

Independent excerpt-only check: register a course c with eighteen par-4 holes and stroke indexes 1..18, tee t with rating 720 and slope 113, and player p with index 180. Record all-5 rounds on days 1, 2 and 3. Each has AGS 90 and differential 180 (18.0); none is exceptional. The first two rounds retain index 180 and have no calculation; each appends (day,180). The third selects the newest tied differential, averages 180 and applies -20, giving index 160 (16.0), low 180, and calculation k=1/average=180/adjustment=-20 with no caps. INDEX reports rounds 3 and that calculation; HISTORY has three 18.0 differentials with only round 3 starred; LOWINDEX at day 4 is 16.0 on day 3. REMOVE restores index 180, the absent calculation and record length 2. The next INDEX is 18.0/rounds 2 without calculation, HISTORY has two unstarred entries, and LOWINDEX at day 4 is 18.0 on day 1. The same selected capture/restore paths also preserve an absent initial index or an earlier nonempty calculation. Restoring only scores cannot reconstruct the registration-time index, dated cap history, or the saved calculation context by a fresh single update. This check used no implementation facts outside the excerpt and did not execute the program.
