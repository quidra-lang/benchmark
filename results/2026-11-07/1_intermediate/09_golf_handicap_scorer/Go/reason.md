## Evidence and language semantics

The scanner input and strings.Fields dispatcher separate command and args; numeric helper Atoi and the relevant switch branches validate and register the data. Positional Tee/Course/IndexRecord/Calculation/Eligible struct fields identify aggregate values. A keyed Player literal leaves its slices nil/empty and Calculation nil by Go zero-value semantics. newRound captures Previous, PreviousRecords and PreviousCalculation before updating. Index and Calculation pointers are replaced by freshly allocated values in updateIndex, preserving saved pointers; REMOVE restores them and reslices records. counting copies its slice before sorting, and lowIndex compares index then date within the half-open window. The COURSEHCP body sharing a branch with ROUND is omitted, but its distinguishing condition/else remain to establish the ROUND route. fmt, scanner, sort and append/slice semantics need no library implementation.

Independent excerpt-only check: register a course c with eighteen par-4 holes and stroke indexes 1..18, tee t with rating 720 and slope 113, and player p with index 180. Record all-5 rounds on days 1, 2 and 3. Each has AGS 90 and differential 180 (18.0); none is exceptional. The first two rounds retain index 180 and have no calculation; each appends (day,180). The third selects the newest tied differential, averages 180 and applies -20, giving index 160 (16.0), low 180, and calculation k=1/average=180/adjustment=-20 with no caps. INDEX reports rounds 3 and that calculation; HISTORY has three 18.0 differentials with only round 3 starred; LOWINDEX at day 4 is 16.0 on day 3. REMOVE restores index 180, the absent calculation and record length 2. The next INDEX is 18.0/rounds 2 without calculation, HISTORY has two unstarred entries, and LOWINDEX at day 4 is 18.0 on day 1. The same selected capture/restore paths also preserve an absent initial index or an earlier nonempty calculation. Restoring only scores cannot reconstruct the registration-time index, dated cap history, or the saved calculation context by a fresh single update.
