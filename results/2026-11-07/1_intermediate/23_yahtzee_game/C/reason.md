## Evidence and language semantics

Common valid input has 10 commands: GAME 1 A; LOAD 1 2 3 4 5 6 6 6 6 6 2 2 2 2 2; ROLL; SCORE ONES; UNDO; REDO; ROLL; UNDO; HOLD 00000; ROLL. Each semicolon separates a line. The first roll is 1 2 3 4 5, ONES scores 1 (both bonuses remain zero), and scoring starts round 2. UNDO reopens ONES and restores round-1 dice/roll state; REDO re-enters the same score and restores the saved next-turn state. The next roll consumes the five sixes. Undoing the entry again restores the original round-1 hand but leaves the queue consumed; HOLD 00000 clears redo, and the final roll 2 uses the five twos. The face counters reflect all 15 consumed faces. C struct assignment copies embedded fixed arrays: ScoreEntry.before and RedoEntry.after therefore include independent face/hold/roll state. Game and Table calloc initialization establishes zero counters, bonuses and empty histories; the FORCED enum is zero, and explicit score initialization marks open boxes -1. Restoring one score/bonus and Turn leaves loaded_head/tail, random_state and face_counts untouched. const array input and index-specific candidate writes support retaining only the upper-category return from normal_score. The successful roll/hold clear history only after boolean validation succeeds. The fail buffer, strtoll/name/pattern/category parsing, command returns and printf path establish all accepted outputs and rejected-command ordering.

The bonus calculation and stored bonus delta are retained even though this trace has zero bonus. Loaded faces avoid depending on a long RNG arithmetic trace, but the generator/queue split and independent counters remain explicit.
