## Evidence and language semantics

Common valid input has 10 commands: GAME 1 A; LOAD 1 2 3 4 5 6 6 6 6 6 2 2 2 2 2; ROLL; SCORE ONES; UNDO; REDO; ROLL; UNDO; HOLD 00000; ROLL. Each semicolon separates a line. The first roll is 1 2 3 4 5, ONES scores 1 (both bonuses remain zero), and scoring starts round 2. UNDO reopens ONES and restores round-1 dice/roll state; REDO re-enters the same score and restores the saved next-turn state. The next roll consumes the five sixes. Undoing the entry again restores the original round-1 hand but leaves the queue consumed; HOLD 00000 clears redo, and the final roll 2 uses the five twos. The face counters reflect all 15 consumed faces. J arrays and boxed nouns are values: entry =. ...Dice;Holds... and turn_snapshot retain independent dice/hold values when global nouns are later replaced by amend/slice operations. restore_turn explicitly binds only Current, RollCount, Dice and Holds; Cards and YBonuses are separately amended, while Queue, RandomState and FaceCounts remain advanced. The broader simulation-only snapshot/restore verbs are not on this trace and are omitted. The candidate vector constructor is included to establish upper-slot indexing; the no-joker branch preserves ONES. Explicit _1 unused boxes and derived totals/round show score restoration. Successful roll/hold replace Redo only after validation; fail/throw/catcht, token boxing and custom number/join/emit helpers establish the visible trace.

## Minimization and alternatives

A single first-turn upper-category entry is the smallest trace that covers score insertion, used-box and bonus state, turn advancement, undo/redo restoration and later dice consumption. Choosing ONES avoids unrelated lower-category scoring; no bot, advice, simulation, records or end-of-game path is needed. The bonus calculation and stored bonus delta are retained even though this trace has zero bonus. Loaded faces avoid depending on a long RNG arithmetic trace, but the generator/queue split and independent counters remain explicit.
