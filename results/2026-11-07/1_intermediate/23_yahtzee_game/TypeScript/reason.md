## Evidence and language semantics

Common valid input has 10 commands: GAME 1 A; LOAD 1 2 3 4 5 6 6 6 6 6 2 2 2 2 2; ROLL; SCORE ONES; UNDO; REDO; ROLL; UNDO; HOLD 00000; ROLL. Each semicolon separates a line. The first roll is 1 2 3 4 5, ONES scores 1 (both bonuses remain zero), and scoring starts round 2. UNDO reopens ONES and restores round-1 dice/roll state; REDO re-enters the same score and restores the saved next-turn state. The next roll consumes the five sixes. Undoing the entry again restores the original round-1 hand but leaves the queue consumed; HOLD 00000 clears redo, and the final roll 2 uses the five twos. The face counters reflect all 15 consumed faces. Typed nullable boxes/turns make empty categories and finished-turn representation explicit, but TypeScript types do not deep-copy mutable arrays. The selected Turn spread copies and copy method provide that required runtime evidence. Entry retains Player identity, and redo stores a Turn/Entry pair; derived upper bonus and round follow restored box occupancy. DiceRoller is a separate object whose loaded shift/RNG/count mutation is absent from history restoration. Table assertions run before roll/hold mutations and redo clearing. The supported bounded Number arithmetic and standard Array methods suffice without their implementations; dynamic handler.call and output accumulation close the command route.

## Minimization and alternatives

A single first-turn upper-category entry is the smallest trace that covers score insertion, used-box and bonus state, turn advancement, undo/redo restoration and later dice consumption. Choosing ONES avoids unrelated lower-category scoring; no bot, advice, simulation, records or end-of-game path is needed. The bonus calculation and stored bonus delta are retained even though this trace has zero bonus. Loaded faces avoid depending on a long RNG arithmetic trace, but the generator/queue split and independent counters remain explicit. Full score/undo/redo mutation routes and the selected snapshot or copy implementation establish what is restored; the omitted source is not used to claim hidden mutation absence. Parser, rule defaults, successful validation and output helpers close the trace.
