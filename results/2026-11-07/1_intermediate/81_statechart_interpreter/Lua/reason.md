## Minimization and alternatives

A single atomic state and targetless transitions minimize state-entry/exit/history dependencies while still exercising parsing, ordered transition priority, default guards, conflict filtering, action logs and dump output. A chart with guarded arithmetic or changing targets would require expression evaluation and target/history closure without adding a fact needed to locate this matching change. Queue/completion creation and separate timer/eventless dispatch anchors are retained to delimit the modification; their scheduling algorithms are not changed. Only the successful branches and custom helpers needed for this trace are retained, plus the match alternatives that distinguish old/new behavior. Each selected complete line was reviewed for necessity; the excerpt alone was checked against the common requirements. Independent function/method outer frames and unrelated commands are omitted; internal branch, loop and local-helper boundaries needed to interpret the selected trace are retained. This boundary repair preserves every prior selected line and all five shared requirements; it does not add unrelated expression, scheduling or state-history implementations.
