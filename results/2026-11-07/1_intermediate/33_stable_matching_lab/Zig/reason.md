## Evidence and language semantics

Matching defaults to null partners. Remaining is initialized once and applicants resets to empty sequences each round. append/index implement actual per-round membership, and receiver preference scanning admits only listed applicants with free capacity; proposals is explicitly set to applications. Acceptance writes a proposer's partner and consumes one remaining slot. Already accepted proposers never enter a subsequent round's applicant set, and the receiver considers only that fresh set, so earlier acceptances are never compared against later arrivals or evicted. A later preferred applicant is accepted only into an unused slot. Attempts to an unacceptable receiver or a full receiver still count because the increment precedes receiver screening; matched proposers and proposers without that round's choice do not attempt.
