Newton differences then contain 3/2,0 and reconstruct the constant 3/2, registered as R = 3/2. Replace the final pair with 0 2 to obtain a later conflicting x and ERROR DUPLICATE X 0; if a later argument has denominator zero, the earlier rational-validation stage wins.
