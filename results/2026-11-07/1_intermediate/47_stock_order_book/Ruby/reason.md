## Evidence and language semantics

The match loop reselects the best opposite-side order each iteration. The ordering key prioritizes the best price and then the oldest priority. A fill consumes the smaller of the incoming remainder and the maker visible slice; the retained maker remainder is the hidden-plus-visible quantity. Exhausting a slice with a positive remainder assigns a fresh priority and exposes min(peak, remainder), placing it behind older resting orders at the same price. The trade output is produced before that refresh output. The same-trader guard cancels the resting maker and continues before fill, fee, and refresh processing, so the incoming remainder is not consumed. Ruby Struct instances are mutable referenced objects. min_by compares the two-key arrays lexicographically; every loop reselects the best eligible order. next skips fill, fee and replenishment for self-trade prevention. The visible slice uses Array#min, and puts calls retain trade-before-refresh order.
