At Celsius -3, the current iteration binding supplies both the calculation and original-temperature field. The real division or multiplier yields 26.6, and the exact output line is "-3 C = 26.6 F".

The block parameter celsius resolves inside fahrenheit and format; 1.8 keeps fractional arithmetic and %.1f affects only text.

The current-temperature binding, arithmetic and line interpolation establish this one iteration. Other input temperatures and later iterations cannot affect its value.
